# Linear A research — public source guide

**Research through investigations 36–37 · Baseline dated 18 October 2026**

This guide identifies the principal sources behind the public research baseline. Its numbered keys match the baseline's references. It is a curated guide to the project's evidence and correction history, rather than a complete bibliography of Linear A scholarship or a replacement for the original editions.

## How to read these references

Entries **[1]–[19]** identify project reports, research records and supporting reviews. They document what the project proposed, checked, corrected or left unresolved. They are not separate ancient witnesses. A conclusion repeated in several project reports may still depend on the same inscription, reading or initial analysis.

Entries **[20], [22] and [23]** identify original scholarly publications or editions. Their inspection status differs: selected original pages were checked for the consolidation, while the complete argument in [23] remains uninspected. Entry **[21]** records the consolidation and its audit scope. Public packaging adds no independent validation to any of these sources.

The dated baseline records current project decisions. Original publications and object evidence control the observations, with disagreements and later source-specific corrections kept explicit. The date of a report alone does not settle an evidential conflict.

### Public availability and access limits

The public reference packet summarizes the research; it does not reproduce the complete underlying research archive. This guide supplies no verified standalone public download for the project report groups **[1]–[19]** or the underlying audit materials in **[21]**. Their identification here should not be mistaken for a promise that the entire original package is publicly downloadable. Public availability outside this packet has not been established for each item.

The original article in **[20]** has the public PDF linked below. **[22]** is identified descriptively without an invented edition-download link. The DOI in **[23]** identifies the publication; it is not evidence that the full text and figures were accessible to the project. Access and inspection statements below describe the 18 October baseline, not a new search conducted for this guide.

## Using the guide for continued research

A contribution should identify the exact source, object, written component and uncertainty that can change a named decision. Distinguish access to a source, inspection of its evidence, and support for an interpretation. Different publications about one object do not automatically supply independent witnesses. Material already inspected for discovery cannot become genuinely unused evidence merely by being omitted from a later calculation.

The current baseline keeps both latest branches parked and records zero active semantic experiments. A new source or observation should first be assessed against its specific reopening condition, with the strongest serious alternative stated alongside the preferred interpretation.
