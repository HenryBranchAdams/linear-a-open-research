## 1. The decision

**The project has built a substantially better constrained research record, but it has not established a construction whose linguistic action and relevant participant roles are independently supported.** Several earlier claims described as breakthroughs were subsequently narrowed or withdrawn. Those older reports remain part of the history; their strongest wording is not the current conclusion. [4–14]

The latest two investigations reached their agreed stopping condition. Investigation 36 improved the original evidence for a compound mark on vessels without finding an observation that distinguishes its serious interpretations. Investigation 37 did not recover the full Phaistos source required to evaluate its proposed responsibility relationship. **Both branches remain parked, for different reasons. This consolidation is not iteration 38 and does not restart the campaign.** [13–14]

The next research objective is specific: establish one defensible connection between a stable written element and an independently observed category, relationship, action or participant role. A narrow intermediate result can be valuable. It must change the comparison between serious alternatives and retain its local scope. An independently supported action-and-participant construction remains the mission; a fluent English rendering is not a success criterion. [14]

The most concrete conditional source target is the **complete Flouda (2024) article**, including its figures, inventory references and reasoning. A legitimate complete copy would allow evaluation of a potentially relevant writing-to-operation link. The last bounded search did not identify a new full-copy route. Obtaining the article would clear access only; the semantic comparison would still need to qualify. [13–14]

## 3. What has survived

### 3.1 Accounting structure is useful; transaction meaning remains open

**KU-RO totaling is a working anchor inherited from scholarship.** Selected lists sum to the written total, and other cases reveal damage, omissions or uncertain scope. HT122 illustrates why a late entry matters: 31 + 1 + 65 = 97, while omitting the KU-DA 1 gives 96. It remains a conditional scope check, not a new word decipherment. [4; LA-H002–003]

**KI-RO can organize counted lists.** HT88 has six surviving unit entries closing with KU-RO 6; HT117a has ten closing with 10. HT94b supplies a damaged parallel whose closing value is **5[**, not an assured complete 5. Grouping and summation do not by themselves distinguish absent, owed, reserved, allocated or otherwise classified items. They also do not make every unit label a person. [7–8]

**Some simple numerical models have been excluded under stated assumptions.** The HT30 comparison needs different ratios for different commodities: the conventional VIN quantities give 1/17, while the additive D versus D+D comparison gives 1/2 if D is positive. That excludes one common rate under that reading and unit model. It does not select a transaction direction or eliminate commodity-specific rules. [8, cycle 23]

HT114a and HT121 similarly do not behave as one fixed basket multiplied by a common scale. Grain changes from 10 to 5 while the conventional NI amount changes from 1 to 2. Neither basket is simply a componentwise part of the other. Flexible conversion weights fitted to the same observations do not supply independently known exchange rates. [8, cycle 31]

**The HT89 discrepancy was an extraction error.** The original sequence 23 + 22 + 24 + 13 + 5 closes at 87. A parser omitted the 13 associated with a lowercase sign variant, producing 74. That defect was fixed and was checked again during the 18 October consolidation. The correction is useful data work; it does not imply that every unresolved account should be forced to balance. [8, cycle 28; 21]

### 3.2 Repeated labels constrain structure without proving historical identity

**The HT86/95 cohort remains a strong structural lead.** Five exact labels recur after the proposed QA-RA₂-WA / QE-RA₂-U identity is withheld rather than assumed. The recurrence is earlier project work and prior scholarship. Later room and layout controls sharpen its description. They do not establish a shared historical person, a common administrative event or a directed transfer. [4, 8, 10–11]

The well-known 20:10 comparison supplies no label-specific identification: all five relevant quantities on one side are 20 and all five on the other are 10. Every one of the 120 label permutations preserves the ratio. The room-conditioned diagnostic also remains conditional on its declared pool and extraction rules; its sampled tails are not probabilities that the labels name the same people. GORILA assigns HT86 to Hand 6 and leaves HT95 unassigned. A blank attribution is neither a confirmed shared hand nor a confirmed different hand. [8, cycle 25; 10]

The five labels divide **3 + 2 across HT86a's visible rule**, while HT95a presents one visible list. The cohort therefore does not always occupy one unchanged local block. Commodity carryover and the scope of HT95b remain qualified. [11]

**DA-ME and KI-RE-TA-NA provide graphic-association contrasts.** DA-ME occurs before GRA+B on HT86a and GRA+K+L on HT120. This rejects an invariant exact-compound association, while leaving broader grain-related and other interpretations possible. KI-RE-TA-NA occurs before an anthropomorphic graphic family on HT108 and a grain compound on HT120 under the retained source readings. That rejects a narrowly invariant following-family model. It does not establish that the preceding sequence is a personal name, nor what such a person did. [11]

**QI-TU-NE remains a useful positional comparison from the earlier work.** Its counted-item, heading and subheading occurrences survive exclusion of a restored comparator. The repeated DI-KI-SE association also survives. Person, office, group, institution, place and account interpretations remain open. SA-RU retains an entity/account preference in the historical analysis; its broader category alternatives were not conclusively eliminated. [6, iterations 11–12]

**External comparisons supply graded category anchors.** PA-I-TO has an established Linear B place-type comparison with Phaistos, and MA-DI has an exact Linear B personal-name-type parallel. These strengthen particular category proposals. They do not independently identify a unique Linear A historical referent or its role in an inscription. [4, 8, cycle 24]

## 5. Why progress plateaued

The central missing link is **independent semantic identification**. A repeated label may identify a person, office, place, group, commodity category or account; several can overlap in actual use. A balance can arise from multiple administrative processes. A stamp identifies a device-use relationship before it identifies a particular person. A recovery room need not be the place or time of the transaction. None of these uncertainties disappears because an English paraphrase fits well. This is the shared implication of the audited countermodels. [4–14]

The project also repeatedly encountered discovery-index artifacts: lost uncertainty markers, restored forms treated as complete, cross-face fragments treated as whole words, sign aliases treated as meanings, missing lowercase variants, and document boundaries treated as linguistic boundaries. Correcting these reduces false leads. It is useful groundwork, but the correction itself does not supply an action or a role. [4, 7–12]

A third issue was continuity. Historical report wording, current decisions and the correction chains between them were not always easy to distinguish. The 18 October consolidation reviewed the intervening investigations, made their supersessions explicit and preserved the inherited scientific registries. It did not rewrite older reports to make the history agree retrospectively. [15; 21]

The work has therefore progressed most clearly in source discipline and in eliminating unjustified shortcuts. It has plateaued in selecting meanings. Both statements should remain visible; describing every source retrieval or passed check as a linguistic advance would conceal the plateau.

## 6. Focused continuation

**Active semantic experiments in this queue: zero.** This baseline introduces no recurring source search. A genuinely new evidence route can reopen bounded source evaluation. Admission to a semantic experiment still requires a differentiating observation; access alone does not supply one.

### Priority 1: conditional access to the complete Phaistos argument

**Target:** Georgia Flouda (2024), “Agency and materiality entangled: new insights into Minoan clay-sealed documents from Protopalatial Phaistos,” *Pasiphae* 18, 117–131, DOI [10.19272/202433301008](https://doi.org/10.19272/202433301008). [13–14; Source 23]

**Current status:** parked, source-access-limited. The exact source is identified; a newly available complete-copy route is not. Evaluate a supplied legitimate copy or one genuinely new identified route. Repeating the same DOI, vendor, abstract or on-request record is not a new investigation.

**After access:** identify the exact carrier, any stable incised writing, and the evidence connecting that writing to the claimed responsibility. Keep HMs789θ / CMS II.5 no. 205 separate from PH Wa52 / HMs689 / CMS II.5 no. 300 unless the source explicitly joins them. Distinguish seal operator, institution, person responsible for an operation and referent of the inscription.

**Decision-changing observation:** an independently established object/use relationship for which a responsibility interpretation and the strongest recipient/counterparty or category interpretation predict different outcomes.

**Stop:** if the copy adds only administrative context without binding the writing to that role, or leaves the strongest serious alternative equally compatible. Obtaining the publication is an access success, not automatic promotion of its interpretation.

### Priority 2: admit a genuinely different evidence opportunity

A new candidate is welcome when it arrives with a specific packet, not simply a new name for a familiar problem. Record:

1. The exact object and written component, with uncertainty that matters to the proposed claim.
2. Two serious interpretations supported by the same context. They need not exhaust all conceivable stories.
3. An independently observed relationship on which their predictions differ.
4. The exact evidence path and a finite acquisition/evaluation scope.
5. What has already been inspected, what will be frozen, and what outcome remains unused.

A direct binding can justify a local category or relationship claim without an arbitrary minimum sample size. Conversely, many dependent occurrences do not establish a role. If the candidate lacks a known differentiating observation, classify the work as bounded exploratory source discovery and state what it is trying to locate. Do not count exploration as an admitted semantic experiment.

Use one active candidate and an independent challenger until its admission decision is clear. The challenger should reconstruct the inference and test its strongest alternative. Parallel source retrieval can help when the tasks are independent; more reviewers do not multiply the ancient evidence.

## 7. What will count as progress

Each future result must state its actual level:

| Level | Example of a valid result | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Recover the complete missing publication. | Its argument has not thereby been confirmed. |
| Reading / object control | Settle a divider, damaged numeral, object alias or physical join. | A sign reading is not its meaning. |
| Structural / physical constraint | Reject a specified fixed basket, boundary rule or production history. | Reject only the model actually contradicted. |
| Semantic discrimination | Independently bind a component to a category or relation and distinguish the strongest serious alternative. | Preserve object, event and component scope; do not infer a whole grammar. |
| Linguistic replication | Freeze a rule and successfully predict a relevant unused independent observation. | A posthoc fit or a second transcription of the same witness is insufficient. |
| Mission construction | Establish the action and relevant participant roles at the claimed level, discriminate serious alternatives, and replicate appropriately. | A status/category result is intermediate; a plausible English sentence or externally inferred physical action alone does not satisfy the mission. |

The 18 October baseline preserves an outcome-exposure record. It records **known exposure to relevant outcomes**, not invented first-seen dates. Central candidate outcomes already used in discovery cannot be recycled as unseen tests. Where historical exposure is unknown, the ledger says unknown rather than unexposed. Reading an entire new article can expose every example in it; leaving one example out of a later calculation does not restore its independence.

Before a prospective test, freeze the target component, reading alternatives, rival predictions, witness selection and success/failure criteria. If source inspection changes the rule, record a discovery-stage revision and identify a different unused test. Treat publication independence, object/event independence and independence of the semantic reasoning as separate checks.

Future progress reports should identify the **latest decision-changing observation, current claim level, strongest remaining rival, evidence blocker and exact reopening condition**. The 80 claims, 28 experiment/feasibility records and 92 source/access records describe continuity. They include historical hypotheses, corrections and access work. They are not 80 discoveries, 28 semantic experiments, a corpus coverage percentage or a measure of how much language is decoded. [13–15]

## 8. Provenance and limits of this public packet
