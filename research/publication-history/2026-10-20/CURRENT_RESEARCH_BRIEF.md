## 3. The most concrete conditional source target

**Georgia Flouda (2024), “Agency and materiality entangled: new insights into Minoan clay-sealed documents from Protopalatial Phaistos,” _Pasiphae_ 18, 117–131.**

Publication reference: [https://doi.org/10.19272/202433301008](https://doi.org/10.19272/202433301008). [23]

### What would reopen access work

A supplied complete copy that can legitimately be shared, or one genuinely new identified route to a complete copy, would justify bounded source evaluation. The required material includes the article's figures, inventory references and reasoning.

The baseline's last bounded search did not identify a new full-copy route. Repeating the same DOI, vendor page, abstract or on-request record is not a new investigation. No missing publication is presumed to contain a decisive result simply because the project has not obtained it. [13–14, 23]

### What to evaluate after access

Establish the exact carrier and written component. Then examine the evidence connecting that writing to a proposed responsibility for an operation. Distinguish:

- The operator of a seal.
- An institution involved in administration.
- A person responsible for a particular operation.
- The person, entity or category referred to by the writing.

Those roles can overlap, but their identity cannot be assumed.

Keep **HMs789θ / CMS II.5 no. 205** separate from **PH Wa52 / HMs689 / CMS II.5 no. 300** unless an explicit source joins them. The full article must be read before its individual-object argument is summarized as established. [14, 23]

### What would admit a semantic comparison

An independently established object or use relationship must make a responsibility interpretation and its strongest serious recipient, counterparty or category alternative predict different observations. The relation must be tied to the particular writing, rather than only to the general administrative setting.

### Stop condition

Keep the candidate parked if the complete copy adds only administrative context, fails to connect the writing to the role, or leaves the strongest serious alternative equally compatible. Record a successful source acquisition at the access level. It does not automatically establish a semantic relationship. [14]

## 4. Admission gate for any other candidate

A candidate can be evaluated when it arrives with a specific evidence packet. A new English gloss for a familiar example is not, by itself, an evidence packet.

Before admitting a semantic experiment, record:

| Required item | Question the brief must answer |
|---|---|
| Exact target | Which object, written component and proposed relation are being tested? |
| Reading and scope | Which uncertainty, segmentation, unit, account boundary or physical event matters to the inference? |
| Serious alternatives | What are the preferred interpretation and its strongest serious rival under the same context? |
| Distinguishing observation | What independent observation would the alternatives predict differently? |
| Evidence path | Where can that observation be obtained, and what is the finite acquisition/evaluation scope? |
| Outcome exposure | What has already been inspected, what remains unknown or unused, and what will be frozen before a prospective test? |

The alternatives do not need to exhaust every imaginable explanation. They must include the strongest serious rival currently supported by the evidence. A fit within a hand-selected model class is not an exhaustive identification of meaning. [14, 21]

A direct, independently supported connection can justify a local category or relationship claim without an arbitrary minimum number of examples. Conversely, many repetitions of the same dependent evidence do not establish a participant role. Keep the scope at the object, component and event level supported by the observation. [14]

If the differentiating observation is not yet known, classify the work as **bounded exploratory source discovery**. State exactly what evidence it is trying to locate and when that search will stop. Do not count that exploration as an admitted semantic experiment.

## 5. Exposure and prediction

A training split describes which rows a particular computational step receives. It does not establish what the research process knew before the hypothesis was designed.

The initial September studies illustrate the distinction. Earlier project material already displayed the key ritual examples and outcomes. Withholding one of those rows from a later calculation did not make it an unused witness for research-level validation. The current baseline retires those claims of genuine unused-evidence semantic success and mission completion. [1–3, 21]

Already examined material can support an explicitly retrospective analysis. A claim of successful prediction requires a relevant unused observation and a rule frozen before that outcome is inspected.

Before a prospective test, freeze:

1. The target component and relevant reading alternatives.
2. The competing predictions and their scope assumptions.
3. The witness-selection rule.
4. The success and failure criteria.
5. The known exposure record.

If source inspection changes the rule, record a discovery-stage revision and identify a different unused test. Reading a complete new article can expose all its examples; excluding one from a later table does not restore its independence. Where earlier exposure is unknown, record **unknown**, not **unexposed**. [14–15, 21]

Assess publication independence, object/event independence and independence of the semantic reasoning separately. Two publications may describe one ancient object. An external contextual argument can still depend on the same unproven role assignment it is being used to confirm.

## 6. Operating and stopping rules

Use **one active candidate and an independent challenger** until the admission decision is clear. The challenger should reconstruct the inference and test the strongest alternative. Parallel retrieval is useful when the tasks are independent; it does not create additional ancient witnesses. [14]

Stop the current task when:

- The agreed source route is exhausted without obtaining the required evidence.
- The packet cannot establish the reading, object identity, common scope or physical relationship needed for the stated test.
- The observation is equally compatible with the strongest serious alternatives.
- The proposed test repeats an already exposed outcome while claiming unused-evidence validation.
- A broader model is rescued only by introducing unconstrained assumptions that remove the proposed discriminator.
- The planned finite evaluation is complete and no new route or observation justifies further work.

Record the actual reason. An access failure is different from contradictory evidence. An unresolved reading is different from a failed semantic model. A result that rejects one common conversion factor does not reject every commodity-specific conversion. [4–14, 21]

## 7. What to report

Every result should identify:

| Report field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Observation | The exact evidence newly acquired, corrected or compared. |
| Result level | Access; reading/object control; structural/physical constraint; semantic discrimination; linguistic replication; or mission construction. |
| Claim | The narrow statement supported at that level. |
| Strongest remaining rival | The serious interpretation still compatible, or the observation that discriminates it. |
| Limits | Reading, unit, account, object, event and exposure qualifications. |
| Disposition | Continue within the admitted scope, stop, or park with an exact reopening condition. |

The mission level requires the writing's action and relevant participant roles to be independently supported, serious alternatives to be discriminated and the claim appropriately replicated. A convincing English paraphrase is not a score. [14, 21]

## 8. Reopening dormant work

The full baseline records conditional reopeners for the accounting, ritual, vessel, seal and provenance branches. They are not a schedule for rotating through old examples.

Examples of evidence that could matter include:

- For HT34 and related accounting: a reading adequate for the comparison, or a result robust across retained readings, together with independently constrained account scope and relation. A new numeral alone need not select a meaning.
- For HT118: an independently linked comparable account with known relevant scope, or a new source constraint discriminating its error and scope models.
- For A588 on the vessel packet: a specific product, capacity, workshop, commission or allocation relation tied to the written component and marking event, or a controlled contrast separating those effects.
- For repeated labels and possible endings: complete source-controlled forms connected independently to the same relevant designation and a discriminating role context.
- For sealing and analytical provenance: an exact object, readable inscription and relevant marking/use-event connection.

Each proposed reopener must pass the same gate. Source retrieval, better imagery and more examples are valuable when they can change the relevant comparison. [4–14]
