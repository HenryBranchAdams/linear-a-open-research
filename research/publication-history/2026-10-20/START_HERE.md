# Start here

Linear A, in the Open is an independent, AI-assisted research record maintained by Henry Adams and contributors.

1. Read [the current research baseline](research/current/CURRENT_RESEARCH_BASELINE.md), consolidated 18 October 2026 through investigation 37.
2. Use [the source guide](research/current/SOURCE_GUIDE.md) to distinguish project reports, original sources and inspection limits.
3. For further work, read [the current research brief](research/current/CURRENT_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md). An unchanged older task is not an admitted current experiment.
4. Consult historical files to trace how a claim developed. Pair older claims with their later correction.

The site overview explains the mission, retained findings, a worked HT34 comparison, corrections and the latest stopping decision. The research page carries the full progression and claim dispositions. The original task archive remains available for provenance and reproduction.

The public source release contains the site and its available research records. It does not imply recovery of every original archive or redistribution of restricted scholarly sources.
