# Review of iteration 10

## Verdict

Iteration 10 was a useful evidence-cleanup and rival-testing round, not a semantic decipherment breakthrough. Its strongest contribution is showing which tempting links cannot bear the proposed interpretations. The next iteration should preserve those gains but spend most of its effort obtaining a small amount of higher-information contextual evidence.

## Review scope

This review used the recovered atlas, candidate/source and experiment records, historical summaries, and relevant code/model excerpts available through the File Library. It also checked selected original scholarly discussions on the web, including Best pp.78–79 as PDF images, the relevant Steele–Meißner manuscript passages, and Davis–Valério/Weingarten article text. The complete original workspace was not mounted in this runtime. The original verification report was retrieved, not rerun.

Two central numerical arguments were independently reimplemented with exact rational arithmetic; the script and output accompany this review. These spot checks confirm conditional calculations, not ancient readings, category assignments or translations.

## Findings retained

### 1. The best repeated packet is genuinely useful, but does not establish identity or direction

With the proposed Q-form equation withheld, HT86/95 still has five matching edited labels. This is better target-independent contextual support than the present TA-NA bridge. It does not decide whether the labels are people, places, institutions, accounts, commodity classes or qualities. Two tablets are not four independent witnesses merely because their faces are considered separately. [R10-ATLAS; R10-CANDIDATES]

The packet and Q comparison are published prior work. Best cites Peruzzi's 1959 comparison; Steele and Meißner explicitly mark the Q correspondence as tentative. Those sources also illustrate that matching personal names across Linear A and B is not itself secure category identification. [BEST; STEELE-MEISSNER]

### 2. DA-ME/DA-ZA is a successful source audit, not morphology

Iteration 10 records that GORILA supports ME at HT95b.2, whereas the frozen SigLA acquisition had ZA. The appropriate disposition is a documented source discrepancy with an explicit overlay. This is not a newly established ancient alternation, and the review does not diagnose why the database reading differed. [R10-DOCUMENTARY; R10-CANDIDATES, C02]

A small maintenance issue remains: the retrieved corpus script's sensitivity scenarios still carry derivative-source/no-image-adjudication wording. The later edition inspection needs an explicit provenance event in the machine-readable chain. That is not necessarily an error in the calculated results.

### 3. The TE-TU rival limits the TE-KE/TE-KI argument

The shared-associate evidence used in the set screen can support both TE-KI and TE-TU as comparisons for TE-KE. This is a concrete non-uniqueness control. It does not prove that the candidates are equally plausible under all possible features, or disprove the published TE-KE/TE-KI comparison. Set overlap discarded order and some scope information; those features may still be tested, without reinstating the older failed circular-order interpretation. [R10-ATLAS; R10-HISTORY; DAVIS-VALERIO]

### 4. The numerical scale rejection is correct and narrow

Within the first section of HT86a, KU-NI-SU, SA-RU and DI-DE-RU each have 20 against 10 in HT95b. A universal factor of two would predict 20 for the proposed Q counterpart, but its observed entry is 10. The independently reimplemented calculation confirms the contradiction.

What fails is the conjunction of same-Q alignment and universal conversion of equivalent quantities. The calculation does not independently establish common event, identical unit, or same-Q identity. It therefore does not refute spelling variation, grammar, or a recurring account across different transactions. The totals 110/67/60 must remain numeral sums, not comparable physical totals. [R10-PACKET-INPUTS; R10-ATLAS]

### 5. HT24 now has a precise acquisition requirement

Under the inherited conditional model, expanded ME gives a doubled-unit visible input minimum of 47.5 against the proposed 45 output. At zero additional input, the maximum feasible yield is exactly 18/19, or about 94.7368%. An independently applicable minimum yield strictly above that threshold would rule out this doubled-unit scenario. At the threshold it is still feasible; 95% excludes it, while 90% does not.

An independently established 45-unit input total would instead exclude the 47.5 input minimum without invoking yield. With only secure ME, the doubled-unit minimum is 41.5. None of these calculations calibrates 45 noduli as 45 units or supplies an observed yield. With arbitrarily missing input and freely adjustable yield, a positive multiplier is not identified. [R10-HT24-MODEL; R9-HANDBACK; accompanying spot_checks.json]

### 6. The corpus-wide negative result is narrower than the headline number

The 802 faces contain 1,401 indexed chunks, not 1,401 certified words. Only 220 faces contribute strict eligible contexts and 250 contribute to the broader complete-chunk audit. The result concerns retained forms and specified initial-A/terminal-sign contrasts with non-target associates, not the absence of morphology or grammatical identity everywhere in Linear A. Its exhaustive computation within the captured representation does not make the representation a complete critical edition. [R10-ATLAS; R10-CORPUS-CODE]

## Changes recommended for iteration 11

**Replace sets of labels with a compact, structured occurrence dossier.** Retain order, heading and logogram scope, numerical binding, damage, commodity subtypes, hand attribution basis and physical context. Avoid enlarging the search space before recovering information that the first screen omitted.

**Investigate category before precise personal identity.** A stable account or institution could provide a useful role anchor even when no individual can be named. Conversely, repeated commodity vocabulary would undermine an assumed participant list. Keep these outcomes separate rather than making all progress depend on identifying one person.

**Separate types of independence.** Target-excluded associates are independent of assuming a particular ending, but are not statistically independent or independent ancient sources. Published editions, derivative databases and multiple papers may ultimately rest on the same witness. Scribal attribution may also depend on the same signs under study.

**Preserve the no-written-verb rival.** Evidence that goods moved does not show that the surviving signs spell a verb of transfer. A list may record a nominal relation or omit the action. The final mission requires evidence for the actual written construction, not an English sentence added around an account.

**Make validation honest about prior exposure.** The complete captured index was already screened. A new random split is not a genuinely prospective test. New physical features may test a frozen prediction; rereading an old edition can improve reliability without adding an independent linguistic witness.

## Most promising concrete next target

Prioritize unchanged **SA-RU** as a bridge from HT86/95 to **HT94a.2 and HT123a.4**. Best points to those contexts for a name claim; Cash and Cash discuss probable toponyms in HT123 instead. Do not accept either conclusion without inspecting what the signs and structure actually constrain. The opportunity is to test a category claim on specific additional contexts, not to announce a new discovery of those attestations. [BEST; CASH]

Use HT117/HT87 as the second structural-role family. Treat AT125 as a conditional replacement route, not an automatic parallel project. Preserve TA-NA, HT24 and the previously tested seal142 cases with their exact reopening conditions.

## Bottom line

Retain iteration 10's conclusions with their stated scope. Tighten the provenance labels and validation design. The key strategic change is to stop treating a changed ending as the necessary starting point: first anchor what a label designates and what the document does, then ask what grammatical information the written form contributes.
