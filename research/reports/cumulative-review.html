01 / Current assessment

What survives nine iterations

The project has become more rigorous about what the inscriptions support. It has not yet established the sentence construction it set out to recover.

The central finding

The strongest surviving results constrain readings, categories and numerical models. None yet independently fixes both a Linear A action and the relevant participants’ roles. The TA-NA forms have not been shown to name the same designation or referent. [P9] [R9]

The cumulative record is therefore not a steadily expanding dictionary. It is a sequence of hypotheses, useful tests and consequential corrections. Several early glosses should no longer be treated as results. Some attractive morphological patterns disappeared when word boundaries and damaged surfaces were checked. Other patterns remain interesting but admit rival explanations. [H1] [H2] [H6] [P9]

RetainDo not promote
Accounting-function anchors; recurring formula structure; documented complete forms.An accounting label into a verb, or a repeated slot into a giver or recipient.
The checked Linear B person-designation context behind ta-na-to.Cross-script resemblance into identity or Linear A case meaning.
The exact, reading-sensitive HT 24 arithmetic.A conditional model exclusion into a translation of ME.

My assessment: the later work is stronger research than the early “partial crack” framing suggested, precisely because it is less willing to turn compatibility into meaning. That is genuine progress in the investigation, but it is not yet a validated decipherment breakthrough.

Coverage matters

This review uses the accessible iteration-nine report and handback, recovered summaries of iterations 1, 2 and 6–8, and selected fresh scholarly checks. The full earlier bundles and round-nine ZIP were not accessible here. Iterations 3–5 could not be reconstructed reliably. This is a cumulative review of the recoverable record, not a claim to have re-audited every historical file.

The accompanying package contains a 32-claim current-state ledger, a source-access register, an explicit coverage record and a small exact-arithmetic recheck. It does not contain the missing historical archives.

03 / Earlier semantic hypotheses

What the accounting branch actually supports

Strong functions are not an established grammar

KU-RO remains a strong totaling-function anchor, and PO-TO-KU-RO an overall/grand-total expression. These are established scholarly interpretations, not discoveries of this project. A fresh check of Meißner and Steele also shows a useful confidence distinction: their discussion treats KI-RO as probably indicating a deficit. [H1] [H2] [W1]

The initial project proposed the finer gloss “outstanding / balance due,” citing HT 94b and HT 88. Without reopening those account comparisons, I would retain that as a working hypothesis rather than certify the distinction between an outstanding obligation and other kinds of shortfall. The exact lexical meaning, grammatical category and etymology remain separate questions. [H1] [H2]

The assessment–fulfillment equation should be retired as a result

The early model linked A-DU, SA-RA₂ and KI-RO as an assessed obligation, fulfilled portion and remainder. The subsequent correction explicitly weakened that equation and the assigned fulfillment gloss. The underlying counterexample rows were not available for a fresh audit in this review. [H1] [H2]

Current disposition

Do not translate A-DU as “assessment” or SA-RA₂ as “fulfilled” in subsequent analyses without re-establishing the supporting contrast. They can remain hypotheses in a ledger, but not fixed inputs to a new translation.

The same caution applies when A-DU reappears on a roundel. Its frequency or heading position does not establish that it is an action word. The roundel literature considers materially different possibilities, including a transactional term and a name or place designation. [P9] [W3]

What this branch contributes to the mission

Accounting supplies relatively firm numerical relations and some commodity categories. That makes it a valuable calibration domain: a proposed interpretation should respect the quantities and record structure. But an account can identify quantities associated with a label without expressing an overt verb, or without revealing whether the label denotes a supplier, receiver, responsible party or location. [P9]

The review therefore keeps the arithmetic and account layout as constraints, while refusing to fill the missing role assignments with modern bookkeeping intuitions. That distinction is central to evaluating the later HT 24 work.

04 / Formula and morphology

Structure survived; some paradigms did not

Recurring ritual slots remain worth studying

Early work recognized repeated ordering around A-TA-I-*301-WA-JA, the SASARA family, U-NA-KA-NA-SI, I-PI-NA-MA and SI-RU-TE. It did not securely translate the latter expressions. The later report still permits competing readings of formula-like sequences, including the wine-associated ZA Zb 3. [H1] [H2] [P9]

An early comparison reported TA-NA | I-*301 | U-TI-NU on IO Za 6 versus TA-NA | RA-TE | U-TI-NU on IO Za 2, with one match among 36 examined pairs and a proposed further pattern. The underlying pair list, segmentation rules and intermediate follow-up were not recovered. That is a historical lead, not a statistical result independently certified by this review. [H2]

The simple singular/plural account of U-NA-KA-NA-SI versus U-NA-RU-KA-NA-TI failed reported counterexamples: the RU-containing forms did not consistently track the accompanying changes the model expected. This weakens that simple rule, not every conceivable analysis of the family. [H2]

A face boundary can manufacture a suffix

The recovered round-six audit reports that the apparent JA-SA-SA-RA / JA-SA-SA-RA-ME contrast on IO Za 16 was created by a continuation across faces. It also identifies A-SA-SA-RA[ on PK Za 4 as fragmentary. The reported collapse of five bounded four-form decompositions is therefore a source-quality correction, not evidence that Linear A lacks morphology. [H6]

Consequential distinction

A database sequence is not automatically a complete word. A face ending is not automatically a word ending. A missing continuation cannot be treated as a zero suffix.

The KU-MI-NA comparison is not a novel discovery

Round nine located the explicit A-KU-MI-NA / A-DU-KU-MI-NA / KU-MI-NA-QE comparison in Davis, printed p.47. I verified that page in this review. The same page also lists TA-NA-TE / A-TA-NA-TE. These remain useful material, but neither comparison can be presented as newly discovered by the project. [P9] [W2]

My reading of the cumulative result is that formula position and recurring segments have generated candidate structures, not independent role assignments. An attractive English sentence may illustrate a hypothesis; it cannot be counted as the evidence that establishes the hypothesis. [P9]

05 / The leading target

TA-NA: three forms, but no identity bridge

FormComplete witnesses in the audited target setWhat follows

These observations are documented in round nine. HT 49a.2 is left-broken and cannot be promoted into a fourth complete TI word merely because one database labels the surviving signs as certain. HT 7a has a human logogram in its heading, not a separate role label attached to each name. Fractional quantities on other TI accounts also rule out treating every associated number as a literal human headcount. [P9]

The non-ending identity test is useful but inconclusive

The project compared other complete, securely bounded multisign labels across the target account faces, excluding the TA-NA family, single signs, composites, damaged boundaries and doubtful readings. Its reported dataset contains 66 selected rows from nine document identifiers. None of the three TI faces shared a qualifying non-target label with ZA 10a. [P9]

Whole-tablet aggregation admits SA-MA on HT 10a and ZA 10b, the opposite faces from the target forms. HT 7a and HT 10b share DA-RE internally. A PA-JA-RE 1 match on HT 88 and ZA 10b supplies a useful warning: even a matching short label and quantity need not identify a unique person. [P9]

My assessment: this test did not find its intended bridge, but it was not designed or calibrated to prove that no shared referent exists. Sparse accounts may omit associates; related designations need not have identical distributions. Distinct names, regional spellings and grammatical variation remain live alternatives. [P9]

ZA 9 still does not complete the pattern

Round nine reports that the original GORILA III plate and apparatus leave A-TA-NA-[?] unresolved. TE is a tentative editorial possibility; TI is not recovered. Repetitions of that tentative reading in dependent transcriptions are not additional witnesses. A secure TI would affect the apparent site split, but still would not prove shared identity or a case meaning. [P9]

Current status

The family is a legitimate research target. It is not yet a demonstrated inflectional paradigm, and neither “from” nor “to” has been independently assigned. The immediate bottleneck is an independent identity/category bridge followed by a genuine role contrast. [P9] [R9]

06 / External controls

A name context and a transported jar are not a translation

Linear B: a stronger category anchor with strict limits

Round nine reports checking the critical text and photograph of KN De 1618+7171+7174+fr.. The inherited “Dc” identifier is an alias of this same witness, not an extra tablet. Complete ta-na-to precedes e-ko-so in a flock-associated person/responsibility position. Separate Db 1168, As 603 and Dn 1092 records were used as person/place controls. [P9] [R9]

This is a more defensible anchor than a sound-alike alone. It strengthens the classification of the Linear B expression. It does not establish that the Linear A forms identify the same person, carry the same ending, or designate a legal owner, giver or receiver. “Flock-associated person” is safer than importing a specific occupational or ownership relation. [P9]

Two controls limit the tempting case analogy. First, KN Uf(1) <311>.2 ]ta-na-ti is left-broken and survives through an old record of a missing object, so it is not a complete counterpart. Second, under the ordinary-o-stem hypothesis, the Linear B nominative and dative would both be written with final -to; the easy -to/-ti bridge fails that conditional test. Other stem analyses would require independent support. [P9] [R9]

THE Zb 6: physical history does not identify the label’s function

The report’s Boulotis 2008 check supports the four-sign label A-TA-NA-*46, and excavation notices support incision before firing. That establishes a label on a vessel and constrains when it was written. It does not select maker, owner, source, destination or dedication as the label’s function. [P9]

The recovered fragments were not an intact labeled jar in a demonstrated recipient workplace. The Cretan attribution remains qualified in the retrieved evidence rather than an independently proven local production source. The report also preserves differing discovery/recovery dates and an older webpage identifier that refers to a different one-sign object. These are provenance problems to keep visible, not silently harmonize. [P9]

Withdraw the shortcut

The object’s journey cannot make *46 mean FROM, or make TE mean TO. A replicated label contrast tied to independently established sources and destinations would be different evidence. The TA-NA-TE / A-TA-NA-TE comparison itself was already discussed by Boulotis in 2008. [P9]

The primary LiBER and Del Freo links did not return usable documents during this review. Their substantive checks are therefore credited to the iteration-nine report, not represented as newly repeated here.

07 / Roundels and textile contexts

The pivots added anchors, not participant roles

ZA Wc 2: numeral improvement, segmentation unresolved

The newer autopsy discussed in round nine favors a final numeral 2 rather than MI; the apparent diagonal is interpreted as a crack. But A-TI-KA(-)A-DU(-)KO 2 still allows different word divisions. Recognizing a possible Linear B counterpart for A-TI-KA does not independently segment the Linear A text. [P9]

The primary roundel discussion, checked selectively in this review, explicitly preserves alternatives to the name/action/commodity parse. A-DU cannot serve as an independently established verb while its boundary and category are themselves being inferred from that parse. [P9] [W3]

The report also records a two-versus-four seal-impression inconsistency in the chapter, with Perna supporting two. That discrepancy should remain attached to any computation that depends on the count. [P9]

A common seal-to-quantity ratio is conditionally excluded

In the reported selected examples, GO Wc 1 has 5/5 and HT Wc 3024 has 6/6 for face quantity versus impressions. Under one common scale, these imply one unit per impression. MA Wc <5> instead has 4+A704 against one or two impressions. If A704 is an additive positive proper fraction, the examples do not share that simple ratio. [P9]

That is a conditional constraint, not a translation. Missing impressions, different units or a nonadditive interpretation alter the test. Even a perfectly established counting system would not tell us whether the goods were incoming or outgoing. The exceptions are published evidence; the project’s contribution is a formal statement of the constraint. [P9]

Anetaki: a commodity-category context is useful

The reported KN Zg 57 C AB54+77 context supplies a cloth+KA convention. It does not translate KA as “purple,” identify a person, or identify an action. The ring and handle are separate carriers, apparently with different hands, not a fully established parallel-text pair. [P9]

My assessment of the pivots

These branches make the evidence less dependent on free verbal interpretation by adding physical and numerical constraints. They still leave the mission’s action and role assignments open. Their value should be judged by which alternatives they actually eliminate, not by how readily an English transaction can be supplied.

08 / Strongest analytical result

HT 24: one doubtful sign changes the exclusion

The proposed model asks whether ME could double a unit while the preserved entries and nonnegative missing quantities fit a lossless common total of 45. The answer depends on which ME components are admitted. The table below reproduces the report’s conditional results; I checked the arithmetic with exact fractions in this review. [P9] [R9] [A1]

ME scenarioMinimum at ×2Residual to 45Necessary multiplier ceiling
Secure ME with 10 only41½+3½47/20 = 2.35
Also accept doubtful ME with 647½−2½59/32 = 1.84375
Restore ME on all preserved entries63−1810/7 ≈ 1.42857

The first lower bound does not exclude doubling; the second does under this model. The third assumes a stronger restoration. A nonnegative residual is not an epigraphically valid restoration. In particular, omitting doubtful ME from a lower-bound calculation does not prove that the ancient entry lacked it. Lost extensions of preserved numerals may also consume the apparent allowance. [P9]

The result is conditional in two different ways

Reading dependence: the damaged ME beside 6 remains unresolved. Coarse database certainty assigned to a composite cannot erase uncertainty about one of its components. Model dependence: the common total, fraction values, 10:1 conversion, common batch, relation of 45 noduli to quantities and distribution of ME are not all independently fixed. The strongest objection is that the common-total model itself remains unestablished. [P9] [R9]

A processing model removes the simple contradiction

If 45 is output from at least 47½ input, the maximum yield at that lower bound is 45 / 47½ = 18/19. The required loss is therefore at least 1/19, about 5.263%. More missing input requires greater loss. This is a mathematical reconciliation, not an observed ancient processing yield. [P9] [A1]

The 45-unit accounting model is earlier scholarship developed by Del Freo from Palaima and Hallager. Del Freo’s treatment is qualified and discusses processing losses; the project should not describe him as having made an absolute exclusion or overlooked loss. What the iteration contributes is the exact sensitivity and yield calculation, with scholarly priority still unestablished. [P9]

What the recheck did—and did not do

The included Python script verifies the reported table and yield relation with 11 exact assertions. Its base quantity, 63/2, is inferred algebraically from the reported all-entries scenario. It does not independently read the tablet, validate the total, rerun the original corpus code or translate ME.

My assessment: this is the cleanest surviving analytical result because it identifies exactly which assumptions change the conclusion. It is not the closest result to a full sentence: neither an action word nor supplier/receiver roles have been recovered. [P9]

09 / Synthesis and priorities

The bottleneck is independent meaning, not more resemblance

Keep five levels of inference separate

LevelQuestionCurrent position
ReadingWhich signs and word boundaries are actually preserved?Some secure; ZA 9, damaged ME and several boundaries remain open.
CategoryPerson, place, commodity, numerical relation or formula slot?Several useful anchors; not all transfer across scripts or contexts.
IdentityDo the forms denote the same designation, account or referent?Not established for the TA-NA family.
Role and actionWho supplies or receives what, and which written expression identifies the action?No independently supported combination yet.
ConstructionDoes the same analysis discriminate rivals and predict another example?Mission remains open.

This is my synthesis of the project’s main branches. A “person” category does not equal a “giver” role; an imported vessel does not make its inscription say “from”; a plausible processing operation does not identify a processing verb. Most of the overextensions in the recovered record cross one of these boundaries. [H2] [H6] [P9]

The strongest improvements—and the remaining weaknesses

The later reports actively preserve disconfirming readings, source dependencies and assumptions. That is a substantial improvement over the initial semantic framing. The remaining weaknesses are coverage gaps in the cumulative archive, the inability to re-audit some early controls, and the lack of independently grounded semantic contrasts. No prior p-value or apparent paradigm should be reused without its raw selection and boundary rules.

Database pages, mirrors and editorial suggestions must be grouped by their underlying ancient witness. Younger and SigLA can encode the same edition; an alternative identifier can denote the same tablet; a reproduced jar drawing can derive from one publication. Repetition across these sources is not independent replication. [P9]

What deserves the next research effort

For the sentence mission: keep an identity-and-role test ahead of further free-form translation. A distinctive contemporary person/account link under two complete TA-NA forms would help; so could a replicated, independently grounded link between designations. Then test whether the variation tracks a role recognized without using the ending to define that role. This does not require imagining the same individual across the Linear A and Linear B periods.

For a tractable analytical paper: maintain HT 24 as a separate branch. Resolve the doubtful ME if possible, and seek independent constraints on total, units and processing. This could strengthen a narrow accounting result even if it contributes no sentence.

For the cumulative project record: recover the earlier handoff/archive and make the claim ledger authoritative. A proposed result should have a witness, rivals, dependency flags, a discriminating test and a current disposition. A software pass should remain a software pass, not a confidence score for a translation.
