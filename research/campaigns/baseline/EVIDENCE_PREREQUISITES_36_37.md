# Evidence required before these semantic branches reopen

The two-run campaign has a finite decision rule. A source improvement can be useful without authorizing another semantic iteration. The requirements below are concrete reopening conditions, not an automatic research queue and not a claim that Linear A cannot yield further results.

## 1. A588 on THE Zb13 and KN Zb34 — evidence-limited branch

**Current basis:** KN Zb34 / NM1160 is source-checked in GORILA IV, printed pp. XIX, XXXII–XXXIII and 79. Its published entry is a compound mark. The original magazine attribution is qualified by Firth (2016), pp. 246–247. THE Zb13's component and prefiring phase retain all Iteration 35 reading and scope qualifications. The conventional VIN background does not determine the extra component's meaning.

**Required new evidence:** a stable written component bound independently to a product property, capacity, workshop, commission, allocation or participant relation. A controlled comparison in which the mark tracks one of these while alternatives vary is one route; a direct independently established binding could also suffice.

**Questions an exact object dossier or specialist assessment would need to resolve:**

1. What independently observed feature identifies the relevant contents, production or allocation relationship, and which exact object and written component does it concern?
2. Does that relationship concern the original marking event, later use, or only the recovery deposit?
3. What serious alternative interpretation would predict a different observation?
4. Is another independent witness available for a prediction fixed before inspecting it?

**Not sufficient:** another occurrence of A588, the conventional wine+TE label, generic pithos function, another prefiring inscription, or an unqualified database findspot. No currently identified missing publication is asserted to contain the required semantic discriminator. A general search for more occurrences remains parked.

## 2. Phaistos agency argument — source-access-limited branch

**Exact requested source:** Georgia Flouda (2024), “Agency and materiality entangled: new insights into Minoan clay-sealed documents from Protopalatial Phaistos,” Pasiphae 18, pp. 117–131, DOI https://doi.org/10.19272/202433301008 . A complete legitimate copy must include figures, captions, object inventory and the argument; an abstract or bibliographic record is insufficient.

**Known target:** HMs789θ / HM Sigma-Tau789θ, associated in the inherited abstract with CMS II.5 no205 and direct sealings. Keep this group separate from PH Wa52 / HMs689 / CMS II.5 no300 unless the full source explicitly joins them.

**Questions for the complete source:**

1. What is the exact inventory and carrier identification of the mentioned combination nodule?
2. Is there a stable incised Linear A component, with a documented reading, on it or on an independently linked object?
3. Which evidence connects that writing to the inferred administrator or responsibility? Distinguish the person operating the seal, institutional control of an operation, and the referent of the writing.
4. What observed attachment, closure/opening or administrative relation distinguishes responsibility from recipient/counterparty or account/category interpretations?
5. Would the same observations survive delegated seal use, separate handling events, or an intended recipient named before closure? Address alternatives supported by this packet, rather than an unlimited list of possible stories.

**Stop after full-source inspection if:** the article supports an administrative practice without linking the writing to a role; or its role argument leaves the strongest rival equally compatible. Recovering the full article clears access only. It does not by itself clear the semantic gate.

## Decision for any future candidate

Before acquiring a broad source set, identify the stable writing, strongest serious rivals, expected differentiating observation, and exact evidence path. If that cannot be done, classify the work as exploratory source acquisition and give it its own finite budget. A new candidate must be justified by a different evidential opportunity rather than by an unused iteration number.
