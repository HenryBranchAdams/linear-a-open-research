# Independent review of research state through iteration 041

**Review date:** 23 October 2026. **Scope:** documentation and scientific-state audit, not iteration 042 and not a new source campaign.

## Verdict

The current conclusions in the final reports for 038–041 are mutually consistent and appropriately limited. The project has not established its action-and-participant construction. These campaigns added five claim records concerning source, reading, object and physical controls; none claims a newly identified meaning, participant role or independently validated linguistic prediction. No semantic experiment is currently active. [R1–R6]

The documentation should present these useful constraints plainly while also stating that semantic selection has plateaued. A report count, a source count or a passed preservation check cannot stand in for a meaning established. The preserved baseline already makes this distinction; the next consolidated entry points should carry it forward. [R1 §5–7; R2–R6]

No scientific reversal is recommended by this review. The needed change is an effective current layer that combines the frozen 18 October dispositions with the later overlays, together with an explicit map to historical reports and exact reopening conditions. The frozen baseline and earlier iteration reports should remain unchanged.

## Matters the consolidated documentation must preserve

### 1. The 2019 Akrotiri access barrier is closed; the sample identity barrier is open

Iteration 040's continuation still asks for a complete 2019 publication. That was correct when written and was superseded by 041. Current instructions must direct future teams to the acquired catalogue packet and the missing exact-object analytical relationship. The 2013 positive-result ACV↔AKR crosswalk is a navigation aid, not a target-mark/residue dataset. Hilditch's integer codes must remain a separate namespace unless a documentary bridge is recovered. [R4–R5; R8–R9]

The four inspected concordance pages have blank entries and numbering irregularities. Therefore “no target join recovered in the inspected records” is supported; “the vessels were not sampled,” “the concordance is complete,” or “no relevant register exists” is not. The complete 2021 residue chapter remains unread; it is an access opportunity, not a rejected scientific argument. [R5; R8–R9]

### 2. The Akrotiri comparison is not a measured equal-capacity experiment

Keep 9384's 2019 rim measurements (outer 15.6 cm; inner 15.1 cm) separate from the later approximate 14–15 cm account. Keep 9730's 14.5 cm rim with its unspecified measurement convention. Only 9384 has the approximately 7.95 L source-reported measurement. No arithmetic averaging or equality assumption is justified. The 2019 height for 9384 describes a mended/restored vessel; 9730's 15 cm is explicitly a preserved fragment height. [R4–R5; R8]

Technical differences make production-related marking a grounded rival without establishing independent workshops. “Different codes within G10” contradicts only a narrowly deterministic one-coarse-type/one-prominent-mark model, conditional on the sign and class assignments. It does not refute all vessel-name or capacity interpretations. The source history of the damaged trident and possible lost continuation must accompany that restricted exclusion. [R4–R5; R8; R10]

### 3. Physical chronology does not transfer automatically into linguistic roles

For KH Wc2123, the source-reported order does not show that the erased writing retained its referent into the stamped use. It also does not establish an expressed verb such as “receive,” cancellation or settlement. For the jars, prefiring marking does not identify the inscriber, and the inscriber is not automatically the inscription's referent. The later Akrotiri source withdraws the necessity of separate potter and scribe; it does not prove they were the same person. [R2; R4; R10]

### 4. Retain the 040 method clarification

The future admission gate should not demand that a result already be established before a finite test can begin. A concrete local relation or category result can be informative without a named historical referent or an arbitrary minimum sample size. Grounded rivals need constrained predictions; an unrestricted hypothetical owner story must not veto all comparative support. At the same time, physical function alone cannot establish what an inscription denotes. In the Akrotiri case the production rival is grounded by observed technical differences, so the current stop is substantive rather than merely philosophical. [R4; R7, “Application clarification”; R9]

### 5. Preserve the original exposure and dependence controls

The 2019, 2021 and 2025 publications concern the same two ancient vessels. New reviewers and new source access do not create independent ancient replications. The 038–041 campaigns were source-led investigations of exposed objects, not unused linguistic prediction tests. The early September ritual claims of genuine held-out validation remain retired, as do the stronger historical interpretations of HT34, HT118, HT9 and HT103 described in the baseline. [R1 §4,7; R2–R6]

## Documentation findings and recommended disposition

1. **Create one current decision register or queue, leaving the frozen baseline unchanged.** The baseline's highest conditional priority and candidate inventory predate 038–041. Current NEXT already includes the recent branches, but a new reader must currently reconcile several layers manually. Include current status, claim level, strongest rival, exact blocker, reopening condition and source/overlay path for each branch. Do not assign an active semantic test merely because a source target exists. [R1; R6–R8; R11]

2. **Make historical versus current authority explicit at entry points.** Older files named CURRENT_STATE, CONTINUATION or RESEARCH_QUEUE are snapshots. The original reports should preserve their former instructions, but navigation must state which addendum supersedes their acquisition tasks and source judgments. This is especially important for 038's “SI Zg1 not deeply investigated” and 040's “2019 unavailable” statements, both historically correct and subsequently superseded. [R2–R5; R8]

3. **Expose the review coverage honestly.** This consolidation can claim a review through 041 using the earlier audited baseline plus a direct review of 038–041. It should not say that every original publication, computation or early chat message has been freshly reviewed. If additional independent historical work is performed, name it separately. [R1 §2; this audit's scope]

4. **Use readable text spacing and concise current summaries.** Recent machine-generated documents contain compressed forms such as “after040,” “The2013” and “printed258.” Preserve originals while making the current overview and next brief easier to read. Clear prose should accompany exact identifiers; identifiers themselves must not change.

5. **Keep preservation verification separate from scientific validation.** Current totals are 85 claims, 36 experiment/access-feasibility records and 113 source/access records; the five latest claim entries explicitly have no new semantic relationship or unused linguistic prediction. These totals aid continuity. Integrity checks do not establish ancient meaning, verify source-page interpretations or replicate physical analysis. [R6; R10; R12]

6. **Do not manufacture iteration 042 by reorganizing the files.** Record this work as a documentation review after 041. A future iteration should begin with an evidence opportunity or bounded acquisition task that can change a named decision. No new semantic campaign, correspondence or public claim promotion follows merely from the documentation cleanup. [R1 §6–8; R6–R8]

## Final assessment

The 041 stopping decision survives review. There is meaningful accumulated source and object control, and there is still no newly established semantic anchor from the recent campaigns. Productive continuation depends on a real independent relationship, especially the precise target-to-specimen/use bridge for the Akrotiri pair, or a genuinely different primary packet that supplies a comparable discriminator. The next researcher should be able to learn that in the first page and reach the underlying record without reconstructing the history from nested summaries.
