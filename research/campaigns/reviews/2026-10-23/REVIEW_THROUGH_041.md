## Finding

The project is ready to resume from a coherent current record. The scientific stopping decision at iteration 041 survives review: no independently supported action-and-participant construction has been established, and the recent campaigns have not selected a new semantic relationship. The useful advance is a better constrained set of readings, objects, physical observations and rejected shortcuts. [1–5]

The most developed recent question concerns Akrotiri vessels 9384 and 9730. Their marks, catalogue identities and manufacturing differences are documented, but the inspected records do not connect either exact target to an identified analytical specimen and diagnostic use evidence. That missing relationship prevents the proposed comparison of contents/use marking against production-related marking. It is a specific research dependency, not a problem that another calculation on the same inputs will solve. [4–5]

The documentation needed a clearer current layer. Most latest scientific summaries were cautious and consistent, but the surrounding environment still exposed obsolete CURRENT_STATE files, ambiguous REPORT filenames, an iteration 010 root verifier, a stale manifest, frozen priorities and deeply nested report paths. A future team could therefore start from the wrong instructions while believing it had read “current state.” This review repairs that operational problem without promoting the scientific result. ### Akrotiri: why the missing relationship matters

Catalogue 1121 maps to inventory 9384, and catalogue 1122 maps to 9730. The original catalogue gives 9384 an outer rim of 15.6 cm and inner rim of 15.1 cm; 9730 has a 14.5 cm rim with its convention unspecified. The later approximate rim account is retained separately. Only 9384 has a reported measured capacity, approximately 7.95 L. The incomplete second vessel does not independently establish equal capacity. [4–5]

The later publication reports different clay texture, interior finish and exterior treatment. These differences give production-related marking a positive evidential basis as a rival to contents/use marking. They do not identify workshops or determine the inscription's referent. A commissioned recipient remains compatible with prefiring marking, but no order or recipient has been independently identified. [4]

Iteration 041 inspected the relevant original catalogue/plates and all four image-only pages of the published analytical concordance. It recovered no direct target-to-sample join. Blank fields, numbering gaps and distinct sample-code systems limit that negative result: the documentation must say “unrecovered in the inspected records,” not “the vessels were never sampled.” The 2013 table provides 17 ACV↔AKR positive-result pairings, with no recovered link to these inventories. Its table was checked as author-hosted text, not pixels. [5]

A useful semantic comparison would require an exact inventory/catalogue-to-specimen link, diagnostic use data and a contrast separating manufacture from use. For example, independently grouped production with varying known use, or known common use across different production groups, could test different marking predictions. No qualifying series is asserted to exist. Later use also has to be connected to the significance claimed for the prefiring mark. [4–6]

The 2019 source barrier is now closed. The 2021 residue article remains unread in full. These are different statuses; repeating the former as an access target would waste effort, while treating the latter as refuted would misstate the evidence. [5–6]

## 6. What should happen next

The next authorized campaign should begin by identifying an actual changed evidence opportunity. The exact Akrotiri specimen/use join is the clearest dependency, but no obtainable register has been identified. It should remain a conditional priority rather than an excuse for another unchanged search.

A supplied complete 2021 residue chapter or Flouda article can justify a bounded source evaluation. If those opportunities remain unchanged, a finite discovery pass can examine a materially different administrative-object packet. It should seek a physical or documented relation that connects stable writing to genuinely differing predictions.

One deep candidate and an independent challenger are sufficient until admission is clear. Parallel source work can help; it does not multiply the ancient evidence. A completed access or source-control task is useful when labeled at that level. Another compatible English gloss, recounted impression count or adjusted arithmetic fit is not a semantic advance.

The full mission remains open. The next productive step is an independent relationship that changes a decision.
