# What is and is not controlled

This is a retrospective qualitative comparison of two exactly identified carriers, not a statistical inference from a random sample. The objects were linked by publication photographs, visible inventory cards, excavated context and ceramic classification, not by a proposed meaning of their marks.

Observed/source-reported dimensions:

- Shared coarse form: G10.
- Different preserved prominent marks:9384AB81,9730AB164a.
- Different technical features: finer/careful interior/brown coating versus coarser/rougher interior/red coating.
- Only one measured whole capacity:9384approximately7.95L.9730's capacity is unknown; equality is inferred.
- Both incised during production according to source assessments; exterior coating applied after incision.
- Unknown original contents/use/order/workshop identity for both.

For Hcontents and Hproduction, the two-row dataset does not hold the alternative explanatory variable constant. Different contents could correlate with different marks and different manufacture; a production-related classification could also produce that pattern. No independent content observation separates them. The fact that each model can fit is not evidence of equal probability, and the production model now has positive material support as a rival rather than an unrestricted rescue story.

| Accept incision-before-coating | Wholly later first creation conflicts with that source assessment | Maker, commissioner, contents and planned use all remain possible. |

Hfixed: within the proposed local convention the preserved prominent mark is a deterministic function only of coarse type. Conditional on accepting the source's class and code identifications, these two instances contradict Hfixed. This is a narrow structural exclusion, not a selected semantic category. Multiple words for a shape, fine classification and multi-component designations are broader hypotheses not excluded by Hfixed's failure.

The production-stage inference is conditional on the author's coating/firing observation, not a new surface autopsy. A modern fill experiment is not an ancient weighing/filling event. A later paper, a new reviewer and repeated photographs do not create new ancient vessels or unused outcomes.

The next genuinely differentiating observation would cross the variables: known content/use within one independently characterized technical group, or known shared content across independently characterized production groups. Grouping must be established without the tested mark as the sole key. A second volume measurement alone could constrain capacity models but would not identify a workshop or contents.
