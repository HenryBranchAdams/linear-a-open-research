## Why this route is materially different

It offers a controlled comparison of real marked products rather than another interpretation of a free-standing administrative token: two physical vessels support a same-form/different-mark contrast and a pre-firing marking stage. The potential discriminator concerns what the marks classify. It does not need an invented personal owner to create uncertainty.
