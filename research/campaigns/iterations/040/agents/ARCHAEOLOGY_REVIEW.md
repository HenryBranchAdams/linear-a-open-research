## Measurement and manufacture

The strongest qualification concerns volume. Printedpp7/10 give9384 height0.295m, rim diameter0.14–0.15m, partly restored base estimated around0.11m; incomplete9730 rim diameter around0.145m. **Only9384 was actually filled with expanded-polystyrene grains**, giving approximately**7.95L**. Equal capacity of9730 is the author's inference from matching form/rim, not a second independently measured volume. No uncertainty or fill-level convention is supplied in the inspected account. Fig13 physically juxtaposes the rims; it cannot independently supply9730's missing body profile and capacity.

Printedp10 also records manufacturing differences:9384 has a more carefully finished interior, finer fabric and brown exterior coating;9730 has a rougher interior, coarser fabric and redder coating. The text discusses possible different workshops. The smooth-handmade/slow-turning observation for9384 is a specialist's interpretation, not a new experimental result of this campaign. A simple content-only account cannot erase those production contrasts.

The clay attribution is **macroscopic**, reported as Theran while Melian clay cannot be excluded bibliographically. It is not petrographic confirmation of the exact specimens. “Locally made” and therefore “locally inscribed” must carry that qualification. The source reports coating **after incision** and incision **before firing**; our displayed photos establish visible incisions and surfaces but do not independently verify microscopic coating/stroke or firing chronology. Preserve those as reported physical assessments.

## Context and object lifecycle

Printedpp3–7 distinguish recovery from original use. Both vessels were reconstructed from dispersed sherds associated with New Pillar Shaft67, **Room2**, principally destruction **layer12**. For9384 the source names scattered groupsAS25–AS30 and excavation groups67NO39–67NO44;9730 includesAS30 material near the latest floorD1/layer13. This is a source-controlled contextual link independent of the marks. It is not a physical join between the two vessels or proof of one filling, user, shelf or transaction.

Room2 is discussed as a possible storeroom, with shelf placement inferred from dispersal. The stratigraphy includes later collapse from Room1, an intermediate contact layer11, and possible prolonged use/visitation into LCΙ. A shared destruction context does not uniquely recover original arrangement or administrative destination. The author explicitly entertains planned recipients and workshop differences. No contents/residue directly tied to either carrier are reported.

The bounded lifecycle supported by the source is: manufacture → reported incision/coating/firing relations → unknown use history → breakage/deposition/dispersal → modern joining/restoration. The exact ancient uses and semantic reference of each mark are not filled in by that ordering.

## Consequential revision of the2021 interpretation

The2021 source inferred that marks were by tablet scribes and treated vase-maker versus inscriber as distinguishable. **The2025 source p13 expressly withdraws the necessity of that distinction**: a community/workshop scribe remains possible, but the potter could also have incised the sign. It is an author's documented refinement, not evidence that a particular potter or scribe has been identified.

Production correlation is therefore credible at the physical level, while production **denotation** is unresolved. A prospective recipient/order, workshop/maker designation, contents/function classification and other uses remain compatible with prefiring marking. The later paper's spice/cumin and ritual suggestions are exploratory interpretations; no independent content analysis is supplied to choose them.

## Verdict

The new source materially improves carrier, context, measurement and production controls. It **does not support a strict exclusion of capacity meanings merely from “same-size pots”**:9730's equal capacity is assumed, and different standards, commodities or conventions can preserve a capacity-related interpretation even for matching volumes. It also does not identify workshop, maker, user, contents or participant role.

Report the current advance as exact source/object control with meaningful narrowing of what can be claimed, including the one-volume limit, manufacturing rival, revised potter/scribe inference and possible incomplete AB81 sequence. A semantic test still requires an independently discriminating contents/use/production relation attached to these exact inventories. No new search, new candidate or unused ancient witness was introduced by this review.
