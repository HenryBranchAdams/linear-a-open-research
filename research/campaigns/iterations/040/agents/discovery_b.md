## Observation versus interpretation

The authors describe a prefiring branch-shaped AB04 and two basal strokes; contents and capacity are expressly tentative interpretations. The text locates P511 with vessels beside, probably fallen from, Room1's western bench. Metalcasting and stoneworking evidence is independently present there. Adjacent Room2 has olive pits and other vessels; no report here identifies P511's residue, actual contents, measured capacity, or manufacturing batch. **Do not transfer Room2's olives or P501's suggested oil use to P511.**

Actual image check: fig14 shows a jar profile with shoulder branch marking; plate101b labels a close-up of P511's shoulder, where a branching incision is visible. Plate101a's complete jar is **P461**, not P511; treating those pictures as the same vessel would be an identity error. At this image resolution the photograph supports location and general branch-like appearance, but does not independently establish firing chronology, a numerical value for the two strokes, exact capacity, commodity or participant role. No fresh epigraphic reading is proposed.

The source's footnote33 compares the sign with KNZb34, PHZb4 and PKZb21. That is a published graphic comparison. It neither supplies a new A588 semantic bridge nor reopens those parked claims. No further analysis of those carriers was undertaken.

## Admission comparison and verdict

| Candidate interpretation | Different observation needed | What this packet supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Intended commodity mark plus capacity indication | Exact contents/residue and a capacity relation independently tied to this marked vessel | Neither recovered |
| Production/workshop/batch designation or other vessel classification | Independently identified production/use grouping, linked without the target mark | Workshop find context only; no exclusive grouping |
| Text names a responsible producer/user or counterparty | Independent identity and role bound to the marking stage | None |

Prefiring inscription is compatible with a commissioned vessel, planned commodity/capacity, workshop/batch classification and various referent assignments. The physical production stage does not select the inscription's meaning. The authors' commodity/capacity suggestion remains live; this screen does not refute it.

**Decision: stop at screening.** Stable target and obtainable primary images exist, but the missing independent content/capacity/use relation remains missing. This finite packet is weaker for semantic discrimination than a controlled independently linked contrast. It should not become another semantic iteration merely by elaborating compatible interpretations.

**Reopening:** exact P511 residue/use record or independently calibrated capacity/manufacturing comparison tied to the carrier and marking event. A generic workshop attribution, another picture of the same branch, or neighboring food remains alone does not qualify. No scientific semantic claim proposed; the useful outcome is a source-controlled negative screen and prevention of object/room conflation.
