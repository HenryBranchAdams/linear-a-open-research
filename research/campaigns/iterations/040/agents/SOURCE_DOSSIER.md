## Exact dependencies and source correction

S1notes59–60 describe the marks project as work in progress and credit excavation/provenance/dating information from colleagues. They cite no earlier complete catalogue of these exact two vessels. S2 supplies the missing inventories and points to Nikolakopoulou2019, I203–204,230–231; II258–259, pl121/plLVII for vessel descriptions and distribution. It also explicitly states these two vessels were **not included as such in Tsoulakou2021**, because they were reconstructed from sherds later (S2p2). Tsoulakou2021 supports stratigraphic context, not an independent duplicate publication of these exact pairings.

**Correction:** S1p286 claimed the tablet-like signs established a distinction between vessel-maker and inscriber. S2p13 explicitly says that separation is not necessary, referring back to its own2021 treatment: either a community/workshop scribe or a potter familiar with wet-clay incision could have made the marks. Retain the earlier position as historical, not as an independently established operator relationship.
