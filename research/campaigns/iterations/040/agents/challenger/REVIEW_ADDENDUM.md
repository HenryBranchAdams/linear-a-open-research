## Greek interpretation controls

The clause concerning capacity uses the author's expectation—“we consider that it would have been the same”—before separately describing the9384 experiment. It must not be translated as two measured equal capacities.

The workshop passage attributes a probable inference to the colleague, then says that, at least theoretically, the signs could distinguish workshops and artisans. That is a concrete, physically motivated rival, not a selected maker identification.

The author’s later stance on operators is unambiguous: “Δεν θεωρώ απαραίτητο το διαχωρισμό ανάμεσα στον τεχνίτη και τον γραφέα.” In context, she no longer requires separating artisan and scribe, explicitly referencing her earlier2021study. This is a revision of that necessity claim, not proof that the potter actually inscribed either mark.

The possible additionalAB81-following sign is explicitly possible-but-considered-unlikely. Neither “the inscription definitely has two signs” nor “it is certainly a singleton” is supported.

## How the comparative assessment changes

The2021 contents preference relied partly on reported uniform carriers and the recurrence of similar signs in administrative media. The later source supplies a previously uncontrolled manufacturing difference that covaries with the marks: differences in interior treatment and clay grain, with possible technical differences. Therefore, a simple interpretation of the pair as a controlled contents contrast is weakened. Manufacture is not held constant.

This is new project evidence against the **adequacy of the experimental control**, not a rejection of content labels. Two vessels could differ simultaneously in workshop and intended contents. Nor does the covariation identify either sign as a workshop label: there is no independent within-workshop replication, no same-content/different-workshop control, and no independently known workshop name joined to the signs.

The strongest present rival is no longer merely “a mark could have been made during production.” It is **production/commission-related designation associated with independently described manufacturing differences**, compatible with incision before finishing. That rival merits direct comparison without demanding an independently named potter. The evidence strengthens it relative to a bare possibility, while leaving it unselected against contents, intended use or planned allocation.

The conditional coarse-form→one-fixed-mark exclusion still holds at its exact stated resolution. It does not generalize to fine manufacturing class or measured capacity. The new details make that scope restriction consequential rather than cosmetic.

## New physical and source constraints

1. Exact object IDs and excavation-link details are recovered.
2. Equal capacity is a hypothesis based on one measurement, not an independently controlled fact.
3. Marks were source-reportedly applied before exterior finishing, refining the broad prefiring stage.
4. Maker/inscriber separation cannot be inherited as an established conclusion; the same author explicitly relaxes it.
5. AB81 is preserved, but full inscription completeness remains qualified.

Each is an actual new source-control or physical constraint. None assigns a denotation, participant role or expressed action.

The phase result can exclude a history in which the relevant inscription was first added after this exterior finish, conditional on the author's surface assessment. It does not distinguish maker, commissioner, intended recipient, contents or future use, all of which can be designated during production.

## Highest-value next contrast

Recover an independently grouped series from these ceramic production classes, with marks and capacities recorded systematically. Determine whether mark identity follows manufacture when intended content/use is controlled, or follows content/use within one manufacturing class. Exact physical comparators should be linked without using the mark as the grouping key.

For the current pair, a direct examination can refine the coating/incision sequence andAB81continuation. Those controls alone cannot settle the semantic alternatives. Residue/use evidence and independent production grouping are the consequential additions.
