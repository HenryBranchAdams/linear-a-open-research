## Exact rival comparison

Let C denote the author's reported shared coarse form-and-size class, and M the attributed written mark.

| Model | Commitment in this pair | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One coarse class, one fixed mark | M is solely and deterministically a function of C at this scope. | Contradicted conditional on the author's shared-class identification and different mark readings. |
| Fine vessel classification or measured capacity | M varies with a finer feature/capacity not fixed by C. | Not tested: measurements and diagnostic fine class controls are absent. |
| Naming with alternative signs/conventions | One class can have synonymous names, variant abbreviations, or different informative components. | Not excluded. No claim that every such rescue has equal empirical support. |
| Contents or intended contents | C can be constant while contents differ. | Compatible; actual contents not independently identified. Prefiring timing requires intended contents or a class planned before firing. |
| Production or commissioned group | C can be constant while makers, batches, workshops or commissioning destinations differ. | Compatible and contextually grounded by prefiring application and unresolved manufacture. |
| Destination marker | Two vessels may share deposition/storage while different intended destinations remain. | Not excluded; source itself admits an intermediate stopping place. |

The first exclusion is exact but narrow. It does not refute all vessel-name semantics, all capacity semantics, or all relationships between form and mark. “Same form” must not become a hidden claim that no conceivable relevant feature differs.

The strongest grounded alternative to contents is a production/commission group label attached before firing. This does not require a named potter or institution to be logically coherent or contextually serious: the independently reported marking stage lies within manufacture/planning, and the source leaves manufacturing provenance unconfirmed. Different visible colors in the figures cannot by themselves prove different fabrics or workshops; they merely prevent treating identical material manufacture as visually established.

## Is the restricted exclusion semantic progress?

It is a valid controlled observation, not merely a restatement of two different signs: the independent physical classification specifies what was held common. The pair shows that the reported coarse vessel class alone cannot supply one fixed mark.

However, a class of deterministic models was chosen at a deliberately coarse resolution. Its failure does not identify what the sign denotes. If no serious published model actually asserts this exact deterministic mapping, do not claim a major semantic hypothesis was falsified. The source's discussion motivates examining the inference from uniform vessels to contents, but the report must preserve the limited target.

The strongest warranted status is **source-controlled physical/text contrast with a conditional model exclusion**. It is useful negative constraint on future interpretation. It is not yet a supported new contents/category/role identification. It also is not a reason to stop all convergent category research: INITIAL_METHOD.md permits a controlled comparison that can actually differentiate contents from manufacturing/commission labels.

## Why the contents argument remains suggestive rather than selected

The source's cross-media references provide a reason to investigate an administrative or commodity-related use. They are stronger than a bare invented “could mean anything” objection. But cross-media sign recurrence does not force the same use on a prefiring vessel, and this finite packet does not identify its contents. The author explicitly recognizes that marks inside tablet vessel ideograms may carry different information from marks on physical vessels.

The observed pair therefore does not yet distinguish contents from an intended-use, production or commissioning distinction. This remains true without demanding a bilingual or independently named individual. The gap is a **controlled contrast between grounded mechanisms**, not simply absence of certainty.

Source-attributed prefiring marking constrains the available histories: an account based exclusively on labels first added after use is inconsistent with the report. It does not imply that contents, destination or responsibility were already materially realized. Intended contents and planned allocation can be specified before firing.

## Source assertions that must remain qualified

- Common shape/size is a reported source classification, not newly measured capacity equivalence.
- Same building/findspot is deposition context, not proof of identical original use event or final destination.
- Distinct glyphs do not establish distinct ancient spoken words.
- Sign identification does not independently establish profession, historical hand, institutional affiliation or separation of potter and inscriber.
- Fig12 and fig13 are distinct vessels; fig14/15 are enlargements, not extra independent objects.
- Cross-media examples repeated by one paper do not constitute new independent material observations until their original dependencies are checked.
- The source is previously published scholarship. New project inspection and new access do not create priority or an unseen validation outcome.

## Tests that could change the semantic comparison

1. Secure original object inventories and locus records, then independently characterize manufacture, capacity and fabric without using the sign as the grouping key.
2. Compare multiple vessels of the same controlled manufacture/form class whose residues or use contents are independently established. Mark/content association must persist after production group is controlled.
3. Conversely, compare differing marks within one independently established production batch, or one mark across independently distinct production groups. This can weaken a simple workshop/batch interpretation at an explicit scope.
4. Test the primary tablet/roundel references as a defined set, retaining layouts, reading alternatives and adverse instances. A sign's numeral slot may favor commodity use there without automatically transferring to vessels.
5. Document incision timing and marks independently of clean drawings; a strong timing correction could change the interpretation history.
6. Freeze a surviving scoped rule before any genuinely unused outcome. Current pair selection and source-led adaptation remain discovery.

No arbitrary minimum sample size is imposed. An exact local diagnostic contrast could suffice; larger generalizations need evidence appropriate to their scope.

## Review disposition

Accept the controlled pair and the restricted deterministic-model failure, with all stated source and physical limits. Do not accept a new translation, new positive contents classification, identified producer/recipient, or mission completion.

A narrow report can say: **within the author's shared vessel class, the incised sign varies; coarse form/size alone does not determine the mark. The additional administrative or production attribute remains unidentified.**

Stop this finite packet when its obtainable decisive dependencies cannot distinguish the grounded mechanisms. Reopen on a concrete new controlled comparison, not merely a stricter or looser phrasing of certainty.
