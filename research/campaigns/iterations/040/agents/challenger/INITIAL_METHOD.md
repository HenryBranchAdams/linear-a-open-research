## Diagnosis

**An exclusive already-known referent would be an unnecessarily strong admission rule for every intermediate semantic result.** If one insists on a direct event-to-named-referent join before considering any category-level inference, several legitimate convergent tests would never be admitted.

There are three different standards:

1. **Admit a test:** a finite packet could change the comparative support for an exact semantic proposition.
2. **Support a category or relationship:** separately grounded observations favor that proposition over serious specified alternatives, at an explicit confidence and scope.
3. **Establish the mission construction:** an action and relevant participant roles have independent support, serious alternatives have been distinguished, and the claimed generalization has appropriate validation.

Standard1 should not demand that standard3 already be met. Nor should standard2 be reported as standard3. The word “independent” describes the evidential chain, not a requirement that the answer was already known from another inscription.

## What convergence can establish

Physical typology can support an object's function without a surviving documented use event if its diagnostic features are well grounded independently of the proposed inscription meaning. Graphic form can constrain a sign's depicted source without determining its linguistic use. Repeated distribution in administrative texts can constrain its syntactic or numerical function without identifying an individual. Those constraints can converge on a narrow domain/category.

For example, if an independently supported weighing object carries a scale-like sign, and the same securely identified sign repeatedly occupies a controlled commodity–measurement–number slot on independent documents, the conjunction can favor a measurement-related use. That result need not yield the ancient spoken word, a precise absolute unit, or the reading of every occurrence. This is an operational example, not a finding that the project has already completed the required checks.

Convergence is stronger when each strand supplies a different constraint and was not selected or interpreted solely through the conclusion. It is weaker when the strands are one author's repeated description, when the object was classified from its inscription, or when the distribution was selected from only matching cases.

Do not multiply the apparent force of dependent clues. A picture of scales, a catalogue label derived from that picture, and a later paper quoting the catalogue are not three independent semantic grounds. Conversely, inferential dependence need not be all-or-nothing: source dependence can be disclosed while an original mass observation, a physical attachment and a separate document distribution still contribute distinct constraints.

## Serious alternatives versus unrestricted countermodels

A live rival must identify a mechanism and make at least one observable commitment within the defined scope.

| Rival status | Admission to comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Positively supported | Actual reading, distribution, object typology, contextual parallel or comparable marking practice favors it; must be tested. |
| Contextually plausible | A known relevant practice would produce the observed pattern; mechanism and predicted contrasts specified; retain with its weaker evidential status. |
| Bare possibility | “Could be an owner/account/ritual sign” with no applicable evidence or constrained prediction; list as a logical residual, not a veto that automatically defeats all support. |
| Unrestricted rescue model | Changes person, institution, unit, spelling or event arbitrarily for every inconvenient instance; cannot be treated as equally predictive. Its flexibility must be declared. |

A rival need not have as much evidence as the favored explanation to be serious. An ambiguous original reading or a well-attested contextual practice can suffice. Nor does absence of positive rival evidence make the favored explanation proven. The question is how the observations compare under constrained explanations, not how many English paraphrases can remain logically possible.

## Operational test-admission record

Admit a bounded semantic test when all the following are explicit:

1. **Proposition:** one exact occurrence or defined use class, claimed category/relation, and what remains outside the claim.
2. **Stable substrate:** object identities, target graphic feature, source access and materially consequential reading branches.
3. **Rivals:** strongest supported alternatives, their evidence and degree of flexibility; separate them from bare residual possibilities.
4. **Independent observations:** acquisition/source dependency graph showing which physical and textual facts are fixed without assuming the proposed value.
5. **Comparative predictions:** an obtainable observation that would favor, weaken or contradict at least one serious rival relative to the proposal. Differences may be graded; they need not be logically exclusive.
6. **Defined population:** inclusion/exclusion rules, denominator, candidate-selection path and confounds. Exact local autopsy evidence can be sufficient locally; distributional generalization needs a suitable comparison population.
7. **Decision rules:** what result merits source control, supported category, unresolved preference, or rejection; what will end the inquiry.
8. **Exposure:** discovery versus predeclared validation distinguished; no reset from new team, host or split.

An exploratory source inquiry may precede this record. It must not be retrospectively described as a preregistered semantic test.

## Evidence grades and permissible language

| Grade | Permissible claim |
| --- | --- |
| Source/reading constraint | “The source supports reading branchA and qualifies branchB.” |
| Descriptive association | “TargetX occurs disproportionately/invariably in this defined slot within the inspected population.” No untested translation follows. |
| Convergent category support | “Independent physical and controlled textual evidence favors a measurement-related use for this occurrence class over the specified rivals.” State limits and surviving alternatives. |
| Identified local relation | “The independently established physical/document relation bindsX to roleR in this local event.” No automatic general rule. |
| Replicated construction | “Action/role rule predicts appropriately independent instances and distinguishes the serious alternatives.” Appropriate mission review required. |

Do not turn these into a ladder where every task must reach the final rung. A supported category is substantive intermediate research if newly established by real evidence. But “plausible,” “favored,” “identified,” and “replicated” are different commitments, and the report must preserve that difference.

## Strongest falsification and stress tests

For a category/measurement proposal, useful tests include:

- **Occurrence census:** inspect every relevant instance in a fixed small corpus, including adverse examples; do not count only the examples cited by the favorable author.
- **Position and substitution:** compare the target's slots to ordinary syllabic names, known commodities, numerical operators and damaged entries; retain layout and reading branches.
- **Target-free object selection:** classify objects from physical/contextual features with the inscription masked or excluded, then examine target occurrence.
- **Confound matching:** compare like site, period, material, document type and layout so a site-specific emblem does not masquerade as a measurement classifier.
- **Independent linkage:** establish that linked records belong together from joins, seal/device, stratigraphy or other justified evidence before testing the written component; a linkage can remain uncertain and must be modeled as such.
- **Counterexamples:** securely read target use in a role forbidden by the proposed scoped rule; or securely established equivalent function using another component when the rule predicts exclusivity.
- **Reading sensitivity:** remove uncertain signs, interchange justified direction/segmentation branches, and ask what still holds.
- **Numerical robustness:** state units and allowable tolerances in advance; test plausible alternate standards; do not choose a convenient fraction after seeing a mass and call the fit independent.
- **Ablation of the favored clue:** remove iconographic resemblance, the object's interpreted function, or the disputed reading in turn. Identify which inference survives and which collapses.
- **Alternative mechanism fit:** require the designation model to say why the target occupies the same controlled numerical slot, rather than merely declare any pattern compatible with a name.
- **Exposure-aware replication:** freeze a surviving scope/rule before an unused outcome if available. Otherwise label the comparison retrospective and seek independent source/reading replication without inventing blindness.

A pattern can favor a hypothesis before a decisive counterexample exists. Use transparent qualitative likelihood comparisons if the sample does not justify numerical likelihoods. Avoid invented probability scores, arbitrary witness thresholds and significance testing on dependent faces/fragments.
