## Admission assessment

The packet merits the exact join: its classification uses a physical variable independent of the mark being compared, and the cast observations make the contrast more informative than exterior hole count. The source gives a reasonable mechanism for distinguishing a nodule that incorporates only one cord end/length from one that holds two ends together. This is sufficient for a **conditional local physical comparison** once identity and reading are secured.

The word *closure* needs qualification. The observed/reconstructed action is securing **two string ends at the nodule**, not an independently observed closure of a particular commodity container. A label-only nodule can hang from a container whose closure was secured elsewhere. Conversely, two ends embedded in clay could belong to an attachment loop without proving a historical receipt/issue or legal authentication event. These alternatives do not erase the mechanical contrast; they restrict its semantic reach.

## Predictions and surviving alternatives, still before writing outcomes

If one secure written element occurs on both table functions, it contradicts a deterministic rule that its local occurrence identifies exactly one of those two carrier functions. It cannot exclude a word for a shared operation, such as labeling, or a statement about goods/closure outside the nodule itself. Stage and referential scope matter: an instruction to perform an operation differs from a report that it happened, and either may refer beyond the present attachment.

If the marks differ, that alone does not identify the reason. Physical subtype, seal device, writer, administrative locality, date, status or content could produce the same partition. This finite set is selected for physical illustration and may not constitute a representative category sample. With so few selected objects, a faithful inventory table and explicit countermodels are preferable to significance scores.

The deterministic operation-code hypothesis is a useful testable bridge proposal. Unless an inspected source actually proposes that precise equivalence, its rejection should be described as a constraint on that bridge, not the refutation of an established decipherment. A result can be sound and locally useful without constituting semantic discrimination among all serious administrative meanings.
