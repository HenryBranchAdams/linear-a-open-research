# C043 — Cord arrangements and the writing on five nodules

## Result

The fixed physical packet does not provide a readable comparison across the two intended cord functions. One exact join is readable: **HMs557 = HT Wa1018**, conventionally **SI-KA**, on a carrier classified as labeling a knotted string-length. The two selected carriers classified as securing cord ends have explicit no-signs fields in the inspected catalogue. One is fragmentary. This closes the admission gate; it does not establish or reject a meaning for SI-KA.

This is source/object control. The proposed operation-code comparison was never admitted as a semantic test. A one-row correspondence would be vacuous.

## Decision and selection

The question was whether a stable mark tracks a separately reconstructed local cord function. The physical selection retained all four Haghia Triada inventories in Montecchi's table and the Zakros member of the illustrated cast series. The set was fixed before the current exact inscription joins, and the [analysis rule](ANALYSIS_RULE.md) was frozen. Montecchi's paper and several objects were already exposed in iteration 034; neither the set nor its objects are claimed as unused validation.

The catalogue fields report the preserved carriers. A fragment's no-signs field cannot establish that its complete original was never inscribed. ZA HMs1143 is not HT Wa1143. Inventory suffixes and site namespaces remain part of each identity.

## Independent physical evidence and its limits

Montecchi, *Classification, Use, and Function of Hanging Nodules in the Neopalatial Administrative Practices (Minoan Crete)*, AA 2017/1, pp. 3–7 and 13, supplies the physical classifications. Printed pages 3, 5, 6 and 13 were inspected as actual pixels by the source investigator, lead and reviewer. The photographs on page 5 show casts of internal cord impressions. Page 6 shows modern trials. They are not separate ancient observations. HMs557's exterior photograph shows apertures; the interior knot interpretation comes from the author's table.

“Securing” is restricted here to holding two cord ends at the nodule. No named container, document or shipment is independently attached to these five examples. A label-only nodule could have hung from an object secured elsewhere. The challenger identified this scope issue before seeing the exact-sign outcome; the lead accepted it.

Exact Arachne carrier records supply inventory, preservation and inscription fields. SigLA's HT Wa1018 entry supplies AB41 and AB77 and one SI-KA sequence; its modern drawing was actually viewed. The frozen discovery corpus agrees. These are distinct access levels: no new physical examination or reading is claimed. Original GORILA II selector 68 requests returned 503; the official CMS digitization returned an access-denied page. No original target CMS/GORILA page pixels were recovered by those routes.

## Analysis and challenge

[JOIN_FINAL.json](evidence/JOIN_FINAL.json) retains all five rows and the source fields. [check_join.py](analysis/check_join.py) reproduces admission and sensitivity summaries from that table; it does not translate signs. No readable securing-class carrier is available under the full set, HT-only set, or complete-carrier-only subset. Recoding every catalogue no-signs field as unknown also leaves the same missing comparison. No statistical test is warranted.

The [independent review](review/FINAL_REVIEW.md) reconstructs the joins and agrees with closure. Its independence is reasoning and checking, not an additional ancient witness. The decisive disagreement concerned scope, not an alternative reading; [adjudication](ADJUDICATION.md) records its resolution.

## What changed and what remains

The project now has an exact, reproducible join for this finite physical packet and a specific explanation of why it cannot answer the proposed question. It has not learned that SI-KA means a label, an operation, a person, an owner or an institution. Operation-code and entity/category interpretations remain untested here.

The next useful observation is a readable inscription on an exactly identified carrier whose internal two-end arrangement is documented, selected through a physical catalogue rather than by a desired sign. Another mirror of the present five records or an exterior two-hole photograph does not supply it. The branch is closed under its declared packet; C044 was opened on a materially different vessel/kiln comparison.
