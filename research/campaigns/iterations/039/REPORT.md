We then asked whether granting the proposed function would actually force a local written meaning.

These are compatibility judgments, not equal probabilities. The strongest alternative does not require denying the stone's weighing function. It denies that a useful physical object must name itself in its inscription. The inspected packet supplies no calibrated associated series, independently linked counterpart, or use record that defeats that alternative.
