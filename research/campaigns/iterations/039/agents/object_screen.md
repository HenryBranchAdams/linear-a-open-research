It reports two electronic masses (3450g and3511g), uncertain context/date, an autopsy-based scratch assessment, and a possible AB01/11 reading. It does not publish an object-specific ancient weighing event or independently linked transaction for L2615. ## Consequential source discrepancy

INSCRIBE says the numeral11 possibility applies if reading **left to right**. Montecchi printed184 says **right to left**. Both statements were inspected; do not silently reconcile them. The discrepancy may be orientational/editorial, but determining that requires the actual figure/surface and explicit orientation. It does not by itself reject either AB01 or11. The Z-scratch assessment and AB01/11 alternative are separate questions.
