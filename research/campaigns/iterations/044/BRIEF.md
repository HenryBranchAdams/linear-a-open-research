## Decision

Does the first vessel sign on HT31, conventionally A410VAS, independently exclude a ceramic production/order/receipt interpretation of the full list? The investigation must distinguish an iconographic/material argument from an independently joined ancient production event.

The accessible packet could change a substantive published interpretation. Militello2011 compares HT31 with the excavated kiln. Initial text suggests a tripod-based objection to assigning the entire record to kiln production. The companion Puglisi2011 catalogue includes an actual ceramic tripod cooking-pot waster (catalogue5.3, excavation c.29a1, firing-chamber context5). This is a grounded rival, not an imagined possible vessel. Its exact form, context and relevance must be inspected before adjudication.

## Competing interpretations

1. A410 depicts a form whose diagnostic features require a metal vessel; if that identification holds, a list composed exclusively of this kiln's ceramic products fails at that row.
2. A410 may denote a ceramic tripod cooking vessel or a broader form class including ceramics; the ceramic waster gives an independent material comparator. Three legs alone cannot establish metal.
3. HT31 is a broader inventory, allocation or acquisition list containing vessels of different materials. This remains possible even if a ceramic interpretation of A410 survives.

The claims that a sign depicts a tripod, that it denotes a metal vessel, that all entries are one production batch, and that the header names a supplier/recipient are separate. No material inference automatically supplies a transaction action or participant role.

## Method and criteria

Record the tablet sign's actual visible features and editorial reconstruction separately. Compare supports/legs, handles, body/rim shape and surface/material cues against the particular ceramic waster and the source's cited bronze/ceramic types. Do not infer material from a modern label, a schematic silhouette alone or an imposed translation. Do not require photographically exact proportions for a schematic sign; equally do not erase a genuinely diagnostic feature.

Verify catalogue, excavation and context identities without joining by proximity. Determine whether the waster is secure production evidence and whether it is representative of the kiln's repertoire. Determine whether HT31 itself is physically or chronologically joined to that kiln/batch. Room59 administrative context is not a kiln receipt by itself.

No numerical fit is planned. Compute only if original quantitative definitions and independent bounds warrant it; otherwise preserve a feature/identity matrix.
