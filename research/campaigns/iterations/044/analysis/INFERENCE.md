# C044 inference and sensitivity

This is a qualitative comparison of independently reported physical objects and a written pictogram. No numerical similarity score, probability or mechanical impossibility threshold was invented. The comparison was discovered retrospectively; the brief and reviewer criteria constrain its scope but do not make it an unused test.

## Evidence that changes comparative support

Three combined features make Malia3068 a close archaeological counterpart: basin shape, upright handles and elongated straight supports. The directly proposed kiln rival preserves a different body and handle, with no legs available. That changes the balance between these particular candidates. It is stronger than identifying both objects merely as tripods.

No observed characteristic of the sign directly measures material. The bridge from pictured morphology to counted referents remains an inference. Graphic conventions can retain forms beyond the original material or period; the target-specific extent of such convention is unknown.

The favorable result is therefore a **graded vessel-class interpretation**. The exclusive-kiln conclusion is conditional. It is not a statement that all serious alternatives are logically impossible or that independent evidence must identify a historical owner's name before any progress is allowed.

## Dependence and selection

HT31, Malia3068 and c.29a1 are separate ancient objects. They are not independently sampled statistical draws: the bronze was selected typologically, and the waster was selected as a concrete local rival. Vandenabeele's sign/object images underlie Duhoux and Militello. Repeated publications and independent reviewers do not create new archaeological witnesses.

The exact `*410-VS` token search is limited to the frozen project index and its notation. It does not establish that the sign has no other attestations in all editions. The index was exposed before this campaign; its sole literal target is not a validation set.

## Stop

Original morphological evidence, exact rival identity and the strongest material-neutral alternative have been examined. A bounded failed search for a closer ceramic form cannot justify a stronger result. The first useful extension is a diagnostic ceramic comparator or target-specific material use, followed by an independent event link if an administrative construction is pursued.
