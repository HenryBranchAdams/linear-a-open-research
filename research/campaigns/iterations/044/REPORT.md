# C044 — HT31's first vessel: a tested bronze-cauldron preference

## Result

**The first vessel sign on HT31 is better supported as a bronze-cauldron class than as the ceramic tripod cooking-pot class actually documented at the local kiln.** The independent support is a particular combination of features—rounded basin, upright loop handles and long straight supports—shared with an excavated bronze vessel, Malia, Quartier Mu, inventory 3068. This preference survives the exact local ceramic rival and a bounded follow-up. It is a graded local category interpretation, not a categorical rule that the sign could count only bronze objects.

The administrative consequence remains conditional: **if the first sign refers to bronze vessels, the complete list cannot consist exclusively of ceramic products from one firing installation.** This does not identify HT31 as an inventory, order, receipt or issue. No action or participant role is established, and the full mission remains unmet.

The result recovers and tests existing scholarship against a concrete rival. It is new to this investigation's controlled record, not a newly discovered vessel, a new scholarly decipherment or an independently replicated linguistic construction.

## Before and after

Before C044, the project had no retained, source-inspected claim joining HT31.1's form to an independently established material class. Reconnaissance found a potentially damaging rival: an actual ceramic tripod cooking-pot waster from the Haghia Triada kiln. If the bronze inference rested only on three legs, this would be a serious counterexample.

Original-source inspection changed that comparison. Duhoux already acknowledges ceramic tripods and argues specifically from long, thin supports. Vandenabeele supplies a fuller morphological comparison and an actual bronze photograph. The kiln waster preserves a cylindrical wall and a horizontal handle, with no legs available for comparison. It therefore does not contradict the stronger argument.

This is not an inference from unsuccessful searching. The positive bronze counterpart does the evidential work. Failure to find a close ceramic counterpart in the declared packet limits the challenge; it does not establish archaeological absence.

## Exact targets and source identities

The target is the first vessel on **HT31**, conventionally **A410VAS** in GORILA/Militello and **LA545 / VASd1** in Raison–Pope/Duhoux. The frozen discovery snapshot indexes it as `*410-VS`. These are source-specific notations for this target, not arbitrary equivalences across all vessel signs. A411, discussed separately for Chania roundels, is not the target.

The main bronze comparator is **Malia, Quartier Mu, inventory 3068**. The photograph and report identify a physical bronze vessel. It is from a different site and earlier context than HT31. It is neither the counted object nor a member of the Haghia Triada kiln assemblage. Its independence is archaeological object/material independence; its use as a comparator was selected by resemblance.

The local ceramic rival is **Puglisi 2011 catalogue 5.3 = excavation c.29a1**, attributed to **context 5**, the firing-chamber area. It is reported as a firing waster. That is positive production evidence from a specialist's examination. It does not place HT31 in that context or identify one firing batch.

## The comparison

| Feature | HT31.1 photograph and drawing | Malia3068 bronze | Kiln catalogue 5.3 ceramic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Curved, relatively shallow basin | Rounded basin | Cylindrical surviving wall; incomplete profile |
| Handles | Two upright loops above the rim | Three upright loops | Half of a horizontal cord handle |
| Supports | Three elongated, narrow incised strokes | Three long, straight supports | Not preserved in the catalogue description |
| Material evidence | None directly observed in the sign | Reported physical bronze vessel | Reported cooking fabric and firing-waster classification |
| Operational join | Tablet record | Typological comparator only | Exact kiln-context specimen; no tablet join |

The two-versus-three handle difference is real and preserved by Vandenabeele. It prevents an exact object identity claim but does not erase the closer combination of features. The ceramic fragment's missing legs are not counted as short legs or absent legs on the original vessel. Conversely, the broad term “tripod” does not make its horizontal handle and cylindrical wall identical to the sign's morphology.

The width of an incised line is not a measurement of a real vessel's support. We did not perform a mechanical test proving that ceramic legs of the depicted proportions were impossible. The material preference rests on combined morphology and an actual bronze counterpart, not on an alleged physical impossibility established from stroke thickness.

## Challenge and sensitivity

The challenger froze [initial criteria](review/INITIAL_CRITERIA.md), then reconstructed the original comparison before reading the lead's preferred synthesis. The [final review](review/FINAL_REVIEW.md) accepts a graded bronze-cauldron preference and insists on separating three propositions:

1. **Pictured type:** strongly supported as a cauldron form closely matched by a bronze specimen.
2. **Material of the counted category:** bronze is favored, but exclusive bronze extension is unconfirmed.
3. **Administrative scope:** an exclusively ceramic kiln consignment is disfavored only if the material restriction applies; no transaction direction follows.

The strongest surviving alternative is a **material-neutral conventional vessel category** whose shape derives from a bronze prototype. Militello's discussion of graphic persistence grounds that caution. The inspected sources do not demonstrate this exact sign used for independently identified ceramic objects, so the rival does not receive equal evidential weight merely because it is possible.

The finite ceramic follow-up covered the exact waster, Duhoux's direct ceramic cross-reference and a same-site tripod-tray lead. The tray is a flat perforated platform with triangular feet and a text-layer inventory inconsistency, so it is not a close counterpart. The cited “Duhoux2000” chapter resolves bibliographically to **Duhoux2003, “Un nom du ‘bronze’ en linéaire A ?”, pp.17–26**. Its §4.1.2 remains uninspected after the identified public routes failed. These limits are preserved, not converted into a negative census.

## Reading, scope and inference limits

The sign itself is sufficiently stable for this feature comparison. Damage to the first vessel's quantity prevents a numerical argument. The frozen header has a damaged initial span before TI-SA; source editions conventionally give I-TI-SA. **PU-KO may belong to the heading or qualify the following vessel.** A proposed “bronze” gloss would depend on the very material and scope arguments under test and cannot independently corroborate them. No such gloss is retained here.

Other vessel ligatures may name types or characteristics. Damaged numerals and differing segmentation are preserved in the [critical overlay](evidence/HT31_OVERLAY.json); they were not normalized to make an arithmetic fit. The script at [extract_target.py](analysis/extract_target.py) reproduces the exact frozen row and literal `*410-VS` index matches. This verifies project exposure/selection, not linguistic replication or corpus completeness.

Militello's approximate Room59 attribution derives from old inventories and excavation documentation. It is not an attachment to a kiln, an exact consignment or a transaction counterpart. Puglisi's reconstructed deposits include abandonment, collapse and redeposition. Consequently, repertoire overlap or firing-capacity compatibility cannot supply the missing event relation. A broader mixed-material acquisition, allocation, inventory or issue remains possible. The published argument itself does not reject all orders or receipts.

## End state and next decision

C044 closes after source reconstruction, the bounded ceramic challenge and independent review. Highest level: **graded local semantic discrimination at the vessel-category level**, with a **conditional material/scope constraint**. No exclusive material rule, syllabic translation, action, participant role, unused validation or mission completion is claimed.

The highest-value outstanding source target is the exact ceramic comparison in Duhoux2003 §4.1.2. A securely identified ceramic vessel preserving the combined body, handles and supports could weaken the material preference. Target-specific evidence that A410 counted ceramic referents would directly challenge the material restriction. An exactly linked vessel/account or production/dispatch event would be needed to advance toward action and participant roles.
