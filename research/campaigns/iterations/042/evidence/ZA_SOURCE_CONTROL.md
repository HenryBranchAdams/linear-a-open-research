# ZA Zb3: source, measurement and numeral control

24 October2026. Lead investigator. This is a retrospective original-source inspection, not object autopsy. KN evidence is recorded separately in the source investigator's report.

## Exact carrier and independent capacity

Jan P. Stronk, **“A Pithos from Epanô Zakro,” TALANTA4 (1972),85–87**, is the original source of the often repeated556L estimate. The official journal PDF was acquired and all three page images actually inspected. Printedp85 describes the reconstructed pithos discovered in1965 in RoomTheta of the Epano Zakros building, approximately150cm high and85cm in maximum width. The title and find context are retained exactly; this is not a vessel from a generic Zakros palace storeroom.

Printedp86 estimates **556L with an allowance of ±11%**, using πR²h with an average interior radius and the maximum wine height. This is a geometric approximation, not a water-fill measurement or a modern3-D reconstruction. Numerical radius, fill height and profile coordinates are not supplied. The allowance is the author's estimate of error, not a statistical confidence interval. It is used as a source-specific sensitivity range of494.84–617.16L; the central value is not exact.

Stronk's reading is **22**, yielding his roughly25.2L unit. The page87 photographs show the excavation vessels and inscribed fragment, at poor printed quality. They do not allow this team to decide the competing numeral strokes. The published physical inspection is Stronk's, with assistance acknowledged from S. Alexiou; no new examination of the ancient object occurred here.

## The short follow-up does not establish32

Stronk, **“Linear A Liquid Measure: A Contribution,” TALANTA14–15 (1982–1983),27**, was acquired from the official journal and its one page actually inspected. It retains the old556L±11% capacity and conditionally adopts Jan Best's **21**, giving26.48±2.92L. It says liquid filling is impracticable and a mathematical approach is needed. It does not revise22 to32. The follow-up is dependent on the1972 capacity observation, not a second independent measurement.

Best, **“The Zakro Pithos Inscription, Again,” TALANTA14–15 (1982–1983),9–15**, was acquired as a seven-page scan. At the first checkpoint its pixels had not yet been inspected; the numerical21 branch is supported by Stronk's explicitly attributed conditional adoption and is not promoted to the preferred edition reading. The journal's download name ends9–16; the actual PDF contains seven pages. Keep filename and printed pagination distinct.

**Inspection update:** the lead subsequently viewed actual originalpp9–11, including the photograph and reproduced Brice drawing onp10. Best'sp9 argument is specific: the disputed third horizontal stroke near the VIN sign is interpreted as damage rather than a third ten; a lower vertical stroke is interpreted as a divider rather than a second unit. This makes21 a materially grounded historical reading alternative. The photograph is insufficient for this team to settle those strokes, and the supplied drawing is an editor's interpretation. Best's later lexical/translation proposals are not adopted. Pages12–15 remain uninspected because they are unnecessary to the finite numeral sensitivity test.

## Why32 remains the principal comparison

The current corpus/edition tradition gives32. Olivier1992, *Mykenaïka*, printedp446, explicitly compares ZA Zb3's32 after the same logogram with KN Zb27's then revised117. Both the source investigator and lead actually inspected that original page. This supports the preferred32 comparison in a primary scholarly source; it is not a fresh stroke adjudication from ZA photographs. The direct GORILAIV112–113 image route failed during this campaign, so those pages are not claimed as inspected. All21/22/32 branches are included in sensitivity, with different source status preserved.

## New measurement lead: incomplete access

Erika Notti, **“Naming, Writing and Measuring ‘at the Time of Minos’. Some Reflections on Bronze Age Aegean Notations on Clay Vessels and their Representations,” Pasiphae19 (2025),195–212**, DOI10.19272/202533301012, reports a new ZA measurement in its abstract and suggests12–12.5L per written unit. The institutional record marks the full file unavailable; the publisher sells the article. The publicly exposed Torrossa sample is page195 and was actually inspected. It contains the abstract and introductory discussion, not the measurement method or result table.

Do not treat12–12.5L as an inspected physical capacity, multiply it by32 and call the result a measurement, or silently replace the1972 estimate. The full article's geometric method, restored profile, fill convention and numeral premise remain an exact access dependency. A sales HTML response returned with a misleading PDF-like route; its actual content type was checked and recorded as HTML, not an acquired article. No purchase, correspondence or access bypass occurred. Further metadata mirrors cannot resolve this dependency.

## Decision relevance

The old capacity estimate is independent of the proposed words' meaning but is not a precision assay. It can constrain a full-container/shared-unit model together with KN's physical scale. It cannot establish the actual contents or fill level at inscription, a shipment boundary, a recipient, or the identity of an inscriber. Bibliographic repetition of556L does not replicate its measurement.
