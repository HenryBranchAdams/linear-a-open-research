# C042 capacity test: analysis rule before the two-vessel comparison

Date: 2026-10-24. Analyst: comparative agent.

## Exposure at this point

The lead has supplied the proposed target KN Zb 27 / HM5194, a source-reported 34-litre capacity, and reading alternatives 117 / 17. The second carrier is ZA Zb 3; its capacity and number have not yet been supplied to this analyst. This is a source-led discovery comparison, not a blind test. The basic targets occur in existing corpora, and unknown historical exposure cannot count as unused evidence.

## Finite models

M1: each preserved number q directly states this individual carrier's full capacity in one common positive volumetric unit u: V = q*u. The necessary unit-free condition for any two objects is V1/V2 = q1/q2. With measurement intervals [L1,U1], [L2,U2], common-u feasibility is the nonempty intersection of [L1/q1,U1/q1] and [L2/q2,U2/q2]. No measurement uncertainty will be invented: exact published approximations are treated as nominal and a reported tolerance required for formal interval decisions; if absent, report the magnitude of adjustment needed.

M2: q records an amount physically held in the individual carrier, potentially below full capacity: 0 < q*u <= V. Without an independently grounded lower bound on u or a minimum filling fraction, any finite positive capacities/numbers have solutions. It cannot be rejected solely by a mismatch of V/q across objects.

M3: q refers to several containers, cumulative throughput, allocation/obligation or another accounting total beyond this carrier. These do not predict V=q*u for one object. Capacity comparisons cannot choose among them without separate scope evidence.

M4: individual full capacities are recorded with different units by site, commodity, date or metrological system: V_i=q_i*u_i. With unconstrained u_i this is saturated and not a common-unit test. Contextual evidence must independently restrict unit equivalence.

M5: one common unit with a common filling fraction f, q_i*u=f*V_i. For positive common f this has the same ratio condition as M1. Different independently unbounded fractions f_i remove this restriction and reduce to M2.

## Selection and treatment rules

- Fix the two named objects; no selection of additional objects by achieved numerical fit.
- Preserve all source-grounded number branches, including whether 17 is a current reading or a historical restoration error. Treat number deletion only if an editor provides a reason.
- Verify object identity, completeness/restoration, capacity method, and whether the inscription can refer to this same surviving vessel at the relevant time.
- Do not import the Mycenaean 28.8-litre unit (or any other calibration) to force a result. Any calibrated calculation is a separate conditional check and must cite its independent justification.
- Do not infer a transaction direction, owner, recipient, contents or lexical gloss from compatibility or failure.
- Present feasible-unit intervals, required unit/fill-ratio adjustments, and the weakest surviving rival. A failed M1 gives a narrow semantic/scope constraint under its premises, not a broad decipherment.

## Stop

Stop the computation when the published readings/capacities establish either non-overlap at justified uncertainty or underdetermination; more fitted parameters or repeated unit conversions are not additional evidence. Reopen only for calibrated unit, actual contents/use level, exact physical scope, or a materially different source reading.
