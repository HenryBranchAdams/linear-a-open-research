# Campaign 042: what the two jar inscriptions can and cannot mean

**Completed 25 October 2026 (UTC); opened 24 October. Starting authority B2026-10-18 + C038–C041. Level reached: reading/source control and a conditional structural/physical constraint. No semantic experiment or action-and-participant construction established.**

## Result

The numeral expressions on **KN Zb 27 / HM 5194** and **ZA Zb 3** cannot both state their own individual carrier's full capacity in one shared volumetric unit, given the inspected source capacities. A model in which both vessels are filled to the same fraction of capacity fails for the same reason. This conclusion requires no choice of a Linear B liquid unit.

That excludes a specific conjunction of assumptions. It does **not** show that either inscription records a shipment, an offering, a group of containers, a received amount, or a participant. The strongest surviving alternative is a smaller or local denomination. Variable partial filling also permits single-carrier scope. These are serious alternatives; de Fidio 2024 explicitly discusses the small-unit interpretation alongside a collective label.

The project also needs a reading correction: the preferred source numeral on KN Zb 27 is **107**, based on Del Freo's report of his and Zurbach's22 June 2012 physical examination, published 2017. The inherited frozen corpus has17; Olivier 1992 and Christakis 2010 use117. Those bytes remain preserved, with an explicit overlay rather than a silent replacement.

The size/number anomaly is **not a new scholarly discovery**. Olivier 1992p. 446 already observed it. C042 supplies a controlled source history, an explicit test that does not import an absolute unit, quantified sensitivity, and a clear boundary between physical incompatibility and an administrative reading. [S1–S8]

## Discriminating calculation

Let V be vessel capacity, q its written numeral and u a positive common volume unit. A full-capacity reading requires V=q×u on each carrier. Therefore V_KN/q_KN and V_ZA/q_ZA must agree. A common filling fraction cancels in the same ratio.

| Input / consequence | KN Zb 27 | ZA Zb 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Preferred source numeral |107|32|
| Published approximate capacity |34 L|556 L|
| Implied unit **if full capacity is what the numeral records** |about 0.318 L|17.375 L|

Those implied scales differ by about 54.7. Using even the lower end of Stronk's stated allowance,494.84 L, a107-unit KN carrier would have to hold about**1,655 L**, around 48.7 times its reported34 L. This is a necessary revision under the model, not a p-value or a statistical confidence statement.

All nine historical combinations, KN107/117/17 by ZA 32/22/21, fail the full/shared-unit comparison at the published capacities. The weakest case,17 against 32, still needs KN to hold about 262.88 L at ZA's lower estimate. Treating all numbers as source branches avoids allowing a convenient reading to determine the outcome. The finite sensitivity set is not a new adjudication of damaged strokes.

### An additional physical robustness check

The actual HM 5194 profile in Christakis Fig. 6.2 shows width below height. With an explicitly assumed approximately axisymmetric shape and outer diameter no greater than the published53 cm height, the entire vessel fits within a53 cm-by53 cm cylinder. That deliberately generous envelope holds about 116.93 L, below even the weakest historical requirement262.88 L.

This is a **post hoc, profile-dependent conditional bound**. It is not a newly measured jar capacity, a reported confidence interval, or independent validation. A single profile does not prove all transverse diameters or image calibration. The challenger accepted its limited use because the assumptions are visible and the bound is deliberately loose. It addresses the unavailable34 L method without supplying a new ancient event.

## What survives, and why

**A small or local unit.** Full individual-container readings can fit if the inscriptions use different units. The approximate0.30 L proposal for KN is already in published discussion. Fitting a unit by dividing capacity by numeral cannot prove that denomination existed; equally, lack of a known name for it does not refute the rival. A common commodity designation is insufficient to enforce identical units across places and periods.

**Variable partial filling in a common small unit.** For contents, rather than full capacity, the condition is q×u≤V. Any positive u no larger than the smaller V/q satisfies both inequalities. With preferred readings and nominal capacities, u=34/107 gives KN34 L and ZAabout 10.17 L, roughly 1.83% of ZA capacity. This is a constructive countermodel, not a proposed historical reconstruction or evidence that the vessel was used nearly empty. No inspected source establishes its fill at the relevant marking event.

**A scope extending beyond one container.** A collective or cumulative quantity, or an allocation spanning multiple containers or repeated fillings, can exceed the marked carrier's capacity. An intended single filling remains subject to that carrier's capacity. A wider-scope model is compatible with the evidence, but this comparison does not distinguish it from the alternatives above. A suitable linked record could do so; a sanctuary narrative cannot replace that link.

**Other non-volume readings.** The test is explicitly a volumetric model. It does not establish that the number must be a volume rather than another counted category. The finite result remains useful precisely because its premises are explicit.

Prefiring inscription, possible imported manufacture and sanctuary deposition constrain different stages. They do not make the potter, inscriber, sender, seal operator, recipient and written referent identical. The proposed word DI-NA-U is not translated by this analysis; its segmentation/phonetic status is inherited and not required to calculate the numeral relation.

## Stop and continuation

The bounded candidate is closed. Additional arithmetic over these same capacities cannot select an administrative meaning. The most consequential missing observation is an independent denomination or scope relationship applicable to the inscription: a unit equation, a securely matched container/account counterpart, or actual-use evidence establishing the relevant fill or quantity boundary.

Two concrete source tasks remain useful, with different potential effects. The complete Notti 2025 measurement section could update ZA's geometric input; an HM 5194 interior profile, restoration record and original capacity calculation could test34 L. Neither automatically determines semantic scope. The [continuation record](CONTINUATION.md) and prepared, unsent evidence requests state exact objects and required records. No correspondence, purchases, publication or recurring search occurred.
