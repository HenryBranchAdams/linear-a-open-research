# C042: candidate-specific challenger criteria, frozen before detailed synthesis

Date: 2026-10-24. Target named by lead: KN Zb27/HM5194 and ZA Zb3. I have not read the source investigator's notes or the lead's preferred outcome. These criteria precede my inspection of the detailed measurements, readings, and numerical comparison.

## Exact comparison

Test whether each inscription can quantify one full or partial filling of its own physical carrier, under explicitly stated commodity, numerical scope, and unit premises. Competing accounts include an aggregate over other vessels, repeated fillings or flows through this vessel, different capacities/units across sites, or an administrative quantity that is not a volume. None is an established translation.

## Admission gates

1. Join each published capacity or reconstruction to the exact inscription carrier using explicit inventory/edition concordance. A similarly numbered jar or a room's mean pithos capacity is insufficient.
2. Record measurement method and uncertainty: measured surviving volume, reconstructed total volume, geometric approximation, nominal capacity, or author-reported estimate. A published capacity is not automatically an actual measured fill.
3. Verify numeral and commodity/logogram readings and their scope against original edition and any correction. Preserve fraction, missing signs, damaged numeral, and corrections. A normalized corpus alias is not an independent product identification.
4. Establish whether the inscription belongs to the jar's manufacture, later marking, or unknown stage. This can constrain the timing of a proposed content statement, but does not itself identify the quantified referent.
5. Keep intersite common-unit equivalence explicit. No Linear B unit such as 28.8 L is automatically imported into Linear A. Separate direct source estimates from assumptions.

## Finite quantitative tests

- For numeral n and carrier capacity C, a full-single-fill reading requires unit u=C/n (within the capacity/reading interval). Compare these implied-unit intervals. Non-overlap rejects common-unit full-fill equivalence under declared premises.
- A partial-fill reading requires n*u <= C. It may survive even when full-fill equivalence fails; report upper bounds on u instead of claiming that all contents readings fail.
- If an independent lower bound on unit u exists, compare it with C/n. Only independent unit information can rule out partial fill through overcapacity.
- Distinguish mass, volume, count and administrative units; conversion requires independently supported commodity density or count capacity where relevant.
- For uncertain numerator/denominator readings and reconstructed capacities, show the plausible interval or finite alternatives. Avoid presenting a point estimate's mismatch as decisive when source uncertainty spans the difference.
- If one inscription's quantity applies to a group of vessels or an annual/repeated-use flow, this is a different scope; a single-carrier contradiction does not refute it.

## Serious alternatives and asymmetry

Full-fill nominal capacity and actual partial contents are different hypotheses. Intended future contents can obey the same capacity bound as one present filling, while a transaction through time need not. A production batch marker need not encode carrier capacity. Units may differ by commodity, site, time or notation; test positive evidence for shared units rather than accepting arbitrary free parameters or forbidding all variation.

Prefer a local exclusion on one secure carrier if it needs fewer cross-site assumptions than the paired comparison. Conversely, if no unit lower bound is independent, non-overlapping full-fill implied units supports only a common-scale exclusion. It does not establish aggregate accounting as the unique remaining meaning.

## Independence and review

Capacities and numerals must not be estimated from each other. Shared modern metrological convention is not independent ancient corroboration. Two papers repeating one vessel measurement are one physical observation. The exact two carriers are selected in reconnaissance, so the comparison is retrospective discovery/constraint, not an unused validation set.

I will reconstruct the source chain and arithmetic independently; disagreements will be documented before adjudication. Highest possible result from this packet alone is a structural/physical constraint or a narrowly scoped semantic discrimination among stated quantity meanings.
