# Independent challenger: initial criteria

Date: 2026-10-24. Prepared before receipt of a favored candidate or synthesis.

## Decision standard

A local semantic discrimination is an observation that changes comparative support between serious context-grounded interpretations of an exact written element. It need not identify a historical name or eliminate every imaginable story. Local scope must be explicit: object, event, dossier, institution, period, or construction. A conclusion about one scope does not silently extend to the whole script.

## Variables to keep distinct

- Physical maker: formed or manufactured the object.
- Inscriber: produced the surviving written marks.
- Seal operator: applied a particular impression in a particular event.
- Seal owner: controlled a seal; not necessarily its operator.
- Institution: administrative entity whose procedures structured an event.
- Owner/controller of goods: not necessarily issuer, recipient, or inscriber.
- Issuer and recipient: directional relationships relative to an established transfer.
- Written referent: what a stable element denotes, which can differ from all actors above.

An object's function, manufacturing sequence, archive location, or seal association can constrain possible meanings without identifying the written referent. Positive evidence for a production rival should be tested; a hypothetical production story unsupported by the packet should not automatically veto a narrower conclusion.

## Source-chain requirements

1. Verify the exact source, object identifier, locus, date, and any joining identifier. Inventory, catalogue, sherd, specimen, seal, and laboratory codes remain separate until an explicit concordance joins them.
2. Distinguish actual inspected pixels, page text, author-reported physical examination, and our own observation. Text extraction does not inspect damage or sign shape.
3. Record stable reading, segmentation, damage, erasure, and layout alternatives before comparing interpretations.
4. Identify independent evidence for the physical event or relationship; the proposed translation cannot establish that relationship and then use it as corroboration.
5. State which source, object/event, and inference are independent. Separate texts summarizing the same excavation are not two ancient observations.

## Rival reconstruction

Choose the strongest alternative supported by context and evidence. Compare predictions on the same records. Narrow roles that can coexist until a genuine difference remains; e.g., 'institution' and 'recipient' can both describe one entity. A simple equivalence proof is sometimes more informative than a false binary. Explicitly test whether quantity, vessel capacity, product category, batch, account designation, workshop, owner, and recipient could generate the observed association.

## Selection and analysis checks

- Declare the finite packet and selection rule independently of the desired reading.
- Count ancient events and objects, not publications, photographs, or multiple signs on the same object, as independent units when appropriate.
- Check plausible readings, grouping, layout scope, units, and source-dependent measurement alternatives.
- Arithmetic closure, corpus clustering, and analogy generate or constrain interpretations; they need an independently known physical or social relationship to establish semantics.
- If an observation is expected under both serious rivals, call it nondiscriminating even if it makes a preferred narrative plausible.
- Permit a directly diagnostic counterpart or use record to carry a local result; do not impose an arbitrary minimum sample size.

## Exposure and challenge

Record prior exposure honestly. My independent reasoning cannot supply independent ancient evidence. Freeze criteria or a rule before unused evidence when genuinely unused material exists. Retrospective data splits do not restore unused status. Report damaging alternatives even when unresolved. Adjudication must address each material objection or reduce the claim.

## Outcome labels

Access; reading/object control; structural/physical constraint; semantic discrimination; linguistic replication; full action-and-participant mission. Assign the highest level actually earned and preserve useful lower-level progress without treating it as decipherment.

## Candidate stopping rules

Stop when the finite available packet cannot discriminate, a proposed discriminator collapses, or concrete access routes are exhausted. Record the exact observation required to reopen. A failed candidate does not close materially different opportunities.
