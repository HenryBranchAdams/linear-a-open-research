## Decisive correction

The preferred numeral must be **107**, not 117. Maurizio Del Freo's 2017 report, printed p. 17, says that he and Julien Zurbach examined KN Zb 27 at the Heraklion Museum on **22 June 2012**, establishing 107 after AB 131 in place of Olivier's 117. The report promises publication in a supplement to GORILA. Footnote 77 refers to a question already raised in the 2006–2010 report, p. 14. The 2017 author's full text was inspected; the corresponding PDF page pixels were not acquired. This is a **reported physical re-examination**, not a new examination of the ancient object by this team.

## What the capacity source actually supports

K. S. Christakis's 2010 article identifies **HM 5194** as the inscribed vessel in Fig. 6.2 (p. 51) and Figs. 6.3–6.4 (p. 53). It gives height **53 cm**, capacity **about 34 litres**, and an uncertain choice between the two Temple Repositories cists. The article text supplies no target-specific measurement method, fill level, error estimate, or maximum diameter. Its p. 52 reports a prefiring inscription and uses the then accepted 117 reading. Associated vessels HM 5192, HM 5193 and SMP 1568 are separate objects; their attribution to one transported batch is the author's inference. The inferred Cycladic origin rests on morphology and macroscopic fabric assessment. No target contents assay or independently witnessed shipment is provided.

## Grounded rival and implications

A source lead in Pia de Fidio's 2024 chapter, “The absolute values of the symbols for volume,” in John Killen (ed.), *The New Documents in Mycenaean Greek* I, 169–204, target pp. 179–180, explicitly juxtaposes a group-label interpretation with an implicit unit derived from the small vessel's capacity, on the order of 0.30 L. It uses 107 and cites *Aegean Scripts* p. 17. The relevant chapter text was accessible through a public Scribd rendering, which served to locate the original 2017 autopsy report. A publisher/author PDF of this chapter was not acquired. This lead should not be treated as independent ancient evidence. It demonstrates that an implicit local/subunit interpretation is a serious published rival rather than an ad hoc possibility invented by this investigation. [Text locator](https://www.scribd.com/document/750994689/Killen-The-New-Documents-in-Mycenaean-Greek-Vol-1-2024), turn64view0–2. Exact chapter DOI: **10.1017/9781139029049.007**. The [official Cambridge chapter page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/new-documents-in-mycenaean-greek/absolute-values-of-the-symbols-for-volume/ACE8A291774C4E64D6DC53CD91782EFF) offers access purchase/login and only a first-page preview; the target pp.179–180 are outside that preview. This bounded route is closed for this phase without payment, correspondence or bypass.

The original source chain permits a bounded comparison. For a shared positive volume unit and a reading in which the numeral gives each carrier's full capacity, the capacity/number ratios must agree. The nominal KN ratio is 34/107 L. The comparative analyst should test ZA measurements and reading branches with their own source qualifications. An inconsistency can reject that shared-full-capacity model; it cannot on its own decide among different implicit units, partial filling, cumulative quantities, a batch label, or another scope. Known object function and prefiring marking do not establish the meaning of the preceding written group.
