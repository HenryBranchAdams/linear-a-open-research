## Independent observation and rival predictions

Physical capacity is established from the exact object or a documented profile independently of a proposed translation. Under a single-carrier full-capacity model, V = q × u. Under a single-carrier actual-contents model, q × u ≤ V. A common unit is a separate premise; different fills and different site/period units are different models. A multi-container, cumulative, intended or ceremonial account may exceed the physical carrier but excess alone does not choose among them.

Do not impose the later Mycenaean 28.8 L value as a known Linear A constant. First test dimensionless/cross-vessel constraints and sensitivity to exact reading, scope, unit and filling. Any direct source evidence of measurement conventions or same-unit scope takes priority over analogy.
