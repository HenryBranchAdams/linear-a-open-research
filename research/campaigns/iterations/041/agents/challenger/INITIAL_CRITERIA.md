## Exact question and constrained hypotheses

The comparative question is whether preserved marking follows independently identified contents/use rather than a defined manufacturing distinction, or manufacturing rather than contents/use. The scope is an exact occurrence or declared occurrence class, not every use of these signs.

H-C: within the declared relevant convention and at the assay/use resolution specified before interpretation, the mark distinguishes a content/use category.

H-P: within the declared relevant convention, the mark distinguishes an independently characterized production group or manufacturing category.

Declare whether either model asserts a fixed one-category/one-mark mapping, allows synonyms/subclasses, or makes only a graded association prediction. State such flexibility in advance. Broad unconstrained “it could be a maker” or “it could be any ingredient” models cannot veto all results. Conversely, neither a preferred sign meaning nor an inferred workshop may supply its own independent grouping.

These models need not be mutually exclusive. Manufacture can specialize by contents or intended use. If every observed mark changes together with both variables, the packet remains confounded.

Acceptable routes include:

## Gate2: what does the analysis actually identify?

For contents/use, preserve the assay's actual resolution and diagnostic support:
- Sample location and material: interior absorbed residue, adhering deposit, exterior coating, sediment, associated macroremain or another substrate.
- Analytical method, actual diagnostic observation, comparison/control basis and the authors' uncertainty.
- Whether the result establishes a broad material class, a specific plant/animal/product, a mixture, a thermal/use process or merely an ambiguous biomarker.
- Whether local/burial contamination and relevant conservation materials were considered sufficiently for the claimed inference.

A general lipid signal is not automatically olive oil, a fatty-acid ratio is not automatically one species, and soot is not automatically the vessel's stored contents. Such refinements require the source's own diagnostic evidence. Do not impose tests unrelated to the actual claimed category.

Record sampling date and treatment history when they could materially affect the outcome.9384is reconstructed and has been used in a moderngranule-fill capacity experiment; sampling location/history may therefore matter. This is a reason to inspect controls, not an assertion of contamination or an automatic exclusion of every result. A well-supported source inference can survive incomplete treatment metadata if the missing details do not undermine its diagnostic basis.

An absent/non-detected residue does not establish ancient emptiness, a distinct commodity or original non-use. Positive detection and non-detection have different evidential force.

For manufacture, distinguish measured fabric/technology class from workshop identity. Petrographic or chemical similarity can support a production grouping at its validated resolution; it does not automatically identify one potter/workshop. Different finishing can occur within a workshop. Apply the same resolution discipline to both sides of the comparison.

## Decision rules

A. **Access-only:** source/table unavailable, metadata only or wrong-item hit. Record exact attempted route; no negative claim about the unseen source.

B. **Exact object/analytical control:**Gate1passes andGate2yields a justified observation, but no independent contrast separatesH-CandH-P. Add the actual constraint without calling a semantic identification.

C. **Supported local association/category:** diagnostic independent use/function evidence plus secure target writing supports a narrow association; serious specified rivals assessed. Report favored/support level and all operative assumptions. Do not require an independently named person.

D. **Comparative category discrimination:** a cross-variable contrast changes support forH-CagainstH-Por vice versa, robust to consequential reading/grouping/assay branches. State the precise scoped model weakened/excluded and strongest survivor.

E. **Linguistic or mission claim:** requires additional independent support for the expressed action and relevant roles, plus validation appropriate to the claimed scope. PassingA–Ddoes not by itself satisfy the mission.

F. **Contradiction or failed join:** an explicit original map shows a different carrier/sample, or the proposed assay interpretation conflicts with actual diagnostic evidence. Reject only that asserted join/inference; preserve the original source observation and corrected target.
