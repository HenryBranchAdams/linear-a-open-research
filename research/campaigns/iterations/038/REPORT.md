**Campaign:** LA-C038-2026-10-21 · **Date:** 21 October 2026

## Executive result

**This campaign found no new independent semantic anchor.** It made a narrower, defensible change: KH Wc2123 now has a source-controlled reading overlay, and the project can no longer use its flattened index entry as an unqualified, unchanged label associated with the surviving stamps.

The inherited index contains one token, `*810-PI-KA`, without erasure notation. The original edition instead treats the entire three-element group as partly erased, retains the final pair as PI-KA, and tentatively identifies the separate first mark. Later discussion preserves a materially different identification and defers the sign's acceptance. The edition also reports erasure before stamping. This is a **reported physical sequence**, not a newly verified microscopic sequence or a translation. [S03–S05]

The central semantic question remains unanswered. PI-KA could identify an accountable entity, a distinct counterparty, or an account/category. It could also belong to an earlier entry whose referent did not continue into the stamped use. Switching among those assignments leaves the acquired observations intact. Neither a responsible party nor a recipient is independently bound to the writing.

Two narrow scientific records have been added: **LA-038-001**, a source-control qualification of the inherited row, and **LA-038-002**, the conditional physical exclusion supported by the editors' chronology. These are new project records of published evidence and its limits, not new ancient witnesses or claims of scholarly priority. No existing translation has been promoted or refuted. Flouda's complete 2024 argument remains uninspected and access-limited; A588 remains parked under the previous baseline.

## 2. Decisive evidence and access limits

The target is the incised face of **KH Wc2123**, explicitly identified in its first edition as **Khania Museum KH2123**. The source packet is Andreadaki-Vlasaki and Hallager 2007, pp.13–15, fig.5 on p.14 and auxiliary fig.7 on p.16; Del Freo, p.100; and the Comité des signes resolutions, p.586. Exact citations and acquisition scope appear below and in `evidence/EVIDENCE_RECORD.json`.

There is one carrier. The edition reports nine surviving impressions attributable to one device, and places recovery in secondary Geometric fill. Those observations do not independently count transactions, operators, historical persons, or original participants. The drawings, photographs, mirror and author upload are representations of the same published object. [S03]

The primary edition's complete target section was read through a public full-text reproduction; actual pixels of the author-hosted figure5 were inspected. Figure7 was partially cropped and served only as an auxiliary check. The publisher's PDF routes returned errors, and a complete local copy of the original article was not obtained. Web screenshot attempts initially returned placeholders; those were not counted as visual inspection. The later author-hosted image acquisition supplied the actual visual check.

Both later PDFs were acquired, but only the relevant pages and necessary metadata were deliberately examined in detail. Del Freo records the proposed tripod-logogram alternative. The committee leaves A373 unresolved because of the erased surface and a photograph/drawing discrepancy. Neither target paragraph establishes a new target-specific autopsy or a new ancient witness. The printed 2024 volume, the 2021 committee meeting, and the online 2025 release are different dates. [S04–S05]

The team could not identify a decisive crosscut from the acquired images. Therefore the physical chronology is attributed to the original editors throughout. A cleaner drawing cannot settle a photograph's uncertain strokes. A possible ideogram cannot become a securely identified product by appearing beside a syllabic pair.

## 3. Why the physical relationship does not select a semantic role

The relevant inferential chain has six steps:

1. Identify the carrier and its marks.
2. Read a stable written component.
3. Reconstruct the order of physical operations.
4. Establish that the written referent continued into the relevant operation.
5. Bind that referent to a particular administrative relationship.
6. Establish what action or participant role the language expresses.

This packet supports source-controlled work on steps1–2 and a conditional assessment of step3. It does not supply steps4–6. Sharing a clay carrier is not sufficient to cross those gaps, especially when the writing was altered.

Responsibility and receipt can coincide historically. To avoid a false contrast, the decisive comparison below asks whether PI-KA names the responsible entity **rather than a distinct counterparty** in a possible event. This is a logical contrast for testing identifiability, not a claim that this roundel's unknown participants actually were distinct. The separate account/category and abandoned-entry models make the unresolved range wider.

| Observation or requirement | Responsible-entity model | Distinct-counterparty model | Earlier-entry/account model | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Same visible writing and surviving stamp pattern | A responsibility designation could occur on this carrier. | A counterparty designation could occur on the same carrier. | An earlier designation could remain partly visible. | Compatible with all; no role selected. |
| Reported writing → smoothing → stamping | Could be correction of a responsibility entry. | Could be correction of a counterparty entry. | Could be correction or reuse before a later operation. | Ordering alone does not specify the reason or referent. |
| First mark read as A373, tripod variant, or uncertain | Does not independently identify PI-KA's owner or function. | Same absence of a referent link. | Same absence of a referent link. | No justified reading branch resolves the role. |
| Independent identity of the person/institution operating the seal | Required to connect an operator to a designation; still insufficient by itself to prove reference. | Would permit a distinct counterparty unless independently excluded. | Does not establish continuity with the earlier entry. | Not established by the packet. |
| Independent record tying PI-KA to a known role at the relevant written stage | Would select or constrain the responsibility model if the binding were exclusive. | Could instead support a different participant. | Could separate continued reference from abandonment. | The needed discriminating observation is missing. |

This is a compatibility result, not a claim of equal probabilities. No frequency model or prior distribution was estimated. No statistical null model, random split, seed, or significance test is applicable.

The strongest surviving alternative is **a partly erased earlier entry whose designation referred to a counterparty or account, followed by correction or reuse before stamping**. It preserves the reported physical chronology and does not require a different first-mark reading. A continued responsibility designation remains possible too. Neither explanation is independently selected.

### The valid local exclusion

If the editors' sequence is correct, and the erased inscription represents the relevant writing episode, then a history placing its first creation wholly after the surviving impressions conflicts with that sequence. This excludes a physical ordering under stated assumptions. It does not establish administrative cancellation, fulfillment, debt settlement, reassignment, or an expressed verb such as “receive” or “seal.”

Nor does erasure automatically prove that the old referent ceased to matter. A correction can preserve a referent, and an incomplete wipe can accompany abandonment. The acquired record does not decide between those histories. The detailed source comparison, sensitivity branches and formal countermodels are in `analysis/RIVAL_COMPARISON.md`.

- **Object/event:** one carrier has several impressions. There is no independently established total count of relevant historical transactions or operators.
- **Inference:** physical traces can be described without translating PI-KA, but a participant assignment would require an additional premise not supplied by those traces.

The challenger preserved an initial text-based assessment before receiving the lead's interpretation. The later image inspection occurred after briefing and is explicitly nonblind. Independently performed checks covered the decisive source pages, actual figure pixels, alternative sign identification, the role-permutation argument, and equality of the raw index row with the overlay's retained raw value. The frozen snapshot hash and reviewed overlay hash matched.

The verdict is **survives as source control and a conditional physical constraint; semantics unresolved**. The lead accepts that narrowing because the required referent link is absent, not because the team agreed. There is no unresolved substantive disagreement. The full review and adjudication are preserved; integrity checks verify preservation and representation, not linguistic truth.
