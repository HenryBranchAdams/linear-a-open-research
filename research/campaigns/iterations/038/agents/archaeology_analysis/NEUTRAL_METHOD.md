# Exact writing–object–event connection: neutral method

This is a qualitative admission and reasoning aid, not a new ancient observation or semantic experiment.

## Independent linkage

Fix the object/set identity through inventory, join, attachment morphology, impression geometry, sample log, or a comparably independent physical link. Do not link records using the same designation whose meaning or identity is at issue.

Reconstruct manufacture, writing, smoothing/erasure, sealing, attachment/removal, movement, use/storage, and deposition in separate rows. For each proposed order, distinguish physical overlap from a published reconstruction and from a general craft-practice assumption.

## Lifecycle ledger

| Observation | Event fixed | Event inferred | Proposed textual meaning | Surviving relabelings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exact trace/source locator | Only the relation supported by that trace | All extra chronology or motive | Referent/category/role/action claimed | Recipient, operator, institution, account, or category alternatives still possible |

A pre-firing text can designate an intended recipient already known at manufacture. A later stamp need not authenticate all earlier writing. The same device need not imply the same operator. A physical attachment need not identify the linguistic referent as the attached item. Any use of these relations needs its own connecting premise.

## Comparison strategy

1. State a local claim before a broad lexical interpretation.
2. State serious competing assignments as relations to the exact event.
3. Substitute recipient for responsible party, or institution for individual. Ask whether any observed trace changes its predicted likelihood or compatibility.
4. Identify the physical/archival observation that prohibits one assignment without assuming the proposed translation.
5. If all traces remain compatible, preserve the equivalence and name the missing differentiating edge.
6. If a narrow exclusion survives, keep it without extending it to motive, role, or action.

Writing episode, inscription referent, seal device, seal operator, responsible institution, and transaction participant are distinct nodes. Evidence connecting two of them does not automatically connect the others.

## Independence and exposure

Two publications of the same photograph are one observation source. Two faces of a roundel are one object and may belong to one operation. New agent agreement is replication of analysis rather than another witness. Unknown project exposure is not unexposed; discovered observations cannot be retroactively preregistered.
