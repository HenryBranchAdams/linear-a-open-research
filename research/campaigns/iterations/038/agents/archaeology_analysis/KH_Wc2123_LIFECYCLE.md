## Conditional conclusion

If original imagery supports the published marking order, a narrow physical exclusion is justified: a reconstruction placing the **first creation of all surviving inscribed strokes wholly after the present impressions**, with no earlier inscribed phase, would be incompatible with the recorded erasure sequence. That finding does not establish cancellation, reuse, actual goods, a referent category, a participant role, or a textual action.

The most useful next observation for the physical issue is a high-resolution source-controlled image or 3D trace demonstrating the contact/overlap that fixes erasure relative to stamping. The most useful semantic observation would be a separate object/event link independently identifying the entity denoted by PI-KA or the first mark. No such link is established in the screened text.
