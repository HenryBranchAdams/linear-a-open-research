## Source observations (paraphrase)

The 2007 description identifies a complete roundel recovered from Geometric levelling fill. It reports three faint obverse signs, smoothing that partly closed incisions, and attempted erasure preceding rim impressions. AB39–77 is read as PI-KA; the first sign is proposed as A373 and tentatively bird-like. Nine impressions are assigned to one device; nine paired fingerprints and the impressions' alignment are attributed to repositioning during stamping. Drying clay is the authors' explanation. Figure5's seal reconstruction combines impressions. [Source1, pp.13–15]

The committee deferred A373 because of erasure and apparent divergence between photograph and drawings. Expectation of an ideogram on roundels is mentioned as contextual support, not a resolved reading. [Source2, p.586]

Del Freo records his and Zurbach's alternative: the disputed shape may be a variant of the tripod logogram. This is a proposal, not an adopted determination. [Source3, p.100]

## My initial inference

The most stable candidate component is the edition's residual AB39–77, not the proposed identity of the first sign. The complete physical carrier does not make its erased writing complete or semantically active at the later sealing stage.

The article supports a reported physical chronology: inscription, partial smoothing/erasure, and sealing. Because I have not yet viewed figure5 or the object, this is a **source-supported chronology**, not an independently established microscopic sequence. Even if that chronology is confirmed, it does not identify why the writing was erased, what PI-KA denoted, or whether the later seal use belongs to the same administrative event as the text.

The attempted erasure is especially consequential for a proposed link between PI-KA and the later seal user. Their occurrence on the same carrier is insufficient to establish contemporaneous administrative reference. Reuse or alteration of an earlier entry is a concrete explanation compatible with the material description. Conversely, erasure does not prove unrelated reuse; correction or cancellation within one continuing operation is also possible.

## Strong alternatives and predictions

| Proposed explanation | What it needs beyond the present physical sequence | Present status |
|---|---|---|
| PI-KA identifies the person or office responsible for the later stamped operation | An independent link connecting the erased designation to that later operation and its responsible participant | Not supplied by co-location or one repeated device |
| PI-KA identifies an intended recipient or other counterparty in the earlier written entry | An independent directional or referential relation identifying that participant | Also not supplied; the same erasure and stamping sequence can occur |
| PI-KA classifies an item, account, or transaction in the earlier written entry | Independent referent or controlled functional contrast for this component | Remains compatible, including either first-sign reading branch |
| The erased inscription is an earlier record; later stamping uses the same carrier without preserving that record's referent | Evidence of continued or changed use beyond the erasure sequence | Materially plausible but not established as a separate event |

These explanations are not all mutually exclusive. The narrow semantic comparison is the claimed binding of PI-KA to a particular participant in the later stamped event. Neither contextual role vocabulary nor stamp count produces such a binding.

A different comparison is genuinely decidable **if the published chronology holds**: PI-KA first inscribed only after all surviving stamps versus PI-KA present before attempted erasure and stamping. That is a production-history exclusion, not a selected role or translated action.

## Consequential analytical check

There is one carrier. Its nine impressions are nine physical impressions, not nine independent written-name attestations, nine recipients, nine transfers, or nine independent semantic tests. The committee and mirror publications do not add ancient witnesses.

A simple role-permutation check is sufficient; no statistical computation is warranted. Hold the physical sequence, inscription form, and device fixed. Let the erased designation refer to the issuer/responsible office in one account and to the intended recipient in another. No observed relation described in this packet changes when those referents are reassigned. A category/account interpretation also leaves the material observations unchanged. The available observations therefore do not identify the role within that model. This is a local identifiability limitation, not a statement about the whole language.

The first-sign dispute further prevents treating bird-like appearance as an independently known commodity. Resolving bird versus tripod could improve a category hypothesis, but would not alone establish what PI-KA denotes, whose role it identifies, or an action expressed by the inscription.

## Initial verdict and next task

**Remains unresolved at semantic level.** The strongest surviving alternative to a responsibility reading is an earlier erased entry naming a counterparty or administrative category, with later sealing either completing or reusing the document. No observation yet selects between that and a responsibility designation.

**Potential narrow result survives provisionally:** a source-supported temporal constraint on the surviving inscription, conditional on the publication's marking-order judgment. A claim that a production sequence is a semantic anchor needs narrowing.

The next useful task is original figure5 and target-surface inspection to check the reading and basis of the erasure/stamping sequence. If that confirms the account but provides no independent referent binding, source checking is complete without a semantic discriminator. The highest-value additional semantic evidence would be an exact independently linked record or event identifying what the erased designation refers to and whether that reference persists into the stamped phase. Another stamp from the same device, without that link, would not decide it.
