## Main source result

The original edition treats all three written elements as an attempted erasure: `[[A 373 , AB 39-77 (PI-KA)]]`. It does **not** report an erased PI-KA followed by a retained or replacement A373. The photograph/drawing transcription in figure 5 likewise brackets the entire sequence. The chronological assertion that the attempted erasure preceded the impressions is the original editors' assessment. The available plate supports the presence of smoothed but still visible writing, seal impressions and extensive finger impressions, but I could not independently identify a diagnostic crosscut establishing that order.

The second essential source correction concerns the left element. A373 is the first editors' proposed new sign, not a securely settled bird or commodity identifier. The later committee discussion records a discrepancy between the photograph and drawings and postpones the issue; Del Freo reports a possible tripod-logogram variant. These are consequential reading branches. None establishes a role for PI-KA.

## Epigraphic and physical qualifications

The word “palimpsest” is the editors' subheading, but the acquired description supplies an attempted erased inscription without a documented replacement text. An inference that A373 was added after PI-KA was erased is not supported. Likewise, continued legibility does not prove that the old text remained administratively valid: a cancellation can leave legible traces. Conversely, an incomplete wipe does not by itself prove a full reassignment of the clay object.

Figure 5 distinguishes a face with inscriptions from the rim's seal impressions. The presence of these components on one object is secure at edition level. Their co-presence is not an independent link between the meaning of PI-KA and the later stamped operation. The smoothing chronology, even if fully verified, would fix an order of physical actions, not the action expressed linguistically by the inscription.

The drawing presents the left element as a bird; the photograph is less determinate. The source-confirmed alternative tripod reading warrants retaining `X / proposed A373 / possible tripod variant` rather than assigning a settled commodity. Neither the later report nor the committee paragraph states fresh target-specific autopsy or provides a new target image. Do not infer that no autopsy ever occurred; simply do not count one as established here. The committee describes independent autopsy for different signs in adjacent paragraphs, which must not be transferred to A373.

## Assessment for lead integration

Retain the local physical/source result: an exactly identified roundel bears a three-element inscription treated by its editors as attempted erasure, plus nine same-device impressions, with reported erase-before-impression chronology. The corrected reading and lifecycle prevents an unsupported retained/replacement-label contrast. It does not discriminate a responsible party from a recipient, counterparty or account.
