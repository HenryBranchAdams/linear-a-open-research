## Decision

B-D26 remains `parked_access_limited`. B-D24 is unchanged. This screen neither evaluates nor refutes the complete Phaistos argument, and it makes no new semantic claim. A newly encountered request-only page or DOI citation does not satisfy the reopening condition. Stop after the finite route screen; do not spend the remaining ceiling revisiting vendor/abstract pages.

The useful next evidence is a supplied legitimate complete article, pp. 117–131 including figures, or an exactly identified primary dossier that independently supplies stable writing plus a role-differentiating operation. Full access would clear an access barrier and trigger evaluation; it would not itself establish a participant role.

## Source-inspection checklist for any later admitted packet

1. Match inventory, inscription number, face, catalogue entry, original figure and museum image; do not merge seal design, carrier and object group.
2. Record visible ancient strokes separately from phonetic convention, restoration, divider, join and editorial normalization.
3. Trace decisive facts to full/partial/abstract-only access and preserve source lineage.
4. Determine the physical sequence actually fixed by clay, impressions, breakage, attachments or overcutting; distinguish that from transaction or linguistic action.
5. Specify which observation ties the writing to a relation rather than merely to a seal user, support or room.
6. Test person/institution, responsibility/counterparty and account/category substitutions without changing observed facts.
7. Record prior exposure before any unused outcome; no new download or reviewer creates a new witness.
