## Question and unit

Can the comparatively stable residual component PI-KA be independently bound to a particular administrative relationship on this exact roundel?

Unit: one carrier, with one published target inscription group and nine reported surviving impression placements attributed to one device. The relevant number of historical events, transactions, operators and referents is unknown. No frequency estimate or cross-object generalization is made.

The object linkage is the edition's explicit inventory/inscription cross-reference, independent of PI-KA. The connection between writing and stamps is co-presence plus a reported physical chronology. A connection between the **referent** of the writing and a particular participant in the stamped operation has not been independently established.

## Hypothesis definitions

Let W be the residual designation, E the operation represented by the surviving stamps if they represent an administrative event, A its responsible entity if such an entity is identifiable, and C a distinct counterparty where one exists. These symbols define comparison models; they do not identify ancient persons or assert that A and C were distinct on this object.

- **R:** W designated A and continued to refer to A during E.
- **C:** W designated C, distinct from A, and continued to refer to C during E.
- **K:** W designated an account/item/category rather than a participant; E could concern that category.
- **U:** W belonged to an earlier entry; the carrier was corrected or reused, and continuity between W and E is unknown or absent.

R and C have different reference assignments by construction. That prevents their compatibility being hidden by the fact that someone can both be responsible and receive something. The distinct-entity construction is a counterexample to unique identification, not proof that two entities actually existed. K and U are additional context-supported alternatives; U can combine with an earlier R, C or K assignment.

## Observable projection

The inspected packet observes representations of marks and impressions and supplies an editorial chronology. It supplies neither a known A/C identity nor a known linguistic binding W→A or W→C.

Take a possible history compatible with R and keep fixed the carrier, visible marks, attempted smoothing, and surviving impressions. Reassign W to a distinct counterparty in the corresponding C model. No inspected trace changes. An account label can likewise occupy the designation position without changing those physical observations. This establishes multiple compatible models. It does **not** estimate their probabilities, prove all explanations equally likely, or prove non-identifiability for every possible future source.

The U model is stronger than a simple role swap because the attempted erasure permits loss of continuity with the later use. It requires no new positive claim that a second transaction actually occurred. It demonstrates that continued reference is another unverified assumption.

## Physical ordering comparison

Let I be first creation of the erased inscription, S the attempted smoothing affecting it, and T the surviving stamping. The publication's reconstruction gives I < S < T. Under that reconstruction and a single relevant inscription episode, the alternative T < I is inconsistent. That is the valid local exclusion.

The campaign did not newly verify a decisive crosscut. Therefore it does not promote I < S < T from reported chronology to independently established microstratigraphy. If this chronology is weakened to only “marks and impressions co-occur,” the physical exclusion is lost, while the semantic non-identification remains. If direct examination later verifies the chronology, the exclusion strengthens, while the referent gap remains.

No logical implication connects I < S < T to any particular verb meaning, grammatical subject/object, settlement of an obligation, or abandonment of an account. Such implications would require additional historical or linguistic premises.

## Sensitivities actually compared

1. **First mark:** proposed bird-like A373; possible tripod-logogram variant; unidentified. For each branch, R/C/K/U remain compatible. A settled iconographic identification would still need an independent relation between that mark and PI-KA before selecting lexical or participant roles.
2. **PI-KA confidence:** retain the editors' residual AB39–77 reading. If that reading were weakened, semantic admission becomes less feasible; no competing role wins. No newly invented syllabic reading was searched.
3. **Timing:** accept the editorial chronology conditionally versus withhold independent physical confirmation. Only the narrow physical exclusion changes.
4. **Continuity:** continuing referent during correction versus abandoned or repurposed earlier entry. Both are compatible; the erasure is not a semantic cancellation operator by default.
5. **Identity:** person versus institution; seal owner versus operator; one versus several operators. No inspected observation selects these identities. Same device does not make them interchangeable.
6. **Dependence:** one carrier and its several impressions versus an unjustified nine-event interpretation. The latter would inflate evidence without fixing the referent gap and is excluded from the analysis.
7. **Normalization:** index one-token representation versus source-aware separated first mark plus syllabic pair. The source-aware view prevents a secure-three-syllable-word assumption. No global `*810/A373` alias is imposed.

Seven qualitative sensitivity dimensions were checked. They are not seven statistical hypothesis tests. There were no fitted parameters, inferential p-values, random seeds, held-out sets, language-model scores, or cross-site frequencies.

## Dependency and circularity audit

The following proposed shortcuts fail because they add the conclusion or an unverified bridge:

- “The operator stamped it, therefore PI-KA names the operator”: assumes the text–operator binding.
- “The stamp authenticates the responsible institution, therefore the inscription names it”: conflates authentication function with inscription reference.
- “The first mark is a product, therefore PI-KA is its recipient”: assumes both the mark's interpretation and the role assignment.
- “There are nine impressions, therefore PI-KA received nine units”: imports a quantity/event mapping and direction absent from the target record.
- “The entry was erased, therefore the obligation was settled”: imports the motive and lexical action from a physical change.
- “The index groups all three signs, therefore they form one word”: replaces ancient evidence with a digital normalization convention.

## What could separate the models

A useful observation must change a model prediction rather than merely add another compatible mark. Examples of qualifying evidence types are a securely linked counterpart with independently known roles, or a directly documented attachment/use relationship that fixes W's referent at the relevant written stage. The linkage must be independently established by inventory, join, attachment, or equivalent evidence; using W alone to select the counterpart would risk circularity.

Such evidence could support R over C, C over R, or K over both, depending on what is actually observed. An independent proof of reference continuity would specifically weaken U but would still leave R/C/K to compare. Better images alone address reading and physical order; they do not necessarily fix reference.

No such new counterpart or binding was recovered. The bounded packet stops here. This local result is not a conclusion that Linear A is insoluble.
