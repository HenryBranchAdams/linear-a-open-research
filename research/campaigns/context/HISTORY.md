## Evidence handling carried forward

- Distinguish an inscription, its faces, sequence chunks, drawings, transcriptions and source mirrors. Those are not independent archaeological witnesses.
- Preserve broken boundaries, uncertain sign components, restorations, join alternatives, unknown counts and uncertain hands in separate fields.
- Full seal catalogue identifiers are entity keys. Visual motifs and convenient aliases are not enough to merge seals. Historical tablet/jar aliases require explicit concordance.
- A same-seal pair is an independently matched document feature, not proof that its words name the seal owner. A human logogram establishes a category context, not a grammatical subject.
- Existing results are reusable constraints. Mark unchanged reruns as reproductions; mark added source coverage or newly discriminating evidence separately.
- Compare alternatives with identical evidential access. A hypothesis must not supply its own missing role, word boundary, total, sound law or identity bridge.
- Keep semantic fields null until evidence selects them. No historical report warrants a newly deciphered sentence, percentage deciphered, language-family assignment or publication priority.
