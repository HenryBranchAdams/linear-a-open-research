## Selection reasoning

C043 was initially preferred because a source-defined finite object set supplied an independent physical axis without starting from a desired reading. Its weak point—whether the selected carriers had usable writing—was something the accessible catalogues could resolve. The outcome genuinely changed admission: the comparison could not be made.

C044 then had higher decision value than the remaining screens. It connected a stable pictorial target, independently excavated physical objects and a consequential published interpretation. Both a positive bronze comparator and a real local ceramic rival were inspectable at useful levels. The original source chain changed the rival from a generic tripod objection to a specific feature comparison. Effort shifted accordingly to challenge.

Kalo Chorafi is the strongest rejected **technical** alternative because it distinguishes marking stages on exact objects. HT24 is the strongest rejected **linked-context access lead** because a precise original location page might improve the inherited grouping claim. Neither supplied the independent relationship needed for its proposed semantic interpretation within this phase. They remain concrete opportunities, not dismissed globally.

## Follow-up scope within C044

Three ceramic-source packets were used: the exact Puglisi2011 waster; the directly cited Duhoux ceramic discussion; the Puglisi2010 tripod tray. The latter differs in morphology and has unresolved inventory text. Duhoux2003 full text remains an access dependency. No quota-filling third campaign was opened.
