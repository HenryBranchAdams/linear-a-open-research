Modern casts and trials are not additional ancient objects. A new reviewer is an independent reconstruction, not unused archaeological evidence.

Complete new third-party publications/images remain working acquisitions and are not redistributed in the portable package, consistent with inherited project practice. Their exact public locators, hashes, access scopes and the project's authored observations are preserved. Historical working paths in agent ledgers identify acquisitions; they are not claims that those full bytes are embedded. No inaccessible article is represented as read.
