# Technical-use reconnaissance after C042

## Decision

Three bounded packets were screened. None currently supplies an independently identified local referent or participant role. **Kalo Chorafi is the strongest of these three rejected alternatives:** its original report distinguishes exact marked cups and inscription stages. It would need a controlled production/use grouping or counterpart to become a semantic test. The other two packets supply weaker bridges.

This is reconnaissance, not three new experiments. No preferred lead-candidate synthesis had been received when the initial criteria or this screening was written. The reviewer read the current state, guardrails, relevant baseline dispositions and exposure records. Absence of a phrase from those records is not proof of unexposed evidence. KN Zc6/7 and KKH Zb1 already occur in the frozen discovery corpus; no blind validation is claimed.

## Packet T1 — Kalo Chorafi marked cups and associated objects

**Initial class:** accessible original packet whose inspection could reveal a discriminator.

**Target:** AB67 on AMR Π36184 (X4/A74/2014), compared with AB37 on AMR Π36189 (X13/A17/2018); source Tzigounaki–Karnava2020, pp319–328, especially321–326. KKH Zb1 is the inherited one-sign KI index entry; its museum identity should be source-crosswalked explicitly before adding an alias.

The original report identifies two conical cups: Π36184 has AB67 incised after firing under its base; Π36189 has AB37 incised before firing under its base, with a questionable additional stroke. They come from different spaces. The authors propose a collapsed upper-floor grouping for objects2–6, not an individually identified operation. Stamped vessel fragments, a seal and the CM061 loomweight are distinct carriers. The paper itself preserves possible phonetic use of the cup-shaped AB67. Its Keos and Melos parallels supply no independent role. [T1]

**Comparison and outcome:** A single universal prefiring potter-mark history for both targets fails if the reported stages are accepted. That does not distinguish producer identity, later owner/user mark, category or initial abbreviation. The inspected source supplies no target contents, workshop assignment or operating set. AB67's depiction and its cup carrier are compatible with a vessel reference; syllabic/abbreviatory identifying use remains a grounded rival. No denomination or named participant is inferred.

**Inspection and stop:** Author-uploaded full article text/captions inspected through ResearchGate and Academia; target figure pages located, but direct figure links failed (404 from malformed relative link; corrected public links403). No actual target figure pixels inspected. Further mirror searching would not remove the absent relationship. Park unless an exact manufacturing/use series or counterpart is identified; images could separately improve reading/stage control.

## Packet T2 — inked Knossos cups KN Zc6/7

**Initial class:** accessible interpretive packet whose contextual argument could reveal a useful use relationship.

**Target:** the local construction on KN Zc6/HM P2630 and KN Zc7/HM P2629, with interior placement and reader orientation considered as an independent handling variable rather than a translation.

Flouda2013 pp160–162 describes spiral interior writing oriented for someone holding a cup. She explicitly states that the proposed foundation-deposit/incantation explanation cannot be confirmed; dedication is a proposed alternative. Palaima1988 p280 discusses uncertainty in the cups' dating and the material/practice dependence of their script forms. These observations concern display and handling, not identified participants in the text. [T2a–b]

**Comparison and outcome:** Reading during holding/drinking, dedication, prior commission and other personal use can coexist. Neither the find context nor text orientation identifies who a particular sequence denotes. The packet is not suitable for a constructional action/role test without an independent counterpart. Do not label either a secure foundation deposit or a decoded prayer.

**Inspection and stop:** Flouda's target argument read in the authorized open-book transcription and author-upload excerpt; original Ubiquity chapter/figure route failed403, target figure pixels not inspected. Palaima's complete primary scholarly PDF downloaded; p280 text inspected (not an autopsy report). Evans1921 pp613–616 located; Heidelberg route returned an access-denial page, so those original pages were not inspected. Stop at source screen, rather than upgrading a contextual interpretation through repetition.

## Packet T3 — marked copper ingots and analytical origin

**Initial class:** possible exact analytical source packet; assessed for a mark-to-production contrast.

**Target:** a stable Linear A mark on an individually sampled Cretan oxhide ingot. No exact mark/sample candidate passed admission.

Powell, Barjamovic and Pulak2024 §2.2 p4 explicitly report incompatible numbering between the Gale–Stos-Gale1986 and Mangou–Ioannou2000 analytical datasets, preventing direct sample matching. Their §3.1/3 p5 distinguishes ore origin from where copper was cast into ingots. Their analytical provenance argument is therefore not itself a marking-event origin. Gale–Stos-Gale1986 n27 also cautions that comparisons with Aegean scripts do not assign the marks uniquely to one script. [T3a–b]

**Comparison and outcome:** An ore-source correlation might reflect supply rather than a writer/producer. Later casting, remelting, transport and marking stages remain separate. A secure mark/sample/object crosswalk plus stage evidence is needed before a controlled association can be computed. No material provenance inferred from a sign; no universal rejection of textual ingot marks.

**Inspection and stop:** 2024 author-uploaded article text, target analytical comparison and conclusions read. 1986 publisher abstract and published notes inspected; full original1986 tables and Bass1967 underlying marks argument not acquired. This is a bounded feasibility screen, not an executed geochemical test. No fresh numerical model is warranted from unmatched series.
