Written before exposure to a preferred candidate for this continuation. The inherited project summary includes C042 and its reconnaissance; that is prior exposure, not independent evidence. This reviewer will separately log later candidate exposure.

## Admission

1. Identify the exact written target, object/inventory and source. Keep damaged strokes, segmentation, scope, manufacture and inscription stages separate.
2. Require an independently established counterpart relevant to the proposed relationship: actual attachment or closure, an exact object/use or contents link, a physically linked record, a documented controlled production/use group, or another diagnostic counterpart. General object type alone can change a prior but does not bind the inscription's referent.
3. A packet whose unread pages could reveal such a counterpart is eligible for inspection. A hoped-for unpublished link with no attainable route remains a dependency.

## Comparison

4. State the strongest context-grounded alternative before interpreting the outcome. Ground a production rival in actual technical evidence where available; do not require every logical story to be impossible.
5. Separate maker, inscriber, seal operator, institution, owner, recipient, user and written referent. These can coexist. Ask what observed contrast would distinguish them rather than treating them as mutually exclusive labels by default.
6. Define what each serious interpretation predicts for the independent observation. If both permit the observation freely, that observation is not a discriminator. A finite local relation may nevertheless be supported even if historical names or full grammatical roles remain unknown.
7. A local physical constraint, categorical distinction, or exclusion under explicit premises is admissible progress. It must not be inflated into translation, grammatical action, or full mission completion.

## Reading and selection risks

8. Assess damaging reading and grouping alternatives, shared source dependence, restoration, selective preservation, reuse, residual deposition and post-depositional movement. Do not double-count later citations of one physical observation as new ancient evidence.
9. Declare the selection rule and its exposure history. Exact joins and finite sample calculations may be useful; retrospective partitions and an unexposed reviewer do not create unused ancient validation data.
10. Freeze any rule before genuinely unused observations are evaluated. Where no unused observations exist, characterize the result as a retrospective scoped comparison and state what external observation would most change it.

## Review products and stopping

11. Reconstruct the decisive source chain, inspect the strongest rival and the minimum decisive source pages/figures, and reproduce calculations that actually matter. Distinguish source text, published physical examination, viewed pixels and new measurement.
12. Recommend closure when the finite inspected packet cannot change the comparison or concrete acquisition routes fail. This is branch closure, not evidence that a relationship never existed.
13. Recommend a further bounded investigation when a materially different attainable observation could change the comparison. Do not turn the phase into repeated metadata searches.
14. Record disagreements and residual risk directly. Independent reasoning is not independent ancient evidence.
