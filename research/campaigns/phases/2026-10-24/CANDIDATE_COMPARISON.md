## Selection and reassessment

P01 had the highest immediate decision value because a primary physical observation could change a finite model without translating words. P02 had a closer material connection to a possible referent but weaker reading and segmentation. P06 had more secure recurrence but no observed shared event. No invented numerical score or minimum sample rule was used.

After C042's scope narrowed, the team did not default to stopping. P02 received the visual/variant follow-up, P11 the first-edition figure inspection, P05 a selective prior-exposure check, and P07 a concrete original-source access attempt. None supplied a new admissible semantic comparison. The challenger separately reviewed this stopping decision.

The newly found official host for *The Wor(l)ds of Linear A* led to one useful P02 comparison and a prior-equivalent roundel check. The2025 imprint and2026 release are kept separate; the already exposed chapter was not treated as new evidence. Day 2011's saffron analogy was a peripheral lead, not admitted as another packet: no independently diagnosed contents or category relationship was identified to take it beyond analogy.

The phase ends within its declared bounds. This does not assert that every original publication has been read or every possible source route exhausted. Exact reopeners are retained in the current queue and source records.
