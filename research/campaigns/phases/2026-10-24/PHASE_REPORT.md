# Linear A after 041: a corrected numeral and a bounded physical result

**Phase opened 24 October and completed 25 October 2026 (UTC). Research through C042; documentation D2026-10-25.**

## What we can now say

Two inscribed vessels do not support the interpretation that their written numbers both give each vessel's full capacity in one common volumetric unit. The small Knossos vessel, **KN Zb 27 / HM 5194**, has a reported capacity of about **34 litres** and a preferred numeral of **107**. The large Epano Zakros vessel, **ZA Zb 3**, has a published geometric estimate of **556 litres ±11%** and the principal edition reading **32**. If both numbers specified their own full containers in one unit, the smaller vessel would have to contain more than three times as much as the larger one. The inspected physical evidence contradicts that model. [C042 report](../../iterations/042/REPORT.md)

The result is conditional and useful: it separates **full capacity**, **actual partial contents**, **unit choice** and **accounting scope**, which cannot be collapsed into one interpretation. It does not identify a consignment, offering, sender, recipient or meaning of the accompanying words. A small or local unit, different filling fractions, and a quantity extending beyond the carrier remain viable. The strongest published rival to a collective label is an implicit small denomination; its possibility must be tested with independent metrological evidence.

A source correction also changes the working project. The frozen index retains **17** for KN Zb 27. Olivier's 1992 re-examination gave **117**; Del Freo's 2017 report of an examination with Zurbach on **22 June 2012** gives **107**. The source-based preference is now 107, with explicit historical branches. Original index bytes remain intact. The original Brice publication was not inspected, and later accounts disagree about its numeral; no unverified history is silently supplied. [Critical overlay](../../iterations/042/evidence/READING_AND_OBJECT_OVERLAY.json)

This is **reading/source control plus a structural/physical constraint**. It is not semantic discrimination, linguistic replication or completion of the action-and-participant mission. The qualitative size/number anomaly was already noticed by Olivier in 1992. This phase does not claim priority for it; the contribution is the project's recovered reading history and a reproducible, explicitly bounded comparison that does not assume a Linear B liquid unit. ## The observation that changes the comparison

For a common positive volume unit u, a full-container reading requires V=q×u. Thus the ratio between the two capacities must equal the ratio between the numerals. No absolute unit is needed to test this.

| Model consequence | KN Zb 27 | ZA Zb 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Preferred source numeral |107|32|
| Published capacity input |about 34 L|556 L|
| Unit implied by a full-capacity interpretation |about 0.318 L|17.375 L|

These implied scales differ by about 54.7. Even using the lower end of Stronk's allowance for ZA,494.84 L, a107-unit KN carrier would need to hold about 1,655 L. The historical17/32 combination, the least discrepant branch tested, still requires about 262.88 L. All nine source-grounded combinations of KN 107/117/17 and ZA 32/22/21 retain the mismatch. They are sensitivity branches, not nine independent observations or equally preferred readings. [Analysis](../../iterations/042/analysis/C042_ANALYSIS.md); [reproducible results](../../iterations/042/analysis/capacity_results.json)

The actual profile of HM 5194, independently inspected by the challenger and lead, offers a deliberately loose check on the unreported34 L method. Assuming the profile preserves shape, the vessel is approximately axisymmetric and its outer diameter is no greater than the published53 cm height, the entire vessel fits in a cylinder holding about 116.93 L. That upper envelope is below even the weakest historical requirement. This is a **post hoc conditional geometric bound**, not a new measurement, a confidence interval or unused validation.

### Source qualifications that matter

Christakis's about 34 L is an approximate report. The inspected text gives no target-specific measurement method, fill level or uncertainty. His Fig. 6.2 identifies the exact vessel as HM 5194; associated vessels HM 5192, HM 5193 and SMP 1568 are separate objects, and their common shipment is an interpretation.

Stronk's556 L is calculated from an average internal radius and maximum liquid height. The original 1972 pages and the 1982–83 follow-up were actually inspected as images. The estimate is not a performed liquid-fill measurement, and the stated±11% is not a statistical confidence interval. The later note reuses the same capacity; it is not replication.

Best's originalpp. 9–10 give a concrete argument for21 rather than32: damage might have been counted as a third ten, and a divider as a second unit. The photograph and reproduced drawing locate that dispute but did not let the team settle it. Current32 is supported by the edition tradition and Olivier's original comparison; the definitive GORILA plate was not acquired in this phase. The analysis retains all consequential historical alternatives.

Notti's2025 article announces new ZA measurement work. One sample page,195, was actually inspected; the complete measurement section remained unavailable. Its abstract's12–12.5 L-per-unit suggestion was not multiplied into a supposed observed capacity or substituted for the earlier estimate. The full article remains a precise new access dependency. ## The strongest alternative and what would distinguish it

De Fidio's2024 discussion explicitly contrasts a collective label with an implicit small unit around 0.30 L for KN. That numerical value is fitted to the vessel and does not independently prove a denomination; the interpretation nevertheless has direct scholarly grounding. A shared conventional commodity sign does not demonstrate the same unit size across place, date and administrative convention.

Variable partial filling also survives even if a common unit is imposed. Choose u≤min(V_KN/107,V_ZA/32). For example, using34/107L makes KN full and gives ZA about 10.17 L, roughly 1.83% of its nominal capacity. This is a constructed feasible model, not a claim about ancient contents. A minimum fill convention or independently applicable unit lower bound would be needed to exclude it. Equal filling fractions do not repair the ratio mismatch, but unequal fractions can.

A collective quantity, cumulative total or allocation across multiple containers can also fit. The same physical result cannot choose among those accounts or determine direction. An intended single filling still obeys its carrier's capacity; calling an amount intended does not remove the bound.

The observation most likely to change the semantic decision is an **independently established denomination equation or an exact container/account counterpart**. It must fix the unit or the quantity's scope independently of dividing these same capacities by these same numbers. A documented filling convention or exact-object use record could also help. A more accurate capacity estimate would strengthen physical control, but by itself would not distinguish a local unit from a collective account.

### Why L46 did not become a second deep campaign

L46 was the strongest rejected alternative because a loomweight independently belongs to textile production. Its original editor proposes AB 164a–AB 53–AB 01, but italicizes every sign as doubtful; the shallow first mark, direction and segmentation remain unsettled. The team inspected the actual original figure, four documented AB 164 variants and the recent textile chapter. A partial family resemblance remains; the comparison neither refutes nor stabilizes the reading.

The textile-tool context gives **modest conditional support**, not zero support, to a textile association if the first graph is AB 164 and functions separately. But published AB 164 use as a syllabogram within sign groups provides a serious identifying-sequence alternative. The context also helped motivate the original sign proposal, so it cannot simply be counted again as independent confirmation. No exact cloth, tool set, producer or recipient was joined to the mark. The next useful observation is raking-light/RTI or autopsy of AIN 11057, followed by a catalogue-linked operational set if one exists. ## Challenge, exposure and phase stopping

The challenger saved initial criteria before the favored synthesis, then reconstructed the primary source chain and the mathematical result. Later exposure to the team's conclusion is explicit. The final review accepts the narrow exclusion and rejects uniquely collective or participant interpretations. It also required the priority correction and the distinction between an intended single filling and a wider intended quantity. Both were incorporated. [Adjudication](../../iterations/042/ADJUDICATION.md)

No genuinely unused validation evidence was available. The analytical rule was fixed before some source values reached the comparative analyst, but the objects were already selected and exposed. Several sources describe the same carrier. An independent reviewer is another reasoning check, not another ancient event.

The phase did not stop at the first unsuccessful candidate. The L46 image/shape comparison, ivory-handle first-edition figures, prior-roundel check and final Phaistos access attempt were completed after the first deep selection. The independent [phase-stop review](PHASE_STOP_REVIEW.md) found no omitted obtainable discriminator in the inspected packets. It explicitly preserves PH Wg 45 as uninspected rather than refuted. No C043 was warranted or created. This is a bounded stopping decision, not a claim that all potentially useful Linear A sources are exhausted.
