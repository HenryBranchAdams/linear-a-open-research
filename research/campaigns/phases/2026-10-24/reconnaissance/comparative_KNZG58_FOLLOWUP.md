# KN Zg58: finite source follow-up after comparative reconnaissance

2026-10-24. No deep campaign admitted. This updates CP-04 in `RECONNAISSANCE.md`.

**Exact target.** The six fraction signs on faceδ of the Anetaki ivory handle, KN Zg58. Question: can their observable arrangement distinguish published fraction-value orderings, with reading direction and order principle established independently?

**Packet actually acquired.** Kanta, Nakassis, Palaima and Perna, *An archaeological and epigraphical overview of some inscriptions found in the Cult Center of the city of Knossos (Anetaki plot)*, Ariadne Supplement5, printed2024, online2025,27–43. https://ejournals.lib.uoc.gr/Ariadne/article/download/1841/1751/3474 . All17 PDF pages acquired; text pp32–42 inspected; actual pixels of fig6/p33 and fig7/p35 inspected. Local PDF/text/PNG files are under `sources/anetaki_*`.

**Observation.** The article describes six consecutive fractions but does not transcribe them. Its two overview figures do not provide a legible ordered six-sign target. The promised detailed edition is A.Kanta(ed.), *Anetaki II: the Neopalatial Room1 and the Ivory Repository*. The first report treats ring and handle separately and does not establish a quantitative calibration. [Article pp34,40–43; figs6–7]

**Access result.** One exact-title/fraction-target lookup found the same first report and later references to the future edition; no obtainable complete-edition route. This is a bounded failed access check, not evidence that no edition exists. A specialist corpus survey in the same volume provides an additional catalogue route, but is not yet inspected here and is not a claim that the missing six-sign sequence is accessible.

**Potential discriminator.** A legible faceδ photograph plus an exact edition could identify the sequence, its damage and spacing. A value-order claim would still require a justified ordering rule: a list can be arranged by magnitude, operation, commodity, pedagogy, or another convention. An ordered sequence selected after seeing desired values is not independent metrology. Nor does physical association with the ring make the inscriptions parallel versions.

**Strongest rival.** The sequence is a structured notation list whose order need not monotonically rank magnitudes. That can coexist with the authors' accounting classification. A full edition might discriminate this rival through grouping, entry boundaries, orientation or an identifiable procedural relation; the current overview cannot.

**Decision.** Not presently viable as the phase's second deep candidate. Move from category2 (accessible packet might reveal a discriminator) to category3 (specific missing dependency without an identified acquisition route). The exact next action is to obtain the KN Zg58 faceδ edition/photographs from *Anetaki II* or an author-supplied copy, then freeze the competing order rules before comparing fractions. Do not fill the gap with speculative readings from an overview photograph. A precise evidence request may ask for faceδ images at readable resolution, ordered transliteration with damage marks, direction justification, and the authors' reason for treating order as magnitude. Sending such a request is separately unauthorized.

**Novelty/exposure.** The phase already inherited sparse Anetaki coverage. This follow-up adds image-level source inspection and a precisely specified missing packet; it establishes no fraction values or semantic relationship. No new archaeological discovery or unused validation is claimed.
