# Public research projection through C044

Research authority: B2026-10-18 plus C038–C044. Documentation: D2026-10-26. Public synchronization: 27 October 2026, America/Chicago.

This directory preserves the original bytes of the published project-authored Markdown, structured evidence, registries, analysis scripts and review records from the complete current research snapshot. PUBLISHED_FILES.json records their hashes and the intentionally omitted paths. No research campaign was initiated by this publication.

The complete snapshot also contains nested historical ZIPs, private file inventories and an interrupted-transfer artifact. These are excluded here, as are complete third-party publications and plates. The source snapshot and its own MANIFEST.json/verify.py describe that larger research package; they are preserved for provenance and require the complete snapshot to run. They are not the manifest or verifier of this public projection.

From the site checkout, run `python3 scripts/verify-public-research.py` to check this projection's membership and hashes and independently rerun the C043 eligibility and C044 literal-index computations. The check also reruns the C042 capacity analysis in a temporary copy and compares its recorded JSON outputs. It verifies reproducible outputs, not ancient meanings, material identifications, assays or source autopsies.

Start with context/CURRENT_STATE.md, NEXT.md, iterations/044/REPORT.md and reviews/2026-10-26/REVIEW_THROUGH_044.md. Earlier source metadata and statements that no publication occurred describe the research phases when written; this synchronization does not rewrite those historical records.
