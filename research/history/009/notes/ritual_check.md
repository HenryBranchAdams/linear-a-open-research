# Ritual predicate follow-up

The new AURA 15 chapter by Brent Davis, *Recent Linguistics-Based Advances in the Study of Linear A*, pp. 43–59 (volume imprint 2025, online 2026), was read from the [official full volume](https://epub.lib.uoa.gr/index.php/aura/article/download/2642/pdf_73/5535). Its pp. 46–47 already print the exact A-KU-MI-NA / A-DU-KU-MI-NA / KU-MI-NA-QE comparison and TA-NA-TE / A-TA-NA-TE. These comparisons cannot be claimed as discoveries of this project. The chapter also describes Valério's retreat from interpreting DU-PU2-RE as “lord”; the place/shrine interpretation remains proposed. Reading related-looking expressions is not independent identification of their roles.

The strongest predicate argument revisited was the occurrence of A-TA?-I-*301-DE-KA on ZA Zb 3.2 in a wine-storage context as well as the I-*301 family on ritual objects. The [Younger transcription](https://github.com/mwenge/lineara.xyz/blob/master/commentary/ZAZb3.html), after GORILA IV 112–113, preserves:

```
.1 VINa 32 DI-DI-KA-SE • A-SA-MU-NE • A-SE
.2 A-TA?-I-*301-DE-KA • A-RE-PI-RE-NA • TI-TI-KU
```

The question mark here renders the underline on TA in that edition. The divided words in this two-line inscription do not independently identify either a supplier or a recipient. They are not replaced by English grammatical roles in the dataset.

An explicit counter-source to treating the jar as independently *non-ritual* is Ilse Schoep and Jan Driessen, *An Inscribed Handle from Palaikastro (PK Zb 25)*, Minos 37–38 (2002–2003), pp. 77–80, [author PDF](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jan-Driessen-2/publication/285902837_An_Inscribed_Handle_at_Palaikastro/links/5664309408ae192bbf907d86/An-Inscribed-Handle-at-Palaikastro.pdf), p. 79. They explicitly consider a possible ritual event involving a batch of pithoi. This does not prove ritual function; it shows that storeroom provenance and a wine quantity do not independently exclude it. Their volumetric account also differs from Younger's profile-based estimate. This run did not perform a new physical capacity measurement or adjudicate those estimates, and does not translate I-*301 as “holds”.

The original SY Za 2 OLIV/RE dispute remains consequential. Rose Thomas, *Some reflections on morphology in the language of the Linear A libation formula*, [author manuscript](https://www.finnishsyntax.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2022/01/Reflections_on_Morphology_in_the_Language_of_the_Linear_A_Libation_Formula.pdf), PDF pp. 3–4 and note 7, explicitly records the competing RE reading that would extend JA-SU-MA-TU to JA-SU-MA-TU-RE. Reading OLIV because the predicted syntax needs an object would be circular. No new SY Za 2 palaeographic claim is made in this run.

**Decision:** the administrative/ritual crossover supports a broad domain comparison, but does not choose “gives” over “receives”, “stores”, or a ritual action. No English sentence was inferred from it. The research moved to the incised-numeral roundels and the Anetaki/textile evidence, where additional physical and numerical constraints could actually be tested.
