# Linear A investigation — iteration 9

Research snapshot: 7 September 2026. **The sentence goal remains unmet.** No Linear A action has been independently identified, and no evidence yet identifies `TA-NA-TE`, `TA-NA-TI`, and `A-TA-NA-TE` as forms of the same referent. This iteration reproduced round 8, retrieved the three priority source groups, expanded the corpus search, and executed five research cycles, including pivots to roundels and textile accounts.

The strongest new analytical result is a precise, conditional test on **HT 24**: accepting a doubtful `ME` beside 6 changes whether a doubled-unit interpretation exceeds a proposed total of 45. Allowing processing loss can remove that contradiction. This establishes what the numerical evidence can distinguish; it does not translate `ME`. The strongest advance on the starting target is a directly checked **Linear B person-designation context**, with its role and limits separated from the Linear A comparison.

Full transcriptions, access details, alternative readings, and source dependencies are in the linked branch notes and machine ledgers. Ancient strings below are edited transcriptions, not claims about pronunciation or new readings.

## 1. Identity before grammar: the starting family remains unconnected

The relevant complete edited forms remain `TA-NA-TE` and `A-TA-NA-TE` on **ZA 10a**, and `TA-NA-TI` on **HT 7a, HT 10b, and HT 98a**. On ZA 10a, the two forms occur as separate quantity-bearing entries, associated with 2 and 1. Their presence on the same tablet does not independently establish one entity under two roles. On HT 7a the human logogram is in the heading; it does not directly identify each label's role. [ZA 10 transcription](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwenge/lineara.xyz/master/commentary/ZA10.html), [HT 7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwenge/lineara.xyz/master/commentary/HT7.html), [HT 10](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwenge/lineara.xyz/master/commentary/HT10.html), [HT 98](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mwenge/lineara.xyz/master/commentary/HT98.html).

I tested an identity route that does not use the proposed endings: **do these account faces share other securely bounded, complete, multisign labels?** The audited dataset contains 66 selected rows from nine document identifiers. The strict comparison excludes the target family, single signs, composites, damaged boundaries, and doubtful readings. None of the three `TA-NA-TI` faces shares such a label with ZA 10a. Including damaged HT 49 does not create a match.

Aggregating whole tablets admits one match: **SA-MA**, on HT 10a and ZA 10b, the opposite faces from the respective targets. This is useful sensitivity information, not evidence that opposite faces are unrelated. Within Haghia Triada, HT 7a and HT 10b share **DA-RE**. Neither common account vocabulary nor an identical short name establishes an individual. As a control, **PA-JA-RE 1** occurs in both HT 88 and ZA 10b; even that closer spelling-plus-quantity match is insufficient for a unique person identification. [Audited rows, exclusions, and results](notes/identity.md).

The additional **HT 49a.2** witness remains left-broken. Younger has `]TA?-NA-TI`, whereas SigLA encodes the three surviving signs as sure readings. Both leave the beginning lost. It cannot become a fourth complete word by selecting the more confident encoding. The fractional quantities associated with `TA-NA-TI` on HT 10b and HT 98a also prevent a universal literal-person-headcount interpretation; a person-related account can still carry fractional commodity quantities.

The independent expansion retrieved **all 802 document-face pages in the live SigLA browse index**, yielding **1,401 indexed sequence occurrences** with **zero fetch errors**. Parsed sequence counts matched page metadata for all 802 pages. Sixteen sequences contain TA-NA; the family-relevant hits are precisely the three complete TI forms, broken HT 49, the two ZA 10 forms, and unresolved ZA 9. There is **no literal A-TA-NA-TI** in this snapshot. Most ritual texts and modern THE Zb 6 are outside its coverage. Sequence chunks can end at damage or a face boundary, so these totals are neither complete-word counts nor independent-object counts. [SigLA](https://sigla.phis.me/), [frozen sequence data and acquisition provenance](data/sigla_sequence_inventory.json).

**Result:** the searched contextual bridge was not found. This does not falsify shared identity: sparse accounts can omit associates, and related designations need not have identical distributions. Distinct names, regional/spelling variants, and grammatical variants all survive. Nothing here assigns “from,” “to,” agent, or recipient.

## 2. ZA 9: the original plate does not recover the missing prediction

I retrieved and inspected the publisher's **GORILA III pp.166–167**, including the photograph, drawing, and apparatus. The available full-size scans are 150 dpi. The main edition leaves the sign after `A-TA-NA-` unresolved; the apparatus offers **possible TE**. The nearby sheep composite and quantity are conventionally represented as `OVIS+SI 2?`. SigLA likewise records an unresolved final sign. [Original p.166](https://cefael.efa.gr/detail.php?site_id=1&actionID=page&serie_id=EtCret&volume_number=21&issue_number=3&sp=190&max_image_size=1), [p.167 apparatus](https://cefael.efa.gr/detail.php?site_id=1&actionID=page&serie_id=EtCret&volume_number=21&issue_number=3&sp=191&max_image_size=1).

Same-tablet secure TI and TE were used as controls. The target's surviving near-vertical trace lacks enough diagnostic structure to select either. Younger's tentative TE suggestion depends on this edition; it is not an additional vote from an independent object examination.

The prediction **A-TA-NA-TI** remains unconfirmed. A secure TI would weaken the simple site division between the audited TE and TI forms, but still would not establish lexical identity or a case meaning. The immediate discriminant is better surface evidence at this exact sign, not another restoration based on the expected pattern. [Full source and image audit](notes/za9.md).

## 3. The Linear B comparison now has a checked role context

The inherited `KN Dc 1618+7171+7174` reference is an alias of the witness currently catalogued by LiBER as **KN De 1618+7171+7174+fr.** It is one inscription, not two. The critical text and institutional photograph were inspected. Its lower line contains:

```text
.B  ta-na-to , / e-ko-so ,  o OVISm 16 ⟦2⟧
```

The upper line has a damaged expression, sheep figures 47 and 37, and an erasure. Its proposed collector-name restoration remains conjectural. `ta-na-to` itself is complete and undotted in the edition. [LiBER critical text and apparatus](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/3249).

The first position has independent support as a **person/flock-responsibility designation**. In the same-hand comparison **KN Db 1168**, `ma-di` precedes the same second label, `e-ko-so`; a separate personnel record **KN As 603.2** has `ma-di VIR 1`. Meanwhile **KN Dn 1092** uses `e-ko-so` as a flock-total grouping alongside `su-ki-ri-ta`. These are controls on name and place *categories*, not proof that every repeated name identifies the same individual. [Db 1168](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/3066), [As 603](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/2670), [Dn 1092](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/3473).

The series interpretation places the target in a different position from the place and secondary collector designations. “Flock-associated person” is safer than legal owner or literal herdsman: the responsibilities remain debated. It supplies no Linear A transaction direction. [Civitillo 2025, pp.129–131, 138–140](https://polygraphia.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/P-09-2025.pdf).

A further apparent match was checked: **KN Uf(1) ‹311›.2 `]ta-na-ti , DA 1̣[`**. Its left boundary is missing, the object is missing, and the edition descends through Evans's Handlist and *Scripta Minoa II*. This is not a securely complete second name or a case counterpart of `ta-na-to`. Exact and substring LiBER queries preserve this distinction. [Uf ‹311›](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/619).

A conditional orthographic test also blocks an easy grammatical shortcut. **If** `ta-na-to` is an ordinary Greek o-stem, its nominative and dative would both be written with final `-to`, because Linear B omits the second element of an i-diphthong. An ordinary `-to/-ti` nominative/dative pair is therefore not predicted by that model. Other stems require separate evidence. Published possible adaptations from Linear A final e, u, and i to Linear B o further prevent singling out the i/o comparison as an established exclusive correspondence. [Detailed orthographic sources, controls, and query results](notes/linear_b.md).

**Result:** the external comparison is better classified, but no bridge identifies a Linear A participant under two forms. Name resemblance cannot transfer an entire sheep-account role across scripts, sites, and periods.

## 4. The imported jar: complete text, qualified provenance

The original publication was retrieved: **Boulotis 2008, BAE 257, pp.69–71, fig.8**. The four signs on the jar now called **THE Zb 6** are **AB08–59–06–46**, conventionally **A-TA-NA-*46**. The text is complete as published. The original photograph and the discussion of the NA allograph support this transcription without supplying a value for *46. An older online THE Zb 6 numbering points to a different one-sign object, so the machine ledger preserves that identifier hazard. [Original publication](https://www.archetai.gr/images/pdfs/bae/BAE_257.pdf).

The excavation notices independently support incision **before firing**. They describe fragments and recovery deposits, not a labeled vessel intact in a demonstrable recipient's working location. Notices differ in their references to 1990 discovery and 1992 fragment recovery; these are retained rather than silently reconciled. [Ergon 1993, p.90](https://www.archetai.gr/images/pdfs/ergon/Publ_ERGON_1993.pdf), [PAE 1992, p.178](https://www.archetai.gr/images/pdfs/praktika/Publ_PRAKTIKA_1992.pdf), [PAE 1993, p.185](https://www.archetai.gr/images/pdfs/praktika/Publ_PRAKTIKA_1993.pdf).

“Cretan-made” is a published attribution, not a proven source locality in the evidence retrieved. Michailidou explicitly called for clay analysis; a later discussion still describes macroscopic attribution. No scientific provenance result was retrieved in this search, which is not proof that none exists. [Michailidou 2000–2001, p.9 n.22](https://gredos.usal.es/bitstream/10366/133892/1/Indice.pdf), [complete jar audit](notes/jar.md).

Pre-firing incision excludes creating these same incisions only after the finished vessel arrived. It leaves maker, owner, source, destination, and dedication labels possible. The physical journey therefore does not select **FROM** for *46 or **TO** for TE. Boulotis already discussed the `TA-NA-TE/A-TA-NA-TE` comparison and possible initial A in 2008: the resemblance is prior scholarship.

## 5. Pivots with independent physical or numerical anchors

### ZA Wc 2: a numeral is better supported than a sentence

A newer autopsy discussion supports Perna's reading of the final marks as **2**, rather than MI: the supposed diagonal is treated as a crack. The lexical string is still not securely divided. Analyses of **A-TI-KA(-)A-DU(-)KO 2** as one, two, or three expressions must remain separate hypotheses; DU also carries reading uncertainty. A Linear B **a-ti-ka** exists in **KN V(4) 831.4**, but cannot independently supply the Linear A word boundary, identify a shared person, turn A-DU into an action, or make KO a commodity. [Anastasiadou, *Names on Roundels?*, pp.35–42](https://epub.lib.uoa.gr/index.php/aura/article/view/2642), [KN V 831](https://liber.cnr.it/tablet/view/686).

The newer chapter itself mentions both two and four impressions for ZA Wc 2 in different passages. Perna supports two; the conflicting count remains recorded. I also tested a broader numerical shortcut: one common proportionality between face quantities and impressions. **GO Wc 1 (5/5)** and **HT Wc 3024 (6/6)** force one impression per face unit. **MA Wc ‹5›**, with `4+A704` and reported one or two impressions, cannot fit that common scale if A704 is an additive positive proper fraction. Unknown impressions, different units, or a nonadditive interpretation can evade the test. The familiar exceptions are published; the exact constraint formalization is this iteration's analysis. None of these counts selects goods entering versus leaving. [Readings, count alternatives, and executed test](notes/roundels.md).

### HT 24: a reading-sensitive constraint on a possible ME unit

The Anetaki publication provides a separate textile anchor: **KN Zg 57 C, AB54+77**, conventionally cloth+KA. The associated ring and handle are separate carriers with probably different hands; their publication does not supply a full parallel sign-group edition. The KA adjunct narrows the relevant material context without translating it as “purple” or identifying a participant. [Kanta et al., pp.34, 39–41](https://ejournals.lib.uoc.gr/Ariadne/article/view/1841).

This led to an executable test of **HT 24**, following Del Freo's published wool-account hypothesis. Preserved recto numeric lower bounds are **6 + 10 + 9½ + 6 = 31½**. **ME with 10[ is secure; ME beside one 6 is doubtful.** Under the proposed fraction values, the verso quantities total **1¾ + 1¾ + 1 = 4½**; a proposed 10:1 conversion yields **45**, also the number of associated noduli. Those connections and conversions are assumptions of the tested model, not independently translated grammar. [Del Freo 2020, pp.55–59](https://openportal.ispc.cnr.it/data/2020/421681/2020_421681.published.pdf).

Treat ME as a hypothetical multiplier and require a **lossless common total of 45**, with missing contributions nonnegative:

| ME reading scenario | Minimum if ME doubles a unit | Residual to 45 | Necessary upper bound on multiplier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Secure ME with 10 only | 41½ | 3½ | 47/20 |
| Also accept ME? with 6 | 47½ | −2½ | 59/32 |
| Restore ME to all preserved entries | 63 | −18 | 10/7 |

**The doubled-unit rejection changes with the doubtful sign.** A positive residual establishes numerical compatibility only; it is not an epigraphically valid restoration. The third row is an explicitly stronger restoration scenario. Lost numeral extensions remain possible and are not assigned automatically to the wholly lost entries.

For the first row to describe an actual doubled-unit account, the doubtful marks must not be additional ME multipliers, and unpreserved material must respect the remaining allowance. Merely omitting uncertain ME from a lower-bound calculation does not establish its ancient absence. The 45-unit accounting model comes from earlier scholarship developed by Del Freo from Palaima and Hallager; this iteration contributes the exact sensitivity calculations.

There is also a substantive rival to the lossless assumption. If 45 is output from at least 47½ input, then a yield of at most **18/19**, or a loss of at least **1/19 ≈ 5.263%** at that lower bound, removes the contradiction. Further missing input requires greater loss. **No ancient processing yield is observed here.** This alternative also relaxes a direct equation between 45 noduli and 45 standard input units. Del Freo's conclusion about a different ME unit is qualified, and he discusses processing loss in comparative material; this test does not accuse him of an absolute exclusion or of overlooking loss. [Del Freo, pp.58, 61–62](https://openportal.ispc.cnr.it/data/2020/421681/2020_421681.published.pdf).

The original HT 24 plate and apparatus were inspected. The scan does not resolve the damaged ME?, and SigLA's coarse certainty for the composite does not erase component uncertainty. **New result:** exact conditional multiplier and yield bounds. **Still unresolved:** ME as a unit, quality, processing category, or another modifier; account direction; identities and roles of the listed entities. [Original GORILA I p.42](https://cefael.efa.gr/detail.php?site_id=1&actionID=page&serie_id=EtCret&volume_number=21&issue_number=1&sp=78&max_image_size=1), [full test and assumptions](notes/anetaki.md).

## 6. What would discriminate the surviving interpretations?

The most promising constructions still have these dependencies:

| Candidate | Independent observation | Action support | Participant-role support | Shared unresolved premise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TA-NA family | Complete forms, account positions, quantities; external B person-slot control | None established | Person-related category is plausible for TI; source/recipient unassigned | Same designation or referent across A forms |
| THE Zb 6 label | Complete four-sign text and pre-firing incision | No written transport action identified | Vessel association; maker/source/destination remain open | Meaning inferred from physical history |
| ZA Wc 2 | Sealed object; published numeral reading | A-DU is only a proposed segmentation/function | A-TI-KA and KO require boundaries and category assignments | The name/action/commodity parsing itself |
| HT 24 | Numerical marks and textile-associated signs | Processing is a model of the account, not a translated verb | Listed labels not independently tied to supplier or receiver | Common batch, conversion and direction |

There is no warranted sign-to-English sentence mapping to present. The following tests would change specific inferences:

| Question | Evidence that would change the inference | What it would still leave open |
|---|---|---|
| ZA 9 final sign | Diagnostic original-surface evidence for TI, TE, or another sign | Shared referent and role meaning |
| Same TA-NA referent | Two complete forms linked by an independently distinctive person/account identity | Whether variation is grammatical or orthographic |
| *46 as source marking | Replicated labeling tied to independently established source/destination pairs | Exact pronunciation and sentence action |
| ME as a doubled unit | Resolve HT 24 ME? and independently constrain total, conversion, and processing yield | Participant roles and verbal meaning |
| ZA Wc 2 as a sentence | Independently secure boundaries plus replicated A-DU in directionally anchored records | Whether the action is overtly written or supplied by context |

These are discriminating tests, not predicted text to insert into damage. A further lookalike spelling or shared seal alone would not meet them.
