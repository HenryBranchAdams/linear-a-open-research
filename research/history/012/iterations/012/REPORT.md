# Iteration 12 Report

## Result

Iteration 12 produced a **substantive intermediate structural result**, not a complete decipherment.

The planned HT94 reading problem turned out not to be the critical dependency. A stronger witness exists:

- HT7b securely records **QI-TU-NE 1**.
- HT87 uses **QI-TU-NE** in the heading.
- HT117 uses **QI-TU-NE** as a subheading.
- Both HT87 and HT117 place **DI-KI-SE 1** beneath that QI-TU-NE classification.

The key heading/count contrast therefore survives with HT94 removed.

## Independent category anchor

Ruth Palmer's comparative study classifies HT7 among personnel-only tablets because AB100/VIR occurs on the tablet, and treats HT87 and HT117's W+N 1-list format as best paralleled by Linear B personnel records. Davis & Valério independently note several possible male anthroponym parallels among HT117 entries and one on HT87.

This does not prove every entry is a personal name. It does give the QI-TU-NE contexts a stronger external **personnel-domain** anchor than Iteration 11 had for SA-RU.

## What is now justified

The evidence favors treating QI-TU-NE as a **personnel-related nominal/classificatory designation**. It can:
1. be counted as one administrative/personnel item;
2. head a list of personnel-like entries;
3. repeatedly classify DI-KI-SE in two records.

This strongly weakens:
- a commodity-only interpretation,
- and an interpretation in which QI-TU-NE is only an overt action/predicate.

It does not choose among:
- personnel category/status,
- person/account/office,
- place/institution,
- or homographic/distinct lexical uses.

## HT94 disposition

HT94b's `*21F-TU-[NE] 1` remains conditional because NE is restored in Uchitel's presentation. It is compatible with the new result but unnecessary to it.

## SA-RU correction

The Iteration 11 SA-RU claim is formally amended. “Entity/account/designation” remains a useful working preference, but broader category and commodity-derived account readings were not eliminated.

## Mission test

No supplier/recipient direction, expected-versus-recorded accounting relation, or overt predicate is independently identified. Reversing transaction direction leaves the observations unchanged.

Therefore:

**Full mission: not achieved.**

**Intermediate advance:** replicated membership/classification relation under QI-TU-NE, with an external personnel-domain anchor.
