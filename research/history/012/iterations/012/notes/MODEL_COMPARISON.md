# Model comparison — QI-TU-NE

## Observed facts used

1. QI-TU-NE is counted as `1` on HT7b.
2. HT7 is independently personnel-related through AB100/VIR and format.
3. QI-TU-NE is a heading component on HT87.
4. QI-TU-NE is a subheading on HT117.
5. DI-KI-SE 1 occurs beneath QI-TU-NE on both HT87 and HT117.
6. HT87/117 1-lists contain entries with published possible Linear B male-name parallels.

## Models

### Pure action/predicate
Weakened. Direct quantification `QI-TU-NE 1` is awkward for a term that is *only* a predicate/action. A nominalized event or status remains possible, so this is not formal elimination.

### Commodity-only label
Strongly weakened. The counted occurrence sits on a personnel tablet and the heading occurrences classify personnel-like 1-lists. A commodity term would need additional polysemy/homography.

### Personnel category/status
Fits all secure observations naturally: a category/status can head a list of members and can itself be counted as one administrative item.

### Person/account/office designation
Also fits. An overseer, office, or account holder can head a list and be counted elsewhere.

### Place/institution
Also fits. A place or institution can classify personnel and occur as an administrative entry.

### Nonidentity / homography
Survives. Identical spelling at one site does not logically prove one lexeme. It is less economical because the same form repeatedly appears in tightly related personnel-list structures, but economy alone is not decisive evidence.

## Strongest structural conclusion

The most conservative statement is:

> QI-TU-NE is a **personnel-related nominal/classificatory designation** whose exact ontology remains unresolved.

The repeated QI-TU-NE → DI-KI-SE relation is stronger than a one-off positional analogy because it recurs on two tablets.

## Action/role test

Swap:
- supplier ↔ recipient,
- recipient ↔ responsible account,
- status ↔ category.

No written feature currently changes. Therefore transaction direction is not identified.

The only relation that survives independent structural replication is **classification/membership**.
