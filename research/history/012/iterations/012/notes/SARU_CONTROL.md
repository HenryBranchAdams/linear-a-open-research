# SA-RU control re-audit

Iteration 11 overstated the category exclusion.

## Without cross-document lexical identity
HT86/95, HT94 and HT123 provide separate local observations. They cannot jointly force one semantic class.

## Assuming same lexical item but not same referent
Cross-context behavior favors an administrative/referential label over a narrow direct-grain commodity term, but a commodity-derived account/category remains possible.

## Assuming same historical referent
Not supported. Nothing in the current evidence independently identifies one historical person/place/account across all SA-RU occurrences.

## Control lesson from Iteration 12
A `word + 1` pattern is not, by itself, enough to identify a personal name. HT7 becomes diagnostically useful only because an **independent human/personnel anchor** (AB100/VIR plus tablet format) is available. Future SA-RU work should demand comparable anchors.
