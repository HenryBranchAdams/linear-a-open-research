# Operation and role-symmetry test

## KI-RO / absence / shortfall hypotheses

The active families contain personnel lists under or near KI-RO, but no independently identified complete roster or calibrated expected value is available here.

A shortfall model `K = E - R` is diagnostic only if:
- E is independently known,
- R is independently known,
- K has the same unit and scope,
- and the equation was not chosen after observing K.

Those conditions are not met for the present personnel-family evidence.

## Direction reversal

For HT86/95/123-style commodity records:
- “entity supplied goods”
- “entity received goods”
- “entity is responsible for account”

remain compatible with the same label/commodity/quantity layout.

For HT87/117:
- QI-TU-NE classifies/heads entries,
but that relation does not identify an action such as absent, assigned, exempt, owed, or dispatched.

**Result:** bounded identifiability barrier. Stop translation generation until a new counterparty, expected roster, or operation anchor appears.
