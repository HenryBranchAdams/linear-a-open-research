# HT 117 / HT 87 structural control

HT 117 contains three 1-lists. Its third subheading is QI-TU-NE and includes DI-KI-SE 1.

HT 87 combines QI-TU-NE and MA-KA-RI-TE in its heading and also contains DI-KI-SE 1. It also contains KU-RU-KU 1, compared in published work with Linear B ku-ru-ka, a male personal name.

This makes QI-TU-NE and MA-KA-RI-TE structurally unlike the entry labels and makes DI-KI-SE a stable entry-slot designation. The semantic relation remains unknown.
