# Iteration 11 Report

## Mission result
**No complete action-and-role construction was established.**

Iteration 11 nevertheless produced a substantive category result.

## 1. SA-RU
The key independent observations are:

- **HT 94b:** SA-RU is one of five syllabic labels each followed by numeral 1 under KI-RO; the group is followed by KU-RO 5.
- **HT 123a:** SA-RU is a row-initial label followed by OLIV 16, a quantified *308 entry, and KI-RO with a fractional amount.
- **HT 86/95:** SA-RU recurs in the strong repeated grain-account roster.

These observations do not require translating SA-RU or KI-RO.

### Discriminating result
A grain-specific commodity interpretation fails because HT 123a explicitly writes OLIV after SA-RU and then another quantified category. A generic cross-commodity quality remains logically possible, but it is strained by SA-RU's counted-member slot on HT 94b.

The best evidence-level description is therefore:

> **SA-RU is an entity/account/designation label.**

The evidence still does not distinguish person, place, institution/group, or administrative account.

## 2. Why this advances HT 86/95
Iteration 10 could not tell what the recurring HT 86/95 labels represented. SA-RU now has an independent category constraint from outside that packet, so at least one roster member should no longer be treated as an unconstrained grain specification.

This strengthens the interpretation of HT 86/95 as a roster of administrative entities associated with quantities, without determining transaction direction.

## 3. HT 117 / HT 87
Published contextual work shows:
- HT 117: QI-TU-NE is a subheading; DI-KI-SE 1 is an entry.
- HT 87: QI-TU-NE + MA-KA-RI-TE form a heading; DI-KI-SE 1 recurs as an entry.
- KU-RU-KU 1 on HT 87 has a published Linear B male-name parallel.

This supports **heading/category vs entry** structure. It does not independently establish “missing,” “absent,” “assigned,” or another action/status.

## 4. Direction-reversal test
All of these remain compatible with the SA-RU records:
- SA-RU supplied the goods.
- SA-RU received the goods.
- SA-RU was responsible for the account.
- SA-RU is a place/institution whose account contains the quantities.

Therefore a sentence such as “SA-RU delivered 16 units of olives” is not established.

## 5. Result classification
**Substantive intermediate result.**

Established: category constraint on SA-RU; heading-vs-entry structure on HT 117/87.
Not established: historical identity, person vs place, transaction direction, overt predicate, or sentence.
