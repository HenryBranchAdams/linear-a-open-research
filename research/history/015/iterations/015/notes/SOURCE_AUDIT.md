# HT100 source-critical audit

## Question

Can the damaged quantity following `*304+PA` on HT100 (HM 1306) be independently adjudicated as **4** or **6**?

This reading was selected before the source audit because it has a pre-specified consequence for `KI 2`:

- 4 → all quantities including KI 2 total KU-RO 97.
- 6 → all quantities except KI 2 total KU-RO 97.

## Source hierarchy

### GORILA I — primary critical edition
The decisive source-critical fact is negative: the critical edition does **not** securely supply either numeral at this location.

Bennett's review reports GORILA as `58, ( ]` at the disputed slot rather than 4 or 6.

### Was
The older reading supplies 4, but Bennett explicitly says the relevant figures are damaged and restored.

### CTLA
CTLA supplies 6. Bennett juxtaposes it with the incompatible Was and GORILA readings and says the restorations/readings are not persuasive given the damaged state.

### Uchitel
Uchitel prints `*304+PA [4]`. The brackets matter: this is a restoration, not an observed secure numeral.

### Current GORILA-derived digital transcriptions
The current lineara.xyz / Younger-derived reading specification gives:

`VIR+KA 58  *304+PA  [no numeral]  TI+A 12  KI 2 ...`

The current AIgent structured corpus likewise preserves an unknown marker between `*304+PA` and `TI+A 12`.

These sources are not independent ancient witnesses, but they are useful evidence that modern digitization has not found grounds to promote either restoration.

## Adjudication

**Neither 4 nor 6 is epigraphically secure.**

The correct research transcription for the disputed amount is:

`*304+PA [quantity damaged/unreadable]`

The experiment therefore resolves the *status of the evidence*, not the ancient numeral.
