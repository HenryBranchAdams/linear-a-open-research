# Epigraphic adjudication

## Verdict

**4 vs 6 cannot be settled from the currently accessible published edition evidence.**

This is not a 50/50 linguistic estimate. It is an epigraphic non-reading.

The strongest critical tradition leaves the numeral damaged/unreadable, while:
- 4 is a restoration,
- 6 is an alternative restoration/reading,
- modern GORILA-derived resources preserve the uncertainty.

## Why arithmetic cannot break the tie

The two hypotheses are exactly constructed to fit opposite KI treatments:

### H4
`58 + 4 + 12 + 2 + 5 + 16 = 97`

This makes KI 2 an ordinary positive member of KU-RO 97.

### H6
`58 + 6 + 12 + 5 + 16 = 97`

This makes the non-KI quantities total 97 and allows KI 2 to be treated as excluded/subtractive.

Because the damaged numeral itself is the free variable, the total does not independently discriminate the semantic treatment of KI.

Choosing the restoration that yields one's preferred KI semantics would be circular.

## What would genuinely settle it

A new observation of the clay surface:
1. museum autopsy of HM 1306;
2. high-resolution raking-light photography;
3. RTI / photogrammetry / 3D surface scan;
4. archival photography showing the damaged strokes more clearly than the published GORILA plate.

Absent that, HT100 must remain parked as a potentially decisive but currently unidentifiable polarity experiment.
