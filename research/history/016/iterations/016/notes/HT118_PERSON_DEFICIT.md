# HT118 named-participant construction

## Text
`MA-DI 15 KI 10 • QA-QA-RU 6 KI 4 • A-RI-SU 4 KI 1 • RI-RU-MA 10 • KU-RO 30 KI 15`

## Internal KI arithmetic
`10 + 4 + 1 = 15`

The final KI 15 therefore functions as a total of the three explicitly KI-marked quantities.

## Independent participant anchor
Linear A QA-QA-RU corresponds in the onomastic literature to Linear B qa-qa-ro, attested as a masculine personal name.

This lets the project classify the row's left-hand entity as a person without inferring personhood from the KI structure itself.

## Combined parse
Safe:
`QA-QA-RU | account/main amount 6 | KI outstanding/deficit 4`

Stronger but not internally proven:
`QA-QA-RU owes 4 [units] to the palace`

The stronger direction comes from specialist interpretation of the administrative context, not from a separately written counterparty on the row.
