# HT34 scribal-correction dossier

## Written sequence

`SA+MU+KU 100 • PA₃ 70 • KI-RO 30 [[7]]`

### Critical notation
The final 7 is transcribed in double square brackets, the conventional notation for erased text in Aegean epigraphic transcription.

### Arithmetic
Current text:
- reference: 100
- PA₃-marked amount: 70
- KI-RO amount: 30
- 70 + 30 = 100

If erased 7 were retained:
- KI-RO = 37
- 70 + 37 = 107
- no closure against 100

## Inference
The correction is evidence about *intended relation*, not merely an editorial preference. The KI-RO amount is the exact remainder after the PA₃-marked amount.

## Digital-corpus warning
At least one modern flattened corpus currently presents `KI-RO 37`, demonstrating that dropping the erasure layer destroys the semantic evidence.

The cumulative corpus should retain:
`KI-RO 30 {erased: 7}`
rather than either plain `30` or plain `37`.
