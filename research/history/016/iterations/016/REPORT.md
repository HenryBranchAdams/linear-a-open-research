# Iteration 16 — Substantial breakthrough: KI-RO residual semantics

## Executive result

This run crosses the project's **substantial-breakthrough** threshold.

The breakthrough is not a world-first translation. Current specialists already gloss KI-RO as “deficit/owed.” What is new **within this project** is a much stronger independent internal demonstration of that function, driven by a scribal correction on HT34 rather than by accepting the scholarly gloss.

## 1. HT34 gives a direct internal semantic constraint

The decisive sequence is:

`SA+MU+KU 100 • PA₃ 70 • KI-RO 30 [[7]]`

The `7` is erased. Standard transcription conventions use double square brackets for erased text.

The surviving arithmetic is:

`100 − 70 = 30`

The erased state would instead have given:

`70 + 37 = 107`

Thus the scribe's correction transforms a non-balancing KI-RO figure into the exact complementary remainder.

This is qualitatively stronger than merely observing that KI-RO often appears in a secondary section. The correction itself shows what numerical relation the writer intended.

### Conservative interpretation

On HT34:

> **KI-RO labels the residual / outstanding balance left after the PA₃-marked portion is reconciled against the reference amount.**

“Deficit” is an appropriate functional gloss for this construction. “PA₃ = delivered/paid” is still too specific; PA₃ should be described as a transaction/accounting marker until independently resolved.

## 2. Why the erasure matters methodologically

A flattened modern corpus currently renders HT34 as `KI-RO 37`, because it has lost the erased status of the final 7.

That is not a harmless formatting difference. It destroys the exact arithmetic relation that makes the tablet interpretable.

Iteration 16 therefore also identifies a concrete corpus defect class:

> **erasure-state loss can invert semantic inference.**

This directly validates the project's policy of retaining critical-edition uncertainty and correction layers.

## 3. External convergence

The internal result converges with Ester Salgarella's 2025 specialist synthesis, which lists:
- KI-RO “deficit/owed”
- KA-I-RO “balance”
- perhaps KI-RA “balance”

The project no longer needs to rely solely on that received gloss for HT34: the tablet's corrected arithmetic independently supports the residual/deficit function.

## 4. Named-participant extension on HT118

HT118 reads:

`MA-DI 15 KI 10 • QA-QA-RU 6 KI 4 • A-RI-SU 4 KI 1 • RI-RU-MA 10 • KU-RO 30 KI 15`

The KI subcolumn closes exactly:

`10 + 4 + 1 = 15`

Current specialist work treats KI here as the abbreviated KI[-RO].

Separately, QA-QA-RU has an unusually strong onomastic anchor: Linear B `qa-qa-ro` (As 604) is a masculine personal name, and scholarship treats the Linear A / Linear B pair as a Minoan personal name adapted to Greek morphology.

This lets us parse one Linear A account entry much more tightly than before:

> **QA-QA-RU — main/account quantity 6 — KI deficit/outstanding quantity 4.**

The participant category (person) and the status/account term (deficit/outstanding) are supported by independent evidence streams.

## 5. What we still cannot say

The following stronger English sentence is *not* yet internally forced:

> “QA-QA-RU owes four units to the palace.”

Salgarella's current interpretation of HT118 is indeed that the commodities are owed to the palace, but the tablet's surviving internal structure does not independently identify the palace as counterparty or distinguish debtor from creditor direction.

Therefore the project's earlier action-and-role mission is **very close but not completely satisfied**.

What is now supported is a non-verbal/clause-like accounting relation:

> **named person → recorded/account quantity → outstanding deficit quantity**

This is a substantial advance over merely saying that a word is a “personnel-related classifier.”

## 6. Adversarial checks

### “KI-RO is just a generic second-section label”
HT34 weakens this sharply: the writer corrects the KI-RO amount to the exact arithmetic remainder.

### “KI-RO is 37”
Rejected as a critical transcription. The seven is erased.

### “The 100−70=30 equality is coincidence”
The erasure makes coincidence much less plausible: the writer actively removes the element that prevents closure.

### “KI-RO must mean negative deficit in every occurrence”
Not established. HT34 fixes the local function; HT123 and other records still caution against universalizing transaction direction without context.

### “QA-QA-RU may not be a person”
Much weaker after the independent Linear B masculine-personal-name parallel.

### “The project has now translated a full sentence”
No. The named participant and accounting status are constrained, but the construction is likely non-verbal and counterparty direction is unresolved.

## 7. Breakthrough classification

- Level 3 structural relation: **surpassed**
- Level 4 local semantic/accounting function: **yes on HT34**
- Independently typed participant + status relation: **yes on HT118**
- Fully directed action/counterparty: **not yet**
- Held-out ancient prediction: **not available from already exposed corpus**

## 8. Next frontier

The next genuinely decisive target is no longer “what does KI-RO do?”

It is:

> **Who is the counterparty / which direction does the outstanding balance run?**

Highest-value evidence would be:
1. an HT118-like record with a securely identified place/institutional counterparty,
2. a seal/nodule link tying a named person to inflow or outflow,
3. or a paired assessment/delivery record where the same person appears and the direction is externally constrained.

That is now the shortest route from a deciphered accounting relation to a fully directed clause.
