## Functional interpretation

Cross-domain evidence changes the safest gloss of KI-RO.

- On HT30 it heads a second commodity section.
- On HT123 it participates in an arithmetic differential.
- Cash & Cash leave taxation vs allocation direction symmetric.
- Monti explicitly argues the HT123 quantities can represent surplus rather than deficit.

Therefore the project should no longer encode `KI-RO = deficit/missing`.

The current evidence-level label is:

> **KI-RO = differential / exception-balance marker (functional gloss).**

In personnel records, a designation under KI-RO is a member of an administratively distinguished subset. Whether the subset consists of absent, owing, owed-to, extra, exempt, or otherwise outstanding people is not yet known.
