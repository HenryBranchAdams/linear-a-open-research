# Role and polarity test

## Personnel alternatives
For a person listed under KI-RO, all of the following remain observationally possible:

- absent / missing,
- owing,
- owed-to,
- extra / surplus,
- exempt / released,
- otherwise outstanding for reconciliation.

The one-count and total structure does not distinguish them.

## Commodity control
HT123 independently blocks the assumption that KI-RO always has negative polarity. Published arithmetic can be read as positive difference/surplus.

## Result
The secure role is **member of a differential/exception section**.

This is an administrative role, but not yet the concrete real-world role the project ultimately seeks.
