# Personnel construction

## HT 88
Six one-count designations follow KI-RO and sum exactly to KU-RO 6.

The same tablet has an explicit human/personnel quantity `VIR+KA 20`, which anchors the broader document in personnel administration. The six-member KI-RO list is therefore not just an arbitrary six-item commodity list.

## HT 94b
Five one-count designations follow KI-RO and sum exactly to KU-RO 5.

The obverse contains a separately totaled personnel roster under KA-PA, including VIR 62 and other human-category ideograms, for KU-RO 110.

## Replication
The same section pattern occurs independently on two tablets:
- marker,
- N individually enumerated one-count designations,
- total N.

This is stronger than an English gloss because the construction is recoverable from layout and arithmetic alone.

## Limit
No overlapping named roster has yet been found that tells us *why* those individuals belong to the KI-RO subset.
