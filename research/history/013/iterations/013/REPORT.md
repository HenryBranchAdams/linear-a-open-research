# Iteration 13 Report

## Mission result

Iteration 13 did **not** establish an overt action with independently assigned participant roles.

It did, however, establish the strongest repeated administrative construction in the project so far and refine the functional meaning of KI-RO.

## 1. Replicated personnel construction

HT88 and HT94b independently instantiate:

> **KI-RO • PERSON/DESIGNATION 1 ... KU-RO N**

On HT88 there are six one-count entries and KU-RO 6.
On HT94b there are five one-count entries and KU-RO 5.

These relations are arithmetic and structural; they do not require translating KI-RO.

Both tablets have independent personnel context on the same object:
- HT88 contains VIR+KA 20.
- HT94a contains a personnel roster totaling 110, including VIR 62 and *86 20.

Thus the KI-RO sections are securely embedded in personnel administration.

## 2. KI-RO is not securely 'deficit'

The wider corpus prevents us from simply translating the marker as 'missing' or 'deficit'.

HT30 uses KI-RO to introduce a second list of the same commodity types seen in a preceding SA-RA2 section.

HT123 places KI-RO quantities in a three-column accounting system. Cash & Cash derive them as a differential between actual and reference values, while explicitly acknowledging that taxation vs allocation direction is symmetric. Orazio Monti then published the objection that those HT123 differences can be positive surpluses rather than shortages.

The robust cross-domain core is therefore:

> **KI-RO marks a differential / exception / separately reconciled balance.**

This is a functional gloss, not an etymological translation.

## 3. What the construction now tells us

For HT88 and HT94b we can say, without assuming an English status verb:

> The entries are individual members of a KI-RO-classified differential subset, and KU-RO gives the size of that subset.

This distinguishes them from the unmarked/main administrative population.

It does **not** distinguish:
- missing vs extra,
- owing vs owed-to,
- absent vs exempt,
- supplier vs recipient.

## 4. QI-TU-NE

Iteration 12's QI-TU-NE result survives unchanged. It is a personnel-related classifier and repeatedly governs DI-KI-SE, but Iteration 13 found no independent evidence fixing its semantic status or action.

## 5. Why this is progress

Earlier iterations repeatedly found that plausible translations were underdetermined.

Iteration 13 recovers a construction whose **membership relation is not speculative**:
- KI-RO defines the section,
- each member receives 1,
- KU-RO independently verifies the member count,
- and the containing documents are personnel-related.

The unresolved part is now localized to the **polarity/cause of the status**, rather than the existence of the relation itself.

## 6. Next trigger

Iteration 14 should search specifically for a KI-RO member that can be matched to the same named entity in a non-KI-RO roster, or for a document with independently recoverable expected vs actual personnel counts.

That would let us distinguish 'missing/deficit' from 'surplus/other exception' and potentially turn this structural construction into a concrete status sentence.
