# Iteration 14 Report — Full campaign

## Executive result

Iteration 14 **did not reach Level 4 polarity/action evidence**. It did something important instead: it falsified a premise that had crept into Iteration 13 and localized the shortest remaining path to a genuine breakthrough.

The strongest secure cross-domain statement is now:

> **KI-RO marks a secondary / differential / reconciliation section in Haghia Triada administration. The ontology of individual syllabic entries and the sign/cause of the difference remain unresolved.**

The previous phrase “personnel construction” was too strong at the entry level. Some KI-RO tablets clearly contain personnel information elsewhere, but a one-count syllabic label under KI-RO is not thereby proven to be a person.

## Lane A — recurrence graph

The frozen 802-face SigLA snapshot contains exact KI-RO on 11 faces plus damaged/related cases. The recurrence graph was built for every designation in the best-defined KI-RO-bearing label lists. Four candidates dominate:

1. **KU-*56-NU / KU-PA3-NU** — in KI-RO-bearing HT1, HT88, HT117; also HT3, HT49, HT122, PH31 outside those KI-RO contexts. HT3 gives quantity 3; HT49 gives 1; HT122 repeats it twice.
2. **PA-JA-RE** — HT88 KI-RO 1, but quantity 2 on HT29 and a fractional amount on HT8b, plus ZA10b.
3. **SA-RU** — HT94b KI-RO 1, but also the HT86/95 grain-account packet and HT123 commodity accounting.
4. **PA-TA-NE** — HT94b KI-RO 1 and the nodule HT Wa1019, giving the best cross-media administrative bridge. A possible HT122 recurrence has a source-reading discrepancy and is not used as secure evidence.

No recurrence pair provides an independently known *normal roster → KI-RO exception roster* transition for the same named individual.

## Lane B — roster reconstruction

The campaign's strongest falsification came here. Montecchi explicitly warns that Linear A lists of words followed by `1` and totaled are **not necessarily personal-name lists**, citing KU-*56-NU itself. The recurrence graph independently demonstrates why: the same form occurs with other quantities and in broader list systems.

Therefore the exact construction recovered on HT88/HT94 is structural, not ontological:

`KI-RO • LABEL 1 • LABEL 1 ... KU-RO N`

KU-RO verifies the number/amount of entries in that subsection. It does **not** tell us that every label is a person or why the subsection is exceptional.

## Lane C — numerical polarity

HT100 was the best candidate to fix the sign of KI/KI-RO. It fails for a precise, source-critical reason.

The relevant sequence is conventionally represented as:

`VIR+KA 58 • *304+PA [?] • TI+A 12 • KI 2 • *305 5 • VIR+*313a 16 • KU-RO 97`

Two restorations generate opposite conclusions:

- If the damaged amount is **4**, then `58 + 4 + 12 + 2 + 5 + 16 = 97`: KI 2 is included positively.
- If it is **6**, then `58 + 6 + 12 + 5 + 16 = 97`: excluding KI 2 gives the total, which motivates a deficit/subtractive analysis.

Montecchi proposed the six-unit restoration. Uchitel prints `[4]`. Bennett's textual review reports the earlier `[4]` as restored/damaged, GORILA as not securely supplying a numeral in the damaged slot, and CTLA as `6`, and specifically notes that these restorations are not persuasive given the damage.

**Conclusion:** HT100 does not independently establish polarity until that numeral is adjudicated from the best primary evidence/object. This is now the highest-leverage single reading in the project.

## Lane D — physical/documentary linkage

PA-TA-NE is the best link. Montecchi notes the same syllabic sequence on HT94b and the nodule HT Wa1019. That moves the designation across tablets and sealing administration. But noduli themselves may represent receipts, counting devices, credentials, incoming goods, outgoing goods, or human resources. The physical-media bridge therefore strengthens “administrative designation” while leaving transaction direction open.

HT94 and HT117 also share Scribe 9; HT88 and HT94 share Casa Room 7 but different hands. These links identify administrative networks, not single events.

## Lane E — cross-domain KI-RO model

The broadest model test favors **unsigned difference/reconciliation** over fixed negative or positive polarity.

- HT30 places KI-RO before a second section containing many of the same commodity categories as the preceding section.
- HT123 supports a numerical difference model, while published interpretations acknowledge direction ambiguity.
- HT88/94 contain KI-RO subsections of one-count labels.
- HT100 remains compatible with either inclusion or exclusion because of the damaged numeral.

A neutral reconciliation operator handles these contexts without requiring KI-RO to mean “absent”, “owed”, or “surplus” everywhere. This remains a **functional model, not a lexical translation**.

## Linear B control lane

Deciphered Linear B demonstrates why we must demand a stronger anchor. Mycenaean documents distinguish assessments, actual deliveries, exemptions, and debts with explicit lexical/documentary cues such as `a-pu-do-si`, `o-u-di-do-si`, and `o-pe-ro`; personnel records can explicitly mark `a-pe-o-te` “absent”. A generic one-count list is therefore not an adequate structural diagnostic for absence or deficit.

The control does not transfer Greek meanings into Linear A. It tests our reasoning rule — and that rule fails if it infers “absent” from list shape alone.

## Prediction / falsification outcome

The preregistered working hypothesis — KI-RO reconciles an expected state against an observed state — survives, but only as an abstract model. The search could not identify what the reference state is.

The stronger candidate “KI-RO one-count entries are necessarily people” **failed**. The candidate “HT100 fixes negative polarity” is **non-identifiable from current reading**.

No genuinely unseen ancient witness remained after the full-corpus discovery pass, so no retrospective split is presented as held-out validation.

## Breakthrough status

- Level 3 independent relation: **yes** — secondary/reconciliation section is supported across contexts.
- Level 4 polarity/action: **no**.
- Level 5 held-out prediction: **no**.

## The two highest-value next moves

1. **HT100 primary autopsy:** resolve the damaged numeral after `*304+PA` from GORILA I 164–165 and, if possible, the original object/high-resolution photograph. A secure 4 versus 6 directly flips the arithmetic interpretation.
2. **Expected/actual roster acquisition:** find a physically linked pair in which the same designations form a baseline roster and KI-RO subset. This is the cleanest way to identify the status without relying on etymology.

Until one of those changes, translating KI-RO as “deficit”, “missing”, or “absent” should be treated as a hypothesis, not a result.
