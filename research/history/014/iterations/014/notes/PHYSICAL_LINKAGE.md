# Physical/documentary linkage audit

## PA-TA-NE
The strongest cross-media bridge is HT94b ↔ HT Wa1019. The same form appears in a KI-RO list and on a nodule. This supports a persistent administrative designation/account/entity. It does not determine whether the nodule records incoming or outgoing goods, authorization, personnel, or another function.

## Scribe / room network
- HT94 and HT117: HT Scribe 9, different Casa rooms.
- HT88 and HT94: both Casa Room 7, different hands.

These are useful network links, not transaction identity.

## HT122 reading warning
Modern sources commonly list PA-TA-NE on HT122a, but the frozen SigLA acquisition used by this project has PA-RI-NE. This discrepancy is recorded rather than silently normalized.
