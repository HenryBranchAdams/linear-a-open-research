# Roster comparison

## Tested hypothesis
`KI-RO + WORD 1 ... KU-RO N` is an absent/missing-person roster.

## Failure
The hypothesis requires two unsupported steps: (1) each syllabic word is a person, and (2) KI-RO gives a negative status.

The first step fails as a general diagnostic. Montecchi explicitly points to KU-*56-NU as a counterexample to the assumption that totaled 1-lists necessarily contain personal names. The recurrence graph reinforces that warning: KU-*56-NU, PA-JA-RE and SA-RU move across different quantity and accounting contexts.

## Surviving construction
The secure reading is weaker:

`KI-RO • administrative entry/label ... [KU-RO total]`

The section is distinct/reconciliatory. Entry ontology and polarity are unresolved.
