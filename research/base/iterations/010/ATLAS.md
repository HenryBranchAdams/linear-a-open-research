# Iteration 10 — Candidate-pair atlas

**The run found stronger linked records, but no independently supported action-and-role construction.** Its most useful result is separating a real recurring roster from misleading form matches, then making rival explanations explicit.

Date: 9 September 2026. Mission status: **open**. This is completed exploratory analysis, not a preregistered experiment or a claim of scholarly priority.

## What this run adds

1. **A bounded exhaustive screen:** all 802 faces / 1,401 indexed sequence occurrences in the recovered SigLA snapshot were classified. The strict phonetic filter retains material on 220 faces; the broad complete-chunk audit on 250. Most indexed faces therefore cannot contribute an eligible contextual packet. These are not 802 independent tablets or 1,401 certified complete words.
2. **A source correction:** the strongest raw DA-ME/DA-ZA contrast does not survive GORILA I p.155, which prints ME on HT95b. The raw snapshot remains unchanged; explicit sensitivity overlays record alternate readings and segmentation.
3. **A competing identification:** TE-KE can be matched to TE-KI or TE-TU with the same supporting associates in HT13. The original edition confirms both latter forms as separate entries. Context similarity alone does not choose one.
4. **A sharper HT24 acquisition requirement:** a better ME reading alone cannot distinguish unit multipliers while yield and missing input remain unconstrained. An independent input total, or independently applicable output-yield bound, is needed.
5. **Recovered cumulative context:** original archives restore the previously missing rounds3–5. Thirty-six historical claim records and their archive locations are consolidated. Prior negative results are retained, not recounted as new findings.

## Candidate decisions

### R10-C01 · HT 86 / HT 95: the strongest audited roster

**Related records; identity and role unresolved.** `QA-RA2-WA ↔ QE-RA2-U`

Five other labels recur after the Q forms are withheld: DA-ME, DI-DE-RU, KU-NI-SU, MI-NU-TE, SA-RU. The Q entries follow DI-DE-RU in the inspected edition. Two tablets, not four independent witnesses.

**Test:** All five exact-label quantities are 20 on HT86a and 10 on HT95b. Extending that ×2 conversion to the proposed Q pairing predicts 20, but the QA entry is 10. The conjunction of same Q account and universal conversion of equivalent quantities fails.

**Strongest rivals:** Spelling variation; distinct related accounts; commodity vocabulary; repeated rosters in separate events. Different grain composites and sections prevent treating a common unit as established.

**Required next evidence:** An independent participant/category anchor, followed by a third linked record with an externally recognizable operation. More spelling resemblance is insufficient.

**Access and novelty:** GORILA I pp134–135 and154–155 visually checked. Roster and Q correspondence are published prior work. Exhaustive ranking and exact conditional scale diagnostic were executed in this iteration; publication priority unestablished.

### R10-C02 · DA-ME / DA-ZA: a misleading database contrast

**Do not use as morphology.** `HT86a DA-ME ↔ frozen SigLA HT95b DA-ZA`

The strongest raw terminal-sign candidate changes when the primary edition is checked. GORILA prints ME in HT95b.2; its damaged-sign apparatus concerns a preceding mark.

**Test:** Keep raw SigLA unchanged; rerun with a named DA-ME reading overlay. The apparent DA-ME/DA-ZA substitution disappears.

**Strongest rivals:** A database transcription or interpretation discrepancy, not demonstrated ancient variation. This audit does not diagnose its cause.

**Required next evidence:** Retain the discrepancy record; only a documented revised reading should reopen it.

**Access and novelty:** Primary edition normalized table and drawing inspected by root and source researcher. Source audit and explicit overlay newly executed in this project run.

### R10-C03 · TE-KE / TE-KI, with TE-TU as an explicit rival

**Contextually linked; substitution not unique.** `HT85a TE-KE ↔ HT122a TE-KI; control HT13 TE-TU`

HT85a and HT122a share DA-RI-DA and the opaque chunk DA-SI-*118 when that chunk is admitted. Published hand attribution puts both under Scribe 9. Broader object overlap adds labels on other faces.

**Test:** HT13 contains TE-KI and TE-TU. Either can be compared to HT85a TE-KE with the same independent companions DA-SI-*118 and KU-*79-NI. Co-occurrence does not itself prove either reading is the same referent.

**Strongest rivals:** Short related names or unrelated names; spelling habit; grammar. Similar context does not uniquely select a mapping.

**Required next evidence:** An additional identity anchor that chooses TE-KI over TE-TU, and role evidence independent of the last sign.

**Access and novelty:** SigLA snapshot; Davis/Valério contextual study. HT13 edition check documented in source note; HT85/122 no fresh full plate audit. TE-KE/TE-KI is published. Explicit rival-context screen newly run; no priority claim.

## HT24: a more precise reason to pause

The conditional model is `cR = y[B + (m−1)S + x]`: conversion c, reverse quantity R, yield y, visible lower bound B, ME subtotal S, multiplier m and missing weighted input x. Even with c=10, R=4½ and settled ME reading, every positive m can fit if missing input has no upper bound and yield can approach zero. This is an algebraic non-identification result, not a feasible restoration of every damaged tablet.

Accepting ME beside6, doubled ME is excluded if the output is independently45 and the comparable yield is independently at least95%;90% is insufficient. Those yield values are requirements, not measured ancient yields. An independently established45-unit **input** total excludes doubled ME from the visible lower bound without using downstream yield. The archaeological proximity of45 noduli does not independently calibrate that total.

Sources, exact proof and scenarios: `notes/ht24_fallback.md` and its `identifiability.py`. No new ME reading or actual yield was recovered. Keep the branch parked until an independent total/denomination or processing constraint changes.

Keep TE-KE's TE-TU alternative as a standing falsification control. Keep HT122/HT9 as an unverified packet lead, not a semantic result. The two leading TA-NA obstacles remain referent identity and site-confounded variation.
