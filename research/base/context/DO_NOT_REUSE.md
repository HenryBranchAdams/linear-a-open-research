## Do not repeat these operations as a new research advance

- Recounting the same round 4 seal 142 labels or rediscovering the HT Wc 3009/3010 NA pair.
- Recomputing HT 95 mixed differences, ZA 4/ZA 15 closed-settlement failures or HT 122’s late KU-DA 1 scope correction without added evidence.
- Rerunning the same manually materialized 1, 019-label screen and describing it as a fresh complete-corpus search.
- Counting GORILA, Younger’s mirror and a SigLA transcription as three independent ancient attestations.
- Counting multiple decompositions of one form set, different faces of one object, join aliases, or alternative segmentations as independent confirmations.
- Re-viewing the same insufficient ZA 9 or HT 24 scans and upgrading a doubtful sign because a predicted pattern needs it.
- Re-running HT 24 multiplier arithmetic without a new reading, total/conversion constraint or independently grounded processing model.

Such work may be useful reproduction or QA. Label it that way, and report the new information separately.
