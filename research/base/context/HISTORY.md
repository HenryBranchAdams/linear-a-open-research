1. A compatible IO Za 16 completion became **nonunique** when TE-*123 entered the inventory. Neither longer completion is a recovered prefix.
2. Round 4’s apparently attractive label/hand equation became **sensitive** when tentative hand 109? records were admitted. Do not describe those assignments as independent identity evidence.

4. Derived-index morphology candidates became **boundary-dependent or invalid**: missing endings and cross-face word segments supplied false shorter forms. The index is retained as a discovery aid, not an edition.
5. Round 7–8’s jar manufacture description became more carefully qualified in round 9: **pre-firing incision is publication-supported; precise scientific provenance was not found**. Arrival location cannot independently identify a recipient.
6. The external ta-na-to comparison gained **better Linear B category support** in round 9, but still did not establish any identity across the Linear A forms. The apparent complete ta-na-ti counterpart is left-broken; a simple Greek o-stem case story does not produce the proposed spelling contrast.
7. HT 24 provided a new **conditional arithmetic sensitivity result**, not an exclusion of every alternative ME unit. Doubtful ME and lossless processing are decisive assumptions.

## Evidence handling carried forward

- Distinguish an inscription, its faces, sequence chunks, drawings, transcriptions and source mirrors. Those are not independent archaeological witnesses.
- Preserve broken boundaries, uncertain sign components, restorations, join alternatives, unknown counts and uncertain hands in separate fields.
- Full seal catalogue identifiers are entity keys. Visual motifs and convenient aliases are not enough to merge seals. Historical tablet/jar aliases require explicit concordance.
- A same-seal pair is an independently matched document feature, not proof that its words name the seal owner. A human logogram establishes a category context, not a grammatical subject.
- Existing results are reusable constraints. Mark unchanged reruns as reproductions; mark added source coverage or newly discriminating evidence separately.
- Compare alternatives with identical evidential access. A hypothesis must not supply its own missing role, word boundary, total, sound law or identity bridge.
- Keep semantic fields null until evidence selects them. No historical report warrants a newly deciphered sentence, percentage deciphered, language-family assignment or publication priority.
