The existing workflow suite passes all **16 tests**. Source whitespace checks pass. Reduced-motion behavior is implemented and reviewed in source; media-preference emulation is not available in this browser surface.

Motion is confined to transform/opacity: short text entrances, row entrances, a restrained object scale that never crops the tablet, and a scroll-linked scale at the closing heading. The remaining design ceiling is the imagery: these are four truthful contextual photographs, not exact-source plates for every research witness. Licensed, verified witness images and page-level source viewing would improve the evidence experience. Independent reader testing is still needed before claiming a near-perfect usability score.
