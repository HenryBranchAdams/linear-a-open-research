## Direction

Three directions were drawn: name-as-monument, editorial-report, split-stage. The selected direction is **The printed annual** (`editorial-report`), seed **1737244794**. Its shared axis, edited text columns and alternating full-width fields suit a research record that must explain its qualifications. No direction redraw.

Use the 42% axis on wide screens and collapse it to the left margin on narrow screens. Preserve the masthead image band, asymmetric introduction, typographic findings, a full-width live evidence field, banded history and an inverted closing statement. Specialist tables and source disclosures carry the complete detail without dominating the opening screen.

White, near-black and grey carry reading. Mint is reserved for the evidence demonstration and the final action. No decorative rules, pill navigation, shadows, gradients, small uppercase labels or pseudo-statistical progress indicators.

Boska is the selected display face. The foundry specifically describes it as a display face with hairline punctuation. To meet the user's explicit legibility requirement for dense evidence, body text and controls use the familiar Arial/system sans stack at 18–20px. Boska remains the headline face; the page uses two font families. This is a documented legibility adaptation, not a change of design direction.

The four authentic photographs show a Heraklion tablet, a Zakros tablet, modern Hagia Triada and a Minoan clay sealing. Captions distinguish introductory illustrations from the project's named witnesses. All remain in full colour, unretouched, with linked object/source and license credits. Slow movement is applied to the introductory image frame only; reduced-motion users receive a static image.

## Initial review

The original page was inspected in the managed preview. It led with a generic invitation to work, omitted an introduction to the script, presented the 30 branch alone, and advertised early missions as available. Small muted labels, repeated split sections, thin dividers and a decorative accent headline weakened the reading hierarchy. The original source lint reported 20 failures and 5 warnings.

## Final critique and verification

**Editorial and design self-assessment: 7.8/10.** The page has a clear subject, a strong opening image, a recognizable reading axis and a live example tied to the research's central uncertainty. Body copy and controls carry the dense reasoning in a familiar face. The reader can move from the overview to observations, their limits, correction history and a finite next brief. The design's main remaining limitation is the density of the underlying research; the full record appropriately requires sustained reading. Some finding-level provenance still routes through the baseline and source guide rather than directly to a complete public edition archive.

The review led to concrete changes: the mobile opening puts the mission and paused state before the photograph; every mobile correction pairs the earlier proposition with its current conclusion in one vertical comparison; the HT34 brackets are explained as editorial notation; historical submission and coordination pages carry the same current disposition as the archive; physical timing is described as a limit on participant-role inference, rather than an identification of the writer.
