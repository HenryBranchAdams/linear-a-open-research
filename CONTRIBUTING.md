# Contributing

Begin with research/current/CURRENT_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md and the current research record through campaign 044 (D2026-10-26). There are no active semantic experiments in the current queue.

A useful contribution identifies the exact evidence; at least two serious interpretations; an independent observation that would distinguish them; a finite source or checking route; prior exposure; and a stopping condition. A precise correction or independently supported local category or relation can also be valuable at its stated scope.

The immediate source target is Duhoux2003’s complete ceramic comparison, especially §4.1.2, following C044’s graded local vessel-class preference. Exact cross-material use or a feature-matched ceramic counterpart could change that result. Exclusive bronze denotation, an action and participant roles remain unresolved. The complete Flouda2024 article remains an inherited dependency. A legitimate complete copy or changed access route can enable bounded evaluation; access alone does not establish a meaning. Read the current brief for all exact reopening and stopping conditions. Prepared requests are unsent.

Read and copy without signing in. On-site posting requires ChatGPT sign-in and a chosen public handle. Submissions and discussions are public and unreviewed until checked. They do not automatically run an experiment or create a claim. Use any research method or agent within an explicit budget.

The eight original missions are archived. Their original prompts and inputs remain intact. New lead, replication and external claims and renewal are closed. Existing contributors may release historical claims, submit results or discuss them. Reproduction of an old calculation is support work, not a new linguistic validation.

Accepted or negative-result dispositions need a reviewed-record reference. Source fidelity, uncertainty, serious rivals, outcome exposure and independent evidence govern review. Project review is not represented as external academic peer review.

Credit original authors and image makers. Link restricted plates and publications. Do not assume modern editions or images are freely redistributable.
